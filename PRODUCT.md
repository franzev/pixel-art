# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The sole primary user is the creator and owner of this pixel-art repository. They browse from desktop, tablet, and phone to scan large batches, rediscover a specific render, compare variations, and inspect an asset without navigating the filesystem.

## Product Purpose

The Ashen Archive turns the existing repository into an automatically indexed visual library. Success means the creator can open one responsive surface, narrow hundreds of renders quickly, and inspect an asset at useful scale without maintaining a separate CMS or duplicating catalog work.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, archival, uncanny. The interface should feel like a private working collection shaped by the project's religious-horror world, while remaining quiet enough for the artwork to dominate.

## Anti-references

- Public portfolio and marketing-site conventions.
- Loud neo-brutalism with thick borders, bright primary colors, and offset shadows.
- Ornamental gothic interfaces that compete with the artwork.
- Generic SaaS dashboards, fake analytics, and card-grid chrome.
- File-explorer interfaces that expose repository complexity instead of simplifying it.

## Design Principles

1. Art first. Controls recede and rendered work dominates the surface.
2. Optimize for scanning. Density, predictable placement, and fast narrowing matter more than presentation theatre.
3. Preserve provenance. Category, collection, draft state, and filename remain visible when useful.
4. Require no catalog upkeep. The site derives its index from existing folders, names, and manifests.
5. Adapt structurally. Desktop, tablet, and phone each get an intentional browsing composition rather than a shrunken desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support full keyboard navigation, visible focus, 44px touch targets for coarse pointers, readable dark-theme contrast, reduced motion, semantic controls, and no hover-only functionality. Browser zoom and text scaling must not break the gallery or viewer.
