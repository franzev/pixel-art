## Shared baseline

Every boss was generated separately as one full-body character concept for a
gothic side-scrolling pixel-art Metroidvania.

Shared requirements:

- Square 1:1 canvas with one complete boss and generous padding.
- Plain warm near-black `#171311` background.
- Hard square pixels, connected clusters, stepped edges, clear negative space,
  and restrained 16–22-color impression.
- No painterly treatment, smoothing, blur, anti-aliasing, scenery, floor plane,
  cast shadow, border, text, logo, UI, or watermark.
- Coherent and explicitly countable anatomy.
- No exposed organs or graphic gore.
- No green background.
- No architecture-bodied creatures.
- No resemblance to the Crimson Knight or recognizable commercial characters.
