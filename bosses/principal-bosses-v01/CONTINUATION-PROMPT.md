Continue the Principal Bosses v01 work in this pixel-art project.

`bosses/principal-bosses-v01/drafts/principal-bosses-v01-draft-review-sheet.png`

There are eight active, unapproved boss concepts:

1. Drowned Tithemother
2. First Severance
3. Bride of the Unreturned Road
4. Minister of Last Confessions
5. Marshal Beneath the Balete
6. Saint of the Empty Belfry
7. Hand Without a Rope
8. Door at the End of All Roads

Treat every current source as a concept master, not a native game sprite.
Preserve all existing source files. Never overwrite a draft; use a versioned
filename for revisions. Generate each distinct boss or revision separately.
Keep the background warm near-black and never green. Save a full source and a
nearest-neighbor 256 × 256 reference for every accepted revision.

Start by showing or summarizing the eight-boss review sheet and asking me which
designs I approve, reject, or want revised. Discuss one boss at a time. Do not
infer canonical approval from silence. Do not update lore, retained counts, or
manifests until I explicitly approve individual bosses.

For approved concepts, help define:

- story revelation and relationship to the Silent Toll;
- arena and region;
- phase-one and phase-two combat identity;
- mercy, binding, release, or destruction outcomes where appropriate;
- native frame size, visible gameplay height, pivot or baseline;
- animation states and approximate frame budgets;
- palette and effect-family limits;
- collision silhouette and small-scale readability.

The design intent is a roster of story bosses, not enlarged regular enemies.
Transformations must express narrative changes rather than merely making a boss
larger or brighter. Maintain coherent anatomy, strong silhouettes, restrained
horror, and original Philippine-inflected gothic worldbuilding.

Do not generate new images until I choose a boss and request a revision or
production follow-up.
