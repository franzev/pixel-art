## Shared production constraints

Apply these constraints to every prompt:

- Generate exactly one original boss on a square 1:1 canvas.
- Show the complete silhouette with generous padding around limbs, weapons,
  cloth, wings, effects, and feet.
- Use a plain warm near-black `#171311` background with no scenery, floor,

- Render premium deliberately authored pixel art: hard square pixels, connected
  clusters, stepped edges, strong negative space, and readable value masses.

  UI, logos, frames, cropped elements, accidental extra anatomy, graphic gore,

- Preserve a restrained 16–22-color impression suitable for later native-grid
  reconstruction.
- Never use a green background.
