Keep clothing simple and animation-friendly: fitted tunics, shirts, trousers,
boots, compact armor, and limited functional accessories. Avoid ornate dresses,
layered skirts, long robes, cascading cloth, capes, dangling panels, and

shapes, restrained texture, and selective highlights. Avoid blur,
anti-aliasing, painterly rendering, excessive dithering, or noisy isolated
pixels.

pentagrams, demonic geometry, broken halos, chained relics, corrupted armor,
and deliberate surreal anatomy. Gore must be meaningful and restrained: no
victims, entrails, organ piles, or effects that obscure the face, body, hands,
