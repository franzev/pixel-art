## Quality direction

- Every character has a distinct role, body type, pose, prop or ritual object,
  wing configuration, corruption motif, and effect pattern.
- Clothing was kept simple and animation-friendly after the first generated
  Cantor draft was rejected for excessive robe layers.
- Blood and body horror are controlled and localized.
- Magical effects remain secondary to the face, anatomy, and silhouette.
- Full sources are preserved under this collection's `drafts/` folder.
- Website-ready 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor references are stored under the
  matching `public/art/angels/corrupted-angels-v01/drafts/` path.
