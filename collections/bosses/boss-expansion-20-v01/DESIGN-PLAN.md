# Boss Expansion 20 v01 — Design Plan

**Status:** Pre-generation design matrix  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Target:** 20 separate, unapproved boss concept sources

## Direction

Create twenty high-quality boss candidates for the Ashen Provinces. Each concept
must have a distinct silhouette, anatomy, palette family, combat hook, and story
role.

The user likes the humanoid Bell Giant but does not like monsters whose bodies
are buildings or other architectural structures.

Allowed:

- natural and supernatural beasts;
- humanoid spirits, demons, and authorities;
- folklore-inspired creatures;
- clearly humanoid or animal-like statues and machines;
- constructed armor or ritual equipment worn by a coherent body.

Excluded:

- walking houses, chapels, towers, gates, bridges, or city fragments;
- architecture used as the creature's torso or shell;
- generic enlarged versions of retained enemies;
- multiple concepts in one image.

## Shared generation gate

- One complete boss per square image.
- Plain warm near-black background, never green.
- Full silhouette and all gameplay-defining parts visible.
- Coherent, explicitly countable anatomy.
- Deliberate pixel-art treatment with crisp connected clusters.
- No scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, text, border, logo, or watermark.
- No graphic gore or exposed organs.
- No resemblance to the Crimson Knight or recognizable commercial characters.
- Preserve the full source and create a nearest-neighbor 256 reference.
- Reject and regenerate anatomy failures rather than quietly accepting them.
