## Direction

This collection expands the boss candidate pool while responding to the user's
preference for the humanoid Bell Giant and dislike of architecture-bodied
monsters.

No active boss uses a house, chapel, gate, tower, bridge, or other building as
its body. The set uses coherent animals, folklore-inspired beings, humanoid
spirits, one humanoid wooden effigy, one masked physician with carried
equipment, and one infernal brute.

These files are full-quality concept renders. They are not native sprites,
animation sheets, collision-ready assets, or canonical lore.

1. Physician pass 01 — eight jars appeared on the rack.
2. Physician pass 02 — seven rack jars plus a hand jar still totaled eight.

into seven colored chambers. The active Alimango uses an open frontal stance

## Promotion gate

Do not move these files out of `drafts/`, create a retained manifest, update the
lore compendium, alter canonical counts, or call these production sprites until
the user reviews and explicitly approves individual concepts.

For approved bosses:

1. choose a story role, region, and encounter order;
2. establish native frame size and gameplay height;
3. redraw deliberately on a native pixel grid;
4. define phase silhouettes, pivot or baseline, collision shapes, and attack
   readability;
5. record animation states and frame budgets;
6. promote only after native-scale review.
