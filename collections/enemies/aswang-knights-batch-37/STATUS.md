## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated adult-humanoid aswang knight enemy
concepts. The family uses human-passing shapeshifter cues, ash-pale intact
faces, restrained canine points, blackened wearable armor, abaca, capiz,
rattan, soot-dark cloth, and controlled violet accents. It is an original
Ashen Provinces game interpretation rather than a definitive cultural
portrayal.

The broad aswang direction was checked against University of the Philippines
Diliman material describing the aswang as a human-passing shapeshifter and
against Cultural Center of the Philippines guidance that folkloric beings have
regional variation and should not be reduced solely to colonial demonization.

## Reference roles

The references were visual anchors, not edit targets. Their exact poses,

## Quality-control review

- All five sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs.
- Every image contains exactly one complete full-height adult humanoid with one
  head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two separated feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, weapons, cloth, bow tips, spear tip, and shield rim remain
  inside the frame with generous padding.
- The defining equipment counts are readable: one spear; one pestle-maul; one
  bolo; one crossbow with one loaded bolt; and one pavise with no weapon.
- The pavise is shown at a three-quarter angle with an interior vertical handle
  and aligned load-bearing arm rather than an implausible front-face grip.
- All five use wearable jointed armor with readable sightlines, breathing
  paths, grips, and adult body proportions.
- Backgrounds remain warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast shadow,
  text, border, logo, watermark, or green field.
- Crisp connected pixel clusters and broad value masses remain legible at
  256 × 256.
- The silhouettes remain distinct at 256 pixels: rising spear diagonal,
  closed vertical pestle, low lateral bolo motion, wide horizontal crossbow,
  and broad shield wall.
- All five use a consistent right-facing presentation.
- No animal-derived anatomy, imp proportions, red-dominant body palette,
  exposed organs, or graphic gore was accepted.
