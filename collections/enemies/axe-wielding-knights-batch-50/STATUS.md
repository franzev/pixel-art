## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated adult human enemy knights belonging to
one late-medieval pollaxe order. The group shares a mechanically documented
two-handed pollaxe while differing through stance, silhouette, helmet, armor
layout, cloth system, and encounter role. All faces are concealed by functional
helmets; no female-character identity matrix was required.

## Positive Direction

The Catholic Knights Batch 41 set is the closest strongly reviewed direction
(10/10 kept in the bulk review). Its Ash-Hatchet Penitent was supplied to the
generation system as a visual reference only for mature adult proportions,
wearable dark armor, restrained palette, crisp pixel clusters, full-body
isolation, and severe religious-gothic presence. Its exact pose, helmet,
costume, markings, axe, and silhouette were not copied.

## Direct User Feedback — Palette Fatigue

On 2026-07-31 the user supplied an exact copy of
`public/art/enemies/axe-wielding-knights-batch-50/drafts/03-black-scapular-brace.png`
and reported strong fatigue with its filter, look, and palette.

Treat Draft 03 as direct negative evidence for the repeated combination of
blackened steel, soot and charcoal cloth, dark-brown wood, dirty-ivory detail,
tiny bronze highlights, warm rim light, and an overall brown-black grade. Do
not use it as a palette or lighting reference.

Pixel sampling confirms that the background is not the required flat
`#171311` (`23,19,17`). Sampled background points vary from RGB `14,12,10` to
`17,14,12`, establishing real brown-black falloff rather than a display-only
impression.

This feedback does not by itself approve or reject the concept, weapon,
anatomy, or lifecycle status. All five images remain unapproved drafts. Keep
the historical generation prompts unchanged; future work follows the current
palette and global-grade locks instead.

## Equipment Direction

The weapon is based on Royal Armouries late-15th-century English/Northern
European pollaxes. Recorded dimensions are 1.85 m overall, 25.3 cm axe blade,
10.1 cm rear hammer, 21 cm top spike, 73.6 cm langets, and 2.67 kg. Against an
approximately 1.75 m handler, the render target is about 1.06 times standing
height.

Every draft shows:

- one continuous straight square-section hardwood haft;
- one aligned pollaxe head with axe blade, rear hammer, and top spike;
- visible riveted langets joining the head to the haft;
- two hands gripping only the haft below the langets;
- inspectable hand contacts and head-to-haft attachment;
- a grounded stance carrying the weapon's load.

## Draft Roster

1. **Capiz-Visor High Guard** — tall anti-air elite holding the pollaxe in a
   steep rising diagonal.
2. **Ash-Road Hook Castellan** — wide low guard-breaker sweeping the axe blade
   across the approach lane.
3. **Black-Scapular Brace** — compact counter-thrust elite using the top spike
   from a braced middle guard.
4. **Rain-Mantle Pursuer** — forward-driving chase elite carrying the pollaxe
   through a diagonal chopping guard.
5. **Ivory-Langet Processioner** — narrow formation anchor holding the complete
   pollaxe almost vertically beside the body.

## Quality-Control Review

- All five drafts are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG files.
- Each image contains exactly one complete adult human knight with one head,
  two arms, two hands, two legs, and two separated feet.
- All helmets, hands, feet, cloth, pollaxe heads, top spikes, and haft ends fit
  inside the frame with safe padding.
- All five pollaxe hafts are straight and continuous; the head, langets, shaft,
  and butt remain aligned.
- Both hands visibly contact the same haft on every knight. No hand touches a
  blade, hammer, spike, or langet.
- Focused crops confirm readable head-to-haft joins and two-handed grips.
- Armor remains human-proportioned with plausible sight lines, breathing paths,
  neck clearance, joint articulation, and grounded load paths.
- The five silhouettes remain distinct at 256 pixels: steep rising diagonal,
  wide low horizontal, compact middle diagonal, forward-driving diagonal, and
  narrow vertical.
- The prompts requested a uniform warm-charcoal background, but Draft 03 reads
  with a soft brown falloff and global grade. It fails the current
  palette/filter gate and must not be used as positive treatment evidence.
- No draft contains a green field, scenery, floor plane, text, frame, logo,
  watermark, spell, aura, smoke, floating object, or particle effect.
- Pixel construction remains crisp and readable at 256 pixels without smooth
  painterly gradients or noisy microtexture.
