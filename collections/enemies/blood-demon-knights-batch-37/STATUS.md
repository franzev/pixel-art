## Quality review

- Exactly one adult humanoid per square source.
- One head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet per design.
- Full silhouettes, weapons, effects, and cloth remain uncropped.
- Drafts 01–05 retain at least 14 pixels of subject clearance on every edge.
- Drafts 06–10 retain at least 11 pixels of subject clearance on every edge.
- Active drafts 11–15 v02 retain at least 7 pixels of subject clearance on every edge.
- Armor and weapon grips are mechanically readable.
- Drafts 06–10 use only conventional weapons with visibly straight blades or
  rigid straight hafts; neither sword is short.
- Drafts 06–10 have no hooked, chained, ribboned, curved, warped, bent, broken,
  or magically modified weapons.
- Active drafts 11–15 v02 are visibly young-adult women in practical full armor, without
  sculpted breast cups, exposed skin, corsets, heels, or pin-up poses.
- Active drafts 11–15 v02 use visibly lighter ivory-to-light-beige skin.
- Their faces use five different structures: heart, broad oval, round-oval,
  square, and long diamond, with separately assigned eyes, brows, noses, jaws,
  chins, and lips.
- Active drafts 11–15 v02 preserve the same conventional straight-weapon lock as wave 02.
- Warm near-black isolated backgrounds; no scenery, text, floor, or cast shadow.
- Cool and neutral bodies with controlled blood accents; no red skin or red-dominant body palette.
- No animal anatomy, imps, bulky brutes, graphic gore, wings, or extra anatomy.
- The five roles, postures, weapons/effects, and silhouette anchors remain distinct at 256 pixels.
- Deliberate pixel construction remains readable in the 256 references.

## Redo wave 01

The user selected a five-render trial from the 86 rejected renders recorded in
the review export. These five new versions are draft candidates, not approvals:

- `01-blood-needle-duelist-v02.png`: removed the looping blood string.
- `02-vein-hook-arrestor-v02.png`: removed both blood cords.
- `03-clot-sigil-bastion-v02.png`: replaced the floating ward with one straight
  two-handed longsword.
- `08-sable-greatsword-pursuer-v02.png`: moved both hands onto the handle and
  restored the uninterrupted blade.
- `13-oxblood-greatsword-pursuer-v03.png`: redrew the forearms and sword into a
  clear two-handed high guard.

All five preserve a plain warm near-black background, one complete adult
humanoid, a full readable silhouette, and no unrequested floating effects. Both
greatsword corrections and the Bastion longsword were inspected at full size
and at 256 pixels for handle-only grips, straight blades, and safe padding.

The user subsequently rejected redo candidates 01 and 13 because they remained
visually poor and did not solve the original issues. Both must be regenerated
fresh from reconstructed prompts; do not edit either rejected redo again.

The persistent cross-session queue is recorded in
`art-catalog/REDO-TRACKER.md`: 3 awaiting review, 2 marked `redo-again`, and 81
unattempted.

The first Vein-Hook Arrestor and Arterial Thread Lancer passes were rejected
internally for insufficient right-edge clearance. The first Arrestor also gave
the blood cords unwanted weighted tips. Those passes remain only in the
generator archive and were not kept as project drafts.

The first Longsword Oathguard pass had only two pixels of clearance above its
otherwise valid straight blade at 256 pixels. It was regenerated at a safer
scale and was not kept as a project draft.

The first Ash-Pike Linekeeper pass had only eight pixels of right-edge
clearance beyond its otherwise valid straight pike. It was regenerated at a
safer scale and was not kept as a project draft.
