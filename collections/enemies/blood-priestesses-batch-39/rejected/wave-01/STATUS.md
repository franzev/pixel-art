## Direction Locks

- Exactly one adult human woman per image.
- Plain warm near-black background with no scenery, floor, cast shadow,
  vignette, frame, or text.
- Mature anatomy, intact unmarked skin, and non-graphic horror.
- Soot-dark, muted indigo or ash-violet clothing with restrained abaca,
  bronze, copper, and dirty-ivory material cues.
- Crimson remains a controlled blood-rite accent rather than a dominant body
  or garment color.
- Philippine-inflected materials and practical clothing construction without
  treating living spiritual traditions as villain archetypes.
- One clear encounter role and one countable primary tool or effect per design.
- No animal traits, demon anatomy, horns, imps, bulky brutes, extra anatomy,
  green backgrounds, generic cathedral scenery, or copied silhouettes.
