The brief was treated as five isolated adult humanoid bone-knight enemy
concepts for one review wave. The family uses coherent human-scale skeletal
anatomy, mechanically plausible fitted armor, Philippine-inflected abaca,
rattan, rice-straw, bolo, and monsoon-duty cues, and restrained dark materials.
