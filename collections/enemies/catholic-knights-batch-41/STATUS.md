## Interpretation

The briefs were treated as fifteen isolated mature adult human Catholic knights
in one austere devotional family. The first wave established five armored
roles; the second and third added ten women without sexualized armor or
palette-only variants. Catholic identity is carried by processional, censer,
scapular, capiz, votive, and restrained cruciform ideas rather than cathedral
scenery. Abaca, capiz, local weapon forms, and tropical weathering connect the
family to the Ashen Provinces.

## Positive Direction

The first ten renders established a strong collection family through adult
proportions, wearable armor, restrained pixel density, warm near-black
isolation, and austere devotional treatment. Design 06, the Votive-Lamp
Sabreur, was used as the third wave's family reference for mature female
proportion and fitted armor. Its exact face, pose, hair, costume, equipment,
and silhouette were excluded from copying.

Designs 11–15 also used authoritative museum equipment references, recorded in
`GENERATION-PROMPTS.md`, to lock weapon profiles, component ratios, joins,
handling surfaces, and wearer-relative scale before generation.

## Quality-Control Review

- All fifteen renders are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG files.
- Each review sheet is 1280 × 256 and uses transient nearest-neighbor panels;
  no permanent 256 copies were created.
- Every accepted render contains exactly one complete adult human with one
  head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two separated feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, cloth, and equipment remain within the frame with safe
  padding.
- The third wave contains exactly one barong, one halberd, one hand-and-a-half
  sword, one flanged mace, and one rapier; no extra weapon or prop was accepted.
- Third-wave weapon shafts and blades remain continuous, hands close on
  documented grips or hafts, and components remain legible at full size and
  256 pixels.
- Design 13 was regenerated once because the initial sword exceeded the
  recorded wearer-relative scale. The corrected render preserves the documented
  0.72-height sword ratio.
- Third-wave armor remains human-proportioned with readable neck clearance and
  joint articulation.
- Backgrounds are warm near-black without scenery, floor planes, cast shadows,
  text, frames, logos, or watermarks.
- Crisp connected pixel clusters and broad values remain readable at 256.
- Third-wave silhouettes are distinct: compact broad blade, tall polearm
  diagonal, centered two-hand sword guard, short heavy mace line, and narrow
  horizontal rapier lunge.
- The third-wave female identity matrix differs each face on at least four
  facial or hair axes while keeping all five clearly adult, attractive,
  unmarked, dignified, and battle-ready.
- The third wave contains no spells, magic, glow, aura, smoke, flame, particles,
  bow, arrow, quiver, shield, extra character, extra limb, sexualized armor,
  copied copyrighted character, long crimson cape, cone helmet, horn, animal
  anatomy, graphic gore, or red-dominant palette.

## Internally Rejected or Superseded Attempts

The first concept-03 attempt used a large capiz reliquary shield. The returned
image hid the requested interior grip and forearm strap, so it failed the
project's shield-mechanics rule and was not copied into the collection. It was
replaced by the shieldless Capiz-Mantle Warder.

The first concept-13 render made the hand-and-a-half sword substantially longer
than the researched wearer-height ratio. It was not copied into the collection
and was replaced by the corrected Black-Canon Longsword.
