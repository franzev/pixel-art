# Coastal and Shipwreck Horrors Batch 15 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-28
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design
**Status:** Draft-only

## Shared direction

Create one complete coastal or shipwreck enemy per square image for a gothic 2D
Metroidvania. Use crisp hard-cluster pixel art on a uniform warm near-black
background. Face the gameplay silhouette screen-left, retain generous padding,
and keep props and effects fully visible. Use charcoal, soaked brown, faded
canvas, wet rope, rust, shell ivory, coral rust, slate blue, pale cyan, and
spectral white. Avoid green backgrounds, scenery, smooth rendering, heroic
armor, gore, victims, text, logos, and cropped anatomy.

## Roster

1. **Anchor-Dragged Revenant** — One hooded head, two arms, two hands, two legs,
   two feet, one chain, and one four-fluke anchor dragged screen-right. Two rope
   ends trail behind the body.
2. **Reef-Veil Drowned** — One coral-mask head, two arms and hands, no legs or
   feet, one broad abaca veil with exactly five coral weights, and two seawater
   trails. The design must not resemble the existing White Ladies.
3. **Mast-Clinging Wraith** — One rope-wrapped head, two arms and hands, two
   legs and hooked feet, exactly one broken mast, one sail scrap, and two cold
   water drips.
