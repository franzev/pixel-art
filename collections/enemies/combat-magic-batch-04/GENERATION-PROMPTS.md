# Combat, Magic, Ghost, and Possession Enemy Prompt Set

Generated as 25 separate assets through the built-in image-generation workflow.
Every asset uses a restrained dark base palette plus one controlled combat accent.

## Shared art direction

Create one combat-ready dark-fantasy pixel-art enemy sprite for a 2D gothic
Metroidvania on a square canvas. Center the complete enemy on a plain warm
near-black background with generous padding. Use crisp deliberate pixel clusters,
a readable in-game silhouette, and roughly 16–22 colors.

Use charcoal, ash, faded brown, bone, worn cloth, wood, and aged metal as the
shared foundation. Magic occupies only 10–20 percent of the sprite:

- Blood: black-red, crimson, scarlet
- Acid: ochre, yellow-chartreuse
- Ghost and water: slate blue, pale cyan, spectral white
- Curse: bruised violet and muted plum
- Fire: ember orange and gold
- Insects: amber and violet
- Moon: indigo, silver, and pale blue

Humanoids have one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet. Possessed
civilians retain normal anatomy and recognizable work clothing; possession is
shown as a separate translucent aura or attached spectral silhouette. Avoid plate
armor, heroic knight poses, crimson capes, shields, graphic gore, exposed organs,
accidental extra anatomy, smooth gradients, blur, painterly rendering,
anti-aliasing, green backgrounds, watermarks, and text.
