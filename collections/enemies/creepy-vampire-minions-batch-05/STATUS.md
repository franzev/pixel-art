## Direction

This collection interprets “vampire minions” as low- and mid-tier adult
humanoid enemies rather than vampire knights, aristocratic bosses, ghouls, or
animal-derived creatures.

The approved Veil-Fang Processional and Sampaguita Vow-Matron are visual
references for crisp connected pixel clusters, restrained vampire palette,
pale intact flesh, Philippine-inflected mourning garments, and warm near-black
presentation. Their poses, costumes, faces, props, and silhouettes must not be
copied.

Every design is weaponless. Creepy identity comes from predatory posture,
unblinking faces, visible fangs, rigid or abrupt movement, funeral clothing,
and distinct silhouette geometry. No spells, auras, particles, graphic gore,
animal anatomy, or green backgrounds are allowed.
