## Direction Locks

- Adult female Crown of Thorns knight order.
- Right-facing three-quarter presentation on a square canvas.
- Plain warm near-black background with no scenery, floor, or cast shadow.
- Wearable armor, plausible historical weapon construction, and coherent grips.
- Restrained charcoal, blackened steel, bronze, dirty ivory, indigo, and muted
  violet palette.
- Dry thorn crowns must read as ritual headgear rather than horns or antlers.
- No shields, tattoos, skin markings, graphic gore, green backgrounds, copied
  protagonist silhouettes, or unrequested magic.
- Crisp high-density pixel art with connected clusters and strong 256-pixel
  readability.
