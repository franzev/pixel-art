## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated mature adult Filipina human women forming
an unarmored occult estate court. Family identity comes from one dry woven thorn
crown per character, tailored mourning clothing using baro, pañuelo, saya,
tapis, and abaca cues, severe adult faces, and restrained supernatural accents.

This wave deliberately avoids repeating the armored weapons and silhouettes of
Crown of Thorns Female Knights Batch 38 and the staff, needle volley, vessel,
hooked blade, and oval gate identities of Crowned Thorn Priestesses Batch 45.

## Positive Direction

- Warrior Nuns Batch 34: tall mature proportions, coherent garment
  construction, restrained trim, crisp pixel clusters, and readable props.
- Flesh-Veil Oracle: severe mournful mood, dark value discipline, elegant cloth
  weight, and controlled ember light only.
- Crowned Thorn Priestesses Batch 45: adjacent thorn-crown family signal and
  batch-separation reference only.

All supplied project images were visual references, not edit targets. Exact
poses, costumes, props, effects, and silhouettes were not copied.

## Quality-Control Review

- All five sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG files.
- All five references are 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor RGB PNG files.
- The review sheet is 1280 × 256 and presents the wave in roster order.
- Every image contains exactly one complete adult human woman with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Every figure has exactly one readable dry thorn crown.
- Heads, crowns, hands, props, cloth, effects, and feet remain within the frame
  with safe padding.
- Backgrounds are warm near-black without scenery, floor planes, cast shadows,
  gradients, text, frames, logos, watermarks, or green fields.
- Skin is intact and unmarked; there are no tattoos, scars, blood, punctures, or
  graphic wounds.
- Props and grips remain coherent at full size and at 256 pixels.
- The five silhouettes differ through wide fan diagonal, tall vertical distaff
  plus cord loop, compact low blade, centered salver platform, and recoiling
  book-and-mantle shape.
- Crisp connected pixel clusters and broad values remain readable at 256.
