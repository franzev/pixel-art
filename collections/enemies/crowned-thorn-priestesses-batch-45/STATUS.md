## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five separate isolated mature adult human priestess
enemy concepts. Each figure wears exactly one dry thorn crown as ritual
headgear. Family identity comes from severe field-priest clothing, restrained
Philippine-inflected materials, soot-dark values, and dry briar implements
rather than generic Western cathedral scenery, gore, or thorn-covered armor.

## Draft roster

1. **Briar-Ring Processional** — advancing lane controller with one
   capiz-centered processional staff.
2. **Wreath-Needle Cantor** — narrow stationary artillery caster releasing
   exactly three spectral thorn needles.
3. **Root-Tithe Sower** — compact kneeling trap deployer carrying one dormant
   root knot in one earthen bowl.
4. **Black-Hedge Sacristan** — wide diagonal close-range ambusher using one
   human-scale Filipino pruning bolo.
5. **Crown-Gate Hierophant** — hovering elite support priestess holding one
   oval briar ward ring beneath a five-spire crown.

## Reference direction

The Flesh-Veil Oracle was supplied to the built-in image-generation workflow as
a visual reference only. The drafts preserve its confirmed high-level
qualities: mature statuesque presence, severe occult restraint, warm-dark value
discipline, layered weathered cloth, worn bronze, controlled warm magic, and
visible pixel clusters.

Its exact pose, horned hood, costume panels, hand arrangement, spell-orb column,
ritual markings, and silhouette were not copied.

The Crown of Thorns Female Knights Batch 38 and Blood Priestesses Batch 39
review sheets were inspected only to prevent silhouette, equipment, and role
duplication. They were not used as positive image inputs.

## Quality-control review

- All five sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG images.
- Each source contains exactly one complete mature adult human woman with one
  head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two visible feet.
- All five face screen-left and preserve complete crowns, hands, equipment,
  cloth, and feet with comfortable padding.
- Counted equipment and effects pass:
  - one staff, one bronze ring, and one capiz disc;
  - exactly three spectral thorn needles;
  - one earthen bowl and one contained root knot;
  - one pruning bolo and one empty open hand;
  - one five-spire crown, one handheld briar ring, and one contained ward plane.
- Grips and load-bearing alignments are readable at full size and 256 pixels.
- The five silhouettes remain distinct at 256 pixels: advancing staff vertical,
  narrow artillery fan, low kneeling triangle, wide melee diagonal, and compact
  hovering barrier.
- Every background is warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast
  shadow, gradient, text, frame, watermark, or green field.
- Pixel construction remains crisp and readable at 256 pixels.
- No draft includes animal anatomy, demon anatomy, wings, horns, antlers,
  graphic gore, red-dominant skin or clothing, extra characters, or copied
  protagonist equipment.
