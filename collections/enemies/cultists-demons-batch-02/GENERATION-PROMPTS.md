# Weird Cultists and Demons — Prompt Set

Generated through the built-in image-generation workflow. Every subject was
generated separately to protect its silhouette and concept.

## Shared direction

Create one original weird and scary but restrained dark-fantasy pixel-art enemy
sprite for a 2D gothic Metroidvania on a square canvas. Show one complete full-body
subject centered on a plain warm near-black background with generous padding.
Use crisp deliberate pixel clusters, a readable in-game silhouette, and a
restrained palette of charcoal, ash grey, faded brown, aged brass or stone, dirty
bone, and tiny controlled warm or spectral-white highlights. No green.

Make the subject frightening through masks, ritual props, unnatural posture,
deceptive shapes, and supernatural materials—not gore or body horror. Humanoid
cultists must have one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet, naturally
attached and clearly readable. Avoid exposed organs, wounds, fused bodies,
accidental extra limbs or faces, plate armor, heroic knight poses, crimson capes,
standard swords, shields, smooth gradients, blur, painterly rendering,
anti-aliasing, watermarks, and text.
