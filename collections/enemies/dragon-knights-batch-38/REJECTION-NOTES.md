# Dragon Knights Batch 38 — Rejection and Revision Notes

## v01 direction

The first five drafts were not accepted as the active review direction.

The user's exact feedback was:

> doesnt scream "dragon knights" they look generic and no faction

Operational lessons:

- Functional armor alone does not establish a dragon faction.
- Small ember accents and dragon-themed names are insufficient.
- A coherent order needs repeated, readable helmet construction, heraldry,
  armor geometry, materials, palette, and ritual equipment.
- The v01 images are superseded evidence and must not be used as positive visual
  references for future Dragon Knight work.

The v01 files remain preserved as unapproved drafts. They were not deleted,
promoted, or added to lore.

## v02 response

The v02 Order of the Eclipsed Maw direction introduces these shared locks:

- Hinged dragon-jaw visors with copper fang plates.
- Two long swept-back horn crests.
- Blackened scale-shaped plate rows.
- Copper folded-wing rib pauldrons.
- Deep eclipse-indigo cloth.
- Three tail-shaped tassets.
- An ivory crescent clenched between two copper fangs as the faction badge.
- Controlled ember accents.

The five v02 roles vary through standard, lance, shield, chain-thurible, and
execution-falchion silhouettes while retaining those faction markers.
