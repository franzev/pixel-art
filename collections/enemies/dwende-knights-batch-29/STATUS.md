## Reference and folklore direction

The Mosscloak Marcher and Grove-Gate Warden were used as visual references only for mature proportions, wearable armor, restrained materials, isolated presentation, and small-size readability.

The batch is an original game interpretation informed by Philippine dwende traditions involving compact elder-like beings, spirit mounds, thresholds, local woven materials, and respectful boundary etiquette. It is not a definitive cultural portrayal.
