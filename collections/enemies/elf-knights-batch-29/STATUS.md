## Reference direction

The retained Wild Covenant Mosscloak Marcher and Grove-Gate Warden were used as
visual references only for mature adult proportions, crisp pixel construction,
wearable armor, restrained materials, and isolated presentation. None of their
exact poses, helmets, armor layouts, cloaks, shields, spears, equipment,
heraldry, palettes, or silhouettes were requested.

The existing Forest Knights Batch 26 was inspected to avoid repeating its sling,
capture net, field horn, lantern staff, and pursuit axe identities.

Wave 02 continues the elf family's anatomy, palette, and material language while
adding restrained forest-duty cues through damp cloth, muted moss accents,
inert root-pattern bindings, rattan, weathered hardwood, and practical patrol
equipment. The first five drafts were used only as unapproved batch-continuity
evidence; this does not imply approval.

## Family direction

- Adult humanoid elves with coherent human anatomy and two naturally attached
  pointed ears.
- Wearable dark armor, restrained abaca and capiz materials, and severe
  Ashen Provinces presentation.
- Charcoal, soot black, dull iron, dirty ivory, weathered brown, and controlled
  moon-indigo replace generic bright-green woodland or gold high-elf styling.
- One clear encounter role and a distinct posture, equipment set, armor layout,
  cloth construction, and silhouette for every member.
- Forest influence remains practical and weathered rather than bright leafy
  costume armor, animal anatomy, decorative branches, or a green wash.
