# Experimental-Haired Inquisitors Batch 51 — Status

Last updated: 2026-07-31

## Current State

Five outputs from the interrupted first hair-direction wave are now saved as a
separate gallery collection at the user's request. They are deliberately kept
separate from `short-haired-inquisitors-batch-48`, whose no-long-hair direction
remains unchanged.

These renders are visible review drafts only. None is approved, retained,
canonical, or production-ready.

## Drafts

1. Ivory Cascade Interrogator — floor-length ivory-white hair
2. Crimson Crown Castellan — copper-red crown braids and long braided tails
3. Raven Coil Prosecutor — oversized raven-black pinned curls
4. Ash-Rose Bob Examiner — sculpted ash-rose chin bob
5. Gilt Cable Justiciar — long honey-gold cable braids

## Origin

The renders completed after their generation call had been interrupted. They
were not initially copied into the project because the user replaced that
direction with a short-hair reference. On 2026-07-31, the user specifically
asked why the unusual-hair and long-white-hair renders were absent from the
gallery, so the preserved originals were added here without altering Batch 48.

## Quality and Lifecycle

- Five separate 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG renders.
- Exactly one complete adult woman per square image.
- No weapons, shields, ammunition, scenery, visible spells, or green
  backgrounds.
- Distinct facial structures and hair silhouettes.
- The long-hair concepts are exceptions isolated to this experimental
  collection and do not reverse Batch 48's short-hair lock.
