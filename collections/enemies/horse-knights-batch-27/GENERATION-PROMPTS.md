# Horse Knights Batch 27 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-30  
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Interpretation and visual references

Create five separate mounted horse-knight enemy concepts for the Ashen
Provinces. Each image contains one complete adult human knight riding one
complete adult horse and is treated as one mounted character asset.

These two retained Wild Covenant sources were supplied to the image generator
as visual references:

- `archive/legacy-art/protagonist/wild-covenant-v06/drafts/08-storm-rook-cavalier-source.png`
- `archive/legacy-art/protagonist/wild-covenant-v06/drafts/02-grove-gate-warden-source.png`

They control only mature proportions, deliberate pixel construction, wearable
armor, restrained weathered materials, and isolated presentation. Their exact
poses, helmets, armor layouts, shields, weapons, heraldry, motifs, garment
panels, and silhouettes must not be copied.

## Review gate

Inspect every generation at full resolution and at 256 × 256. Reject malformed
human or horse anatomy, incorrect counts, missing padding, non-pixel rendering,
green backgrounds, scenery, ambiguous equipment, or duplicate silhouettes.
Save viable work only in the batch's `drafts/` paths and keep every asset
draft-only until the user explicitly approves it.
