## Interpretation

The request was treated as five separate mounted enemy character concepts. Each
image contains one complete adult human knight riding one complete adult horse
and is presented as one combined mounted asset.

## Draft roster

1. Road-Toll Lance Reeve — disciplined straight-line charge enemy.
2. Rainline Bow Harrier — mobile ranged harassment and retreat enemy.
3. Funeral-Rein Sabreur — fast ride-by close-pursuit enemy.
4. Abaca-Rein Catcher — leap interruption and pull-control enemy.
5. Hollow-Bridle Lantern Warden — slow occult detection patrol.

## Reference direction

The retained Wild Covenant Storm-Rook Cavalier and Grove-Gate Warden were used
as visual references only for mature proportions, crisp pixel construction,
wearable armor, restrained materials, and isolated presentation. Their exact
poses, helmets, equipment, motifs, garments, and silhouettes were not copied.
