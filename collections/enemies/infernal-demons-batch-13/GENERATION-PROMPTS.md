## Shared direction

Create fifteen original infernal enemies for a 2D gothic Metroidvania. Favor
lean or statuesque humanoid demons, goat- and ram-headed figures, occult
casters, winged hunters, duelists, and severe elites. Use infernal scarlet,
bruised flesh-pink, dirty bone, soot-black, blackened horns, and controlled
ember-orange light on a warm near-black background.

Use genuine crisp low-shade pixel art: hard square pixels, clean stepped edges,
large connected clusters, deliberate negative space, and two or three shades
per material. Show one complete creature per square source, facing screen-left,
with all limbs, horns, wings, tails, weapons, and spell effects uncropped.

Flesh-pink surfaces are intact supernatural hide. Avoid exposed organs, flaying,
realistic wounds, victims, and graphic gore. Use broad grimdark infernal
inspiration without copying recognizable Warhammer creatures, factions, names,
symbols, armor, weapons, silhouettes, or color arrangements.
