## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated full-height adult humanoid demonic
knights. Each design carries exactly one dominant inverted Latin-cross idea,
shown with the long stem above the crossbar and the short stem below it. The
family remains person-like, cool-neutral, non-graphic, and mechanically
wearable rather than animal-derived, imp-like, red-dominant, or brute-shaped.

## Reference role

The Flesh-Veil Oracle supplied the strongest confirmed occult direction for
statuesque adult proportion, soot-dark layering, worn copper, dirty ivory,
controlled ember light, and mournful restraint. The Pivot-Visor Penitent
supplied armor-construction guidance for human-scale articulated plate, helmet
openings, and plausible equipment handling. Both were visual references, not
edit targets; no exact pose, costume, prop placement, symbol, or silhouette was
copied.

## Draft roster

1. **Inverted-Rood Spine Lancer** — tall reach-control sentinel carrying one
   diagonal spear beneath one rigid inverted-cross back yoke.
2. **Downcast-Visor Estoc Duelist** — narrow punish-and-counter fencer whose
   closed visor uses one inverted-cross sight opening.
3. **Hollow-Rood Gauntlet Pursuer** — low close-range capture enemy with one
   reinforced open-palm gauntlet and one ember-lit inverted-cross cuirass void.
4. **Downward-Vow Arbalist** — kneeling artillery knight with one loaded
   arbalest and one black inverted cross on a dirty-ivory oath apron.
5. **Inverted-Rood Ward Cantor** — hovering barrier controller shaping one
   isolated ember inverted-cross spell beside a plain armored body.

## Quality-control review

- All five sources are separate 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs.
- Each source contains exactly one complete adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, cloth, weapon tips, and spell geometry remain inside the
  frame with usable padding.
- Each design shows one dominant inverted-cross idea with the correct
  long-above, short-below orientation.
- Equipment remains countable: one spear; one estoc; one reinforced gauntlet
  and no weapon; one arbalest with one loaded bolt; and one isolated spell
  geometry with no weapon.
- Armor is lean or medium and mechanically wearable, with readable neck
  clearance, joint articulation, and plausible grips.
- Backgrounds remain warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast shadow,
  text, border, logo, watermark, or green field.
- Crisp connected pixel clusters and broad value masses remain legible on the
  1280 × 256 review sheet.
- Silhouettes remain distinct at review scale: tall crossed diagonals, narrow
  fencing line, wide open-palm pursuit, low horizontal kneel, and calm hovering
  vertical.
- No animal anatomy, horns, wings, imps, bulky brute body, graphic gore,
  red-dominant palette, cone helmet, long crimson cape, or copied copyrighted
  silhouette was accepted.

## Internally rejected passes

Seven generations were excluded before the final review wave:

1. The first lancer and first duelist showed upright rather than inverted
   Latin-cross proportions.
2. A corrected lancer pass reduced the symbol to a one-sided T-like yoke.
3. Two arbalist passes failed their requested ammunition counts.
4. The first ward-cantor pass added unrequested cross-like torso jewelry.
5. A viable lancer composition was replaced because its ember tip sat too close
   to the top edge.

Rejected passes were not kept in the project collection. Their built-in
generation originals remain outside the repository.
