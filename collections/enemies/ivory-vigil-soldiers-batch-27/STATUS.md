## Direction

The supplied silver-armored fountain-guard image was used as a visual reference
only. The wave preserves its lean ceremonial presence, polished silver plate,
black-and-dirty-ivory cloth, restrained dull-gold engraving, white-feather
grandeur, and severe mood. None of the drafts copies its exact pose, paired
winged helmet, costume, spear, heraldry, prop placement, or silhouette.

All ten are complete mature adult humanoids facing screen-left on plain warm
near-black backgrounds. Armor stays wearable and jointed, with close-fitting
helmets, sight lines, breaths, neck clearance, human-scale equipment, and lean
or believable medium builds.

Drafts 06–10 form a dedicated melee-and-capes extension. Every member carries
only human-scale melee weaponry and has one complete, visibly readable cape. The
extension contains no firearm, bow, projectile, thrown weapon, standard, ranged
gear, or spell effect.
