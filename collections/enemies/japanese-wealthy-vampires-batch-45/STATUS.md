## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated full-height mature adult Japanese women
who are wealthy vampire enemies. The family uses historically grounded,
fictional elite Japanese clothing and status materials with restrained gothic
vampire cues. The designs avoid geisha caricature, schoolgirl styling, generic
Western vampire costume, animal anatomy, red-dominant bodies, graphic gore, and
duplicate fan or parasol silhouettes.

## Quality-control review

- All five sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs.
- All five references are 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor PNGs.
- The review sheet is 1280 × 256 and presents the drafts in roster order.
- Each image contains exactly one complete mature adult humanoid with one head,
  two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Heads, hands, props, effects, garment hems, feet, and weapon tips remain
  inside the frame with safe padding.
- Backgrounds remain warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast shadow,
  text, border, logo, watermark, or green field.
- Crisp connected pixel clusters and broad value masses remain legible at
  256 pixels.
- The family reads through pale intact faces, visible restrained fangs, small
  red irises, elite Japanese silk construction, black lacquer, indigo,
  dirty ivory, worn silver, dirty gold, and controlled occult effects.
- All five use a consistent screen-right presentation.
- The silhouettes and gameplay identities remain distinct at 256 pixels.
- Counted props and effects are readable: one case and three seals; one kiseru
  and one smoke ring; one katana and one scabbard; one censer and three rings;
  one naginata.
