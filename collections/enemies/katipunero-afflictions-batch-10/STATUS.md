This batch is reserved for 15 alternate-history Katipunero enemy concepts:

1. bite infection
2. ghoul hunger
3. corpse animation
4. possession
5. curse marks
6. vampirism
7. root growth
8. fire
9. rain
10. insects
11. smoke
12. shadow
13. salt
14. moonlight
15. weapon-bound mutation

The user rejected the original recognizably human direction. All fifteen
characters were regenerated as completely non-human monsters. Torn period
clothing and historically plausible weapons remain only as readable traces of
the former fighters. The external afflictions remain the source of the horror;
Filipino identity, culture, and anti-colonial resistance are not.

The superseding monster direction is recorded at the top of
`GENERATION-PROMPTS.md`.
