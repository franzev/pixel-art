## Interpretation and Reference Direction

The request was interpreted as five separate isolated adult-humanoid enemy
concepts in one review wave. They are fictional corrupted colonial friars, not
depictions of a real religious order.

The retained Ash-Veil Processioner was used only as a visual-family reference.
The wave preserves barefoot monastic austerity, weathered layered cloth,
mournful occult menace, warm near-black isolation, mature proportions, and
crisp connected pixel clusters. It does not copy the reference's pose, veil,
candle, garment panels, anatomy details, or silhouette.

The new roster also avoids the crook, palm liturgy, bolo, cord, pestle,
thurible, missal, chalice, and summoner silhouettes already used in the Evil
Monks and Evil Priests draft waves.

## Quality-Control Review

- All five sources are square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG images on warm near-black
  backgrounds.
- Every image contains exactly one complete adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two visible bare feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, clothing, weapons, tools, wisps, and flexible extensions
  remain fully framed with usable padding.
- Defining counts remain readable: one blowpipe; one small paten razor; one
  sword blade and one matching cane scabbard; one lidded bowl and exactly three
  poison wisps; and one continuous solid rattan switch.
- Weapon and prop grips are mechanically coherent at full size and remain
  legible in the 256-pixel references.
- The five silhouettes remain distinct on the 1280 × 256 review sheet.
- No draft contains a second character, extra face, animal anatomy, horn, wing,
  halo, victim, exposed organ, wet gore, scenery, floor plane, cast shadow,
  green background, or red-dominant body palette.
- Five 256 × 256 references were created with nearest-neighbor resizing.
