## Reference direction

The wearable armor, mature proportions, restrained palette, and crisp
pixel-cluster treatment of the Pivot-Visor Penitent were used as the closest
positive direction. Its exact helmet, pose, armor, cape, halberd, and silhouette
were not copied.

The family expresses penitence through abaca bindings, woven palm fiber, capiz,
ash cloth, river-stone burdens, bowed posture, and vow gestures. These cues keep
the group rooted in the Ashen Provinces rather than generic Western cathedral
fantasy.
