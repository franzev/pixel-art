# Reapers Batch 40 — Status

## Goal

Create five distinct Ashen Provinces reapers as isolated adult humanoid enemy
concepts, each with a separate gameplay role, posture, clothing system, weapon
geometry, and movement implication.

## Draft wave

1. Rice-Wake Reaper — low advancing lane sweeper
2. Funeral-Lane Reaper — upright passage controller
3. Severed-Thread Reaper — reactive counter-duelist
4. Drowned-Road Reaper — planted puller
5. Ember-Writ Reaper — drifting delayed executioner

## Direction

- Philippine-inflected rural and mourning materials rather than generic Western
  cathedral fantasy.
- Mature humanoid anatomy, restrained ornament, and mechanically plausible
  scythe grips.
- Soot-black, charcoal, dirty ivory, bark, dull iron, and limited role-specific
  accents.
- Plain warm near-black isolated presentation.
- Deliberate crisp pixel clusters readable at 256 pixels.
