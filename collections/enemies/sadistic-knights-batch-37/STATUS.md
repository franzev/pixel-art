## Interpretation and Reference Direction

The request was interpreted as five separate isolated adult humanoid
enemy-knight concepts in one review wave.

The Mercy Executioner from Sadistic Nuns Batch 35 controlled only the confirmed
positive high-level qualities of restrained non-graphic cruelty, mature
proportions, grounded human-scale weapons, crisp pixel clusters, and warm
near-black isolation. The Low-Gate Warhammer Breaker from Ivory Vigil Soldiers
Batch 27 controlled only wearable articulated armor, believable human volume,
joint clearance, plausible weapon handling, restrained metal values, and pixel
density.

No reference pose, helmet, costume, prop placement, palette, heraldry, or
silhouette was copied. The Crimson Knight's defining long crimson cape,
one-handed sword, and vigilant heroic silhouette were excluded.

## Quality-Control Review

- All five sources are square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG files.
- All five 256 × 256 references were created with nearest-neighbor resampling.
- The review sheet is 1280 × 256 and presents the five references in roster
  order.
- Every image contains exactly one complete adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two visible armored feet.
- All helmets, hands, weapons, cloth, knees, and feet remain inside the square
  sources with safe padding.
- Equipment reads as one seven-thong scourge; one C-shaped mancatcher with two
  inward hooks; one two-handed executioner's sword; one compact flail with five
  large chain links and one studded ball; and one crossbow with one loaded
  barbed quarrel.
- All five figures face screen-left in distinct advancing, backward-braced,
  upright, turning, and kneeling postures.
- Armor remains human-wearable with neck clearance, sight and breathing paths,
  articulated joints, plausible grips, and human-scale weapons.
- The batch remains readable at 256 pixels through five different silhouette
  anchors: lash fan, diagonal capture pole, vertical execution blade, hanging
  flail arc, and horizontal crossbow.
- The palette stays within blackened iron, charcoal, soot, weathered brown,
  dirty ivory, aged copper or bronze, and small controlled accents.
- No image contains a victim, blood, wounds, exposed organs, graphic gore,
  magic, shield, scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, text, green background,
  animal anatomy, or protagonist equipment combination.
