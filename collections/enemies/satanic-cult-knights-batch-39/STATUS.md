## Review notes

- Each design is a separate 1254 × 1254 square RGB source on a warm near-black
  isolated background.
- Each source has a matching 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor reference under
  `public/art/enemies/satanic-cult-knights-batch-39/drafts/`.
- The review sheet is 1280 × 256 and presents the five references in roster
  order.
- Every design is one complete full-height adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, two feet, wearable articulated armor, and
  human-scale equipment.
- The wave varies posture, center of gravity, weapon doctrine, helmet profile,
  garment construction, negative space, and encounter role.
- The family is unified by blackened iron, soot-dark cloth, dirty ivory abaca,
  worn copper, and restrained warm accents.
- No design uses animal anatomy, goat traits, horns, wings, imps, red-dominant
  bodies, bulky tank armor, graphic gore, scenery, green background, a
  penitential cone helmet, or the Crimson Knight's defining equipment
  combination.
- These are concept masters and review references, not native production
  sprites.
