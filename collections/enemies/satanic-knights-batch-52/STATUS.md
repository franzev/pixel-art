## Interpretation

This is a new mixed-gender enemy-knight wave, not a continuation or recast of
`satanic-cult-knights-batch-39`. The cast is three adult men and two adult
women. Satanic identity is expressed through a disciplined anti-liturgical
order, defaced solar devotional objects, matte black wax, filed nail marks, and
physical desecration of armor. It does not use demon anatomy, animal-derived
helmets, horns, visible spells, floating effects, graphic gore, or a
red-dominant body palette.

The closest accepted direction is the 5/5 Sable Longsword Castellan for an
adult martial presence, readable human-scale equipment, and clear silhouette.
It was inspected at full size and 256 pixels as a comparison only. It is not an
image-generation input, and its armor, sword pose, palette, cloth, face, and
silhouette are not copied.

- One complete mature adult humanoid per square 1:1 image.
- Mostly frontal shallow front-three-quarter presentation, slightly biased
  toward screen-right; the forward knee, leading foot, weight, attack line, and
  weapon active end must lead right.
- Perfectly uniform `#171311` background from edge to edge.
- Deliberately pixel-authored high-density pixel art, readable at 256 pixels.
- No visible spells, spell-like effects, particles, smoke, scenery, floor,
  cast shadow, vignette, text, frame, extra anatomy, or unrequested props.
- Every weapon must pass the serial equipment gate before the next concept is
  generated.
- Every raw generator result enters ordered attempt History before inspection.
- Catalog placement, if any result passes, means unreviewed and available for
  review only.
