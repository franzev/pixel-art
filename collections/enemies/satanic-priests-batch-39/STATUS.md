## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated adult humanoid priests belonging to a
fictional infernal order in the Ashen Provinces. Their identity is carried by
severe clerical silhouettes, inverted geometry, soot-dark ritual clothing,
weathered bronze and copper, capiz and abaca details, and controlled ember
effects. No real cultural or spiritual role is presented as inherently evil.

## Reference role

The Flesh-Veil Oracle from
`collections/enemies/infernal-demons-batch-13/drafts/03-flesh-veil-oracle-source.png`
was used as the strongest positive visual direction for elegant adult
proportions, crisp pixel construction, soot-dark layered clothing, pale intact
skin, worn copper, controlled ember light, and mournful occult presence. It was
a visual reference, not an edit target; its exact pose, hood, garments, hands,
spell column, and silhouette were not copied.

## Quality-control review

- All five sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs.
- Each image contains exactly one complete adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two separated feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, cloth, tools, weapons, smoke, and spell effects remain
  inside the frame with usable padding.
- Repeated elements are countable: one sternum sigil; one chain and one censer;
  one codex, one short security chain, and three ember needles; one
  staff-mounted disc; and one bolo.
- Hands and wrists connect plausibly to the censer chain, codex, ritual
  standard, and bolo.
- Backgrounds are warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast shadow,
  text, border, logo, watermark, or green field.
- Crisp connected clusters and broad value masses remain legible at 256 × 256.
- Silhouettes remain distinct at review scale: closed symmetric command pose,
  forward stride with a low swinging prop, tall book-and-projectile fan, wide
  low standard brace, and stalking diagonal melee pose.
- Red is not the dominant body or garment palette. Ember orange remains a
  controlled gameplay accent.
- No animal anatomy, horns, wings, imp-like proportions, bulky brutes, graphic
  gore, copied copyrighted character silhouette, or duplicate family member
  was accepted.
- The first Capiz-Eclipse Deacon attempt was rejected because the visible
  disc-face grip was mechanically ambiguous. It was not copied into the
  project. The saved replacement uses an unambiguous two-hand staff grip.
