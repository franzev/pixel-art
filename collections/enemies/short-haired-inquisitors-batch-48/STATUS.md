# Short-Haired Inquisitors Batch 48 — Status

Last updated: 2026-07-31

## Current State

Two review waves contain ten original adult female knight-inquisitor drafts.
Wave 1 established uncovered, short, face-framing hair as the collection's
primary visual identity. Wave 2 follows the user's newer direction for five
ashen-haired, weapon-bearing militant-sisterhood inquisitors with prominent
bangs.

“Adepta Sororitas inspired” controls only disciplined warrior-nun weight,
severe religious militancy, and practical armored presence. No franchise
insignia, exact armor, backpack, symbol, pose, weapon silhouette, or character
is copied.

No asset is approved, retained, canonical, or production-ready.

## Hair Direction Lock

- Hair, bangs, and hair color are primary visual identity.
- Every Wave 2 woman has prominent, graphic bangs readable at 256 pixels.
- Wave 2 fringe types are blunt, sweeping asymmetrical, crescent pageboy,
  broken side-swept, and sculpted S-wave.
- All Wave 2 hair is ashen: ash white, cool silver ash, smoke grey, pale
  ash-blonde, and charcoal ash.
- Every cut ends at the jaw or collar; no hair extends below the shoulders.
- Hair remains uncovered: no helmets, hoods, veils, crowns, or headpieces.
- No buzz cuts, shaved-side undercuts, mohawks, fauxhawks, high-and-tight cuts,
  graphic shaved patterns, neon dyes, or multicolor fantasy dyes.
