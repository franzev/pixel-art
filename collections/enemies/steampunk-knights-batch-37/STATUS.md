## Direction

The retained Mosscloak Marcher and Grove-Gate Warden were used as the closest
accepted knight direction for mature proportions, wearable armor, restrained
materials, clustered pixel treatment, and isolated presentation. None of their
exact poses, helmets, armor layouts, equipment, heraldry, or silhouettes was
requested.

Steampunk identity is expressed through compact pressure mechanisms, worn
copper and brass, capiz gauge glass, abaca padding, and medieval-industrial
equipment. The wave avoids generic Victorian scenery, loose gear decoration,
box-shaped armor, implausible machinery, and modern tactical styling.
