## Interpretation

The request was treated as five separate adult humanoid Treant knight enemy
concepts. “Treant” means coherent person-like arboreal anatomy with bark,
heartwood, root-shaped feet, and wearable armor—not animal anatomy and not a
normal human knight with decorative branches.

The Flesh-Veil Oracle was supplied to generation as a visual-treatment
reference only. Its mature proportions, severe mood, connected pixel clusters,
broad values, restrained materials, and controlled warm lighting were retained
at a high level. Its pose, costume, veil, profile, spell arrangement, and
silhouette were not copied.

The Balete Forest Court drafts were inspected only to avoid duplicating their
root crown, leaf-duelist, and mushroom-lantern silhouettes. They were not
treated as approved references.

## Quality-control result

Wave 1 Designs 01–05 passed on their first generation attempts. Wave 2 Designs
06, 08, 09, and 10 passed on their first attempts.

Wave 2 Design 07 passed on attempt 4. Three earlier attempts were rejected
internally and were not copied into the project: one had an ambiguous extra
leaf, one undershot the requested leaf count, and one failed to show both hands
gripping the vine. The retained draft has one complete vine ring, exactly four
oversized leaves, and two visible closed grips.
