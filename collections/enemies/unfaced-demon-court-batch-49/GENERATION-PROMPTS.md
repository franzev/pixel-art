# Unfaced Demon Court Batch 49 — Generation Prompts

Last updated: 2026-07-31

## Shared direction

Ten separate infernal-humanoid enemy concepts in two waves of five. The Sable
Longsword Castellan is a visual treatment reference only for deliberate pixel
density, connected clusters, restrained color ramps, warm near-black isolation,
and mature full-body proportions. Do not copy its face, armor, pose, sword,
costume, or silhouette.

Every concept is one full-height adult person-like demon with one anatomical
head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet. Horror comes from masks,
bindings, rigid posture, garment construction, and altered facial presentation.
No animal anatomy, animal head, horns, hooves, wings, tail, imp proportions,
bulky brute anatomy, red-dominant body palette, exposed organs, graphic gore,
visible spells, aura, smoke, droplets, particles, scenery, text, or green
background.
