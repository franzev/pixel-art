## Direction

This batch explores full-height person-like demons whose horror comes from
masks, bindings, external restraints, funerary garments, and rigid posture.
It explicitly excludes animal anatomy, goat traits, imps, bulky brutes,
red-dominant bodies, graphic gore, and visible spell effects.

The Sable Longsword Castellan is a visual treatment reference only for crisp
pixel construction, restrained values, mature full-body proportions, and
warm-near-black isolation. Its face, armor, pose, costume, sword, and silhouette
must not be copied.

## Internal review

- All fifteen figures preserve complete adult silhouettes on a plain warm
  near-black field and remain readable in the three 1280×256 review sheets.
- Armed figures have visible hand contacts, conventional weapon joins, and
  coherent load paths. The first Cage-Rib Warden render was rejected and
  regenerated because its lower hand did not contact the halberd shaft.
- The first Backward Processioner render and two targeted retries did not make
  the rear-mounted-mask staging fully literal. The strongest draft keeps the
  true face concealed and the ivory face visibly mounted on the hood, but the
  front-versus-rear read remains a review point rather than an approved fact.
- Wave 3 passed its first internal quality review without regeneration:
  counted latches, crossed restraint bands, garment arch, palm plates, visible
  fingers, and complete limbs all remain readable at review scale.
- Wave 4 preserves the selected blank-face, black-iron restraint, old-teal
  cloth, and conventional-weapon direction while replacing the source rib cage
  with five distinct hardware systems. All grip and join crops passed review.
  The first Crown-Cage Butcher attempt was rejected and corrected because its
  cleaver acquired an unrequested decorative blade hole.
- No draft uses animal anatomy, goat traits, imp proportions, red-dominant
  flesh, graphic gore, visible spell effects, scenery, or text.
