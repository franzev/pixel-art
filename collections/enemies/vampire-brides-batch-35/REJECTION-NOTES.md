# Vampire Brides Batch 35 — Rejection Notes

Last updated: 2026-07-30

## Rejected set

The complete first extension pass for designs 06–25 is rejected.

## Explicit user feedback

> they look copies just different colors... OMG please everyone should have
> unique pose

## What failed

- The pass repeated a five-role template—sword, bow, dagger, spear, weaponless
  rush—across four faction palettes.
- Dress color and trim changed more than center of gravity, torso angle, limb
  arrangement, action line, and movement implication.
- Multiple archers used nearly the same lateral full-draw silhouette.
- Multiple spear users used nearly the same forward lunge.
- Multiple weaponless brides used nearly the same reaching sprint.
- The result read as recolored copies even where garment details differed.

## Mandatory correction

- Solve pose before faction, palette, dress, or weapon.
- Give every replacement a unique combination of facing, torso angle, center of
  gravity, leg geometry, arm geometry, action line, and movement implication.
- Compare each pose silhouette against all approved designs 01–05 and every
  replacement already generated.
- Do not reuse the rejected 06–25 images as positive references.
- Preserve the earlier direct locks: murderous intent, ordinary understandable
  weapons only, no ropes, no chains, no shields, and no weird handles.

The rejected sources, references, review sheets, and prompt record are preserved
under `rejected/duplicate-pose-pass/` only as negative evidence.
