## Interpretation

The initial brief was treated as five isolated adult feminine vampire-knight
enemy concepts. The continuation added five more concepts with visibly
increased but limited skin exposure. The supplied desktop image guided body
proportion only: tall, long-legged, narrow-waisted, and subtly curving,
moderated to believable 7.5–8-head adult anatomy rather than exaggerated
fashion-figure proportions.

Wave 2 treats exposed skin as practical mobility or ritual austerity, not
seduction. Each design exposes only one purposeful region while keeping the
breasts, groin, buttocks, and most of the torso covered.

For Wave 3, the user rejected exposed lower legs as visually strange for the
family and directed future skin exposure toward arms or thighs. Wave 3 therefore
keeps both legs fully armored from knee to toe and uses either bare arms or
controlled outer upper-thigh mobility panels. See `REJECTION-NOTES.md`.

After Wave 3, the user judged Drafts 01–15 too repetitive in hairstyle, face,
hair color, and body musculature. A ten-design continuation was started with an
explicit identity-diversity matrix, but the user stopped and fully rejected the
Wave 4 direction before it was saved. No Draft 16 or later exists in this
collection. The collection remains at fifteen unapproved drafts.
