## QC Result

- All ten source images are 1254 × 1254 pixels.
- All ten display references are RGB 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor copies.
- Every figure retains a soft feminine adult silhouette without visible
  muscular definition.
- Every dress is visibly faded, torn, repaired, dirty, and battle-worn.
- Every figure has multiple readable wounds and dark crimson blood on skin,
  cloth, hands, bandages, and/or weapon.
- Every figure carries exactly one worn but intact realistic melee weapon.
- No accepted render contains an arrow, bolt, bow, crossbow, quiver, firearm,
  ammunition, or arrow-like projectile motif.
- The first war-hammer attempt was rejected after its head read as an axe; the
  accepted replacement has a compact hammer face and small rear beak.
- The batch remains draft-only pending explicit user approval.

## Direction Locks

- Clearly adult women approximately 24–30 years old.
- Soft feminine silhouettes with narrow shoulders and no muscular definition.
- Worn, faded, torn, repaired and dirty dresses; no pristine showroom finish.
- Multiple readable combat wounds, bruises, blood-soaked bandages, and dark
  crimson blood on skin, cloth, hands, and weapons.
- No tattoos or decorative markings on skin.
- Both eyes remain fully covered.
- Exactly one realistic, worn but unbroken melee weapon per character.
- Absolutely no arrows, bolts, bows, crossbows, quivers, firearms, ammunition,
  or arrow-like decorative/projectile imagery.
- Natural garment coverage; no awkward underwear or censorship panels.
- One complete character per square image on a warm near-black background.
- No shields, magic, scenery, missing limbs, exposed organs, or copied prior
  silhouettes.
