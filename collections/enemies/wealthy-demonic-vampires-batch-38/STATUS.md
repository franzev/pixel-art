## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated full-height adult humanoid wealthy
demonic-vampire enemy concepts for one review wave. The family combines
Philippine-inflected provincial elite clothing, aristocratic vampire cues, and
restrained infernal manifestations without animal anatomy, red-dominant bodies,
graphic gore, or generic Western cathedral fantasy.

- All five sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs.
- All five references are 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor RGB PNGs.

- Each image contains exactly one complete adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Heads, hands, props, effects, garment hems, and feet remain inside the frame
  with generous padding.
- Backgrounds remain warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast shadow,
  text, border, logo, watermark, or green field.
- Crisp connected pixel clusters and broad value masses remain legible at
  256 pixels.
- The family reads through pallid intact faces, red irises, restrained infernal
  cues, soot-dark elite tailoring, dirty ivory, indigo/plum, worn gold, and
  controlled ember light rather than bat anatomy or red bodies.
- All five use a consistent right-facing presentation.
- The five silhouettes and gameplay identities remain distinct at 256 pixels.
- Counted props and effects are readable: one ledger and three seals; one
  five-panel fan; one blade and one scabbard; one reliquary and four wards; one
  parasol and three tassels.
