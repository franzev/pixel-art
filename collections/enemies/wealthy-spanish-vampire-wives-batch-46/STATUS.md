## Interpretation

The terse brief was interpreted as five isolated full-height adult Peninsular
Spanish vampire-wife enemies from the wealthy late-nineteenth-century colonial
elite in the Philippines. They are mature married women rather than brides,
nuns, children, generic assassins, or animal-derived vampires.

The approved Vampire Brides 01–05 sheet is the primary positive family
reference. It was used as a visual reference rather than an edit target. The new
wave preserves mature proportions, pale readable faces, dark luxury clothing,
restrained period materials, warm near-black isolation, and disciplined pixel
construction without copying an approved pose, costume, prop, or silhouette.
