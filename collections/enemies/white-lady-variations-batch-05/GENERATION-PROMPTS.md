# White Lady Variations — Prompt Set

Generated as five separate assets through the built-in image-generation workflow.
These are original game interpretations inspired by Philippine White Lady ghost
stories, not depictions of one specific reported apparition or local tradition.

## Shared direction

Create one complete full-body feminine ghost for a dark-fantasy 2D Metroidvania.
Use tall adult proportions, a compact head, normal human anatomy, clearly
separated hands and bare feet, and a readable idle silhouette. Center the subject
on a plain warm near-black square background with generous padding.

Render deliberately authored pixel art with crisp square pixels, connected
clusters, restrained surface detail, selective highlights, and roughly 14–18
colors. The palette is pearl white, aged ivory, silver grey, charcoal, cold
blue-grey, and tiny icy-cyan highlights. White and silver dominate.

Do not use green, emerald, teal, lime, or a green color cast. Avoid neon glow,
gore, decay, screaming faces, body horror, extra limbs, duplicated hands, hidden
feet, smooth gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, painterly rendering, scenery, text,
borders, and watermarks.

## Variations

1. **Roadside White Lady** — Quiet three-quarter idle pose, long white mourning
   dress, black hair and dress hem drifting screen-right, one small antique lantern
   containing a cold-white flame.
2. **Balete Bride** — Restrained bridal dress, broad veil flowing screen-left,
   woven sash, one loop of remembrance beads, and exactly three pale root wisps
   beside the feet.
3. **Rain-Road White Lady** — Slow walking idle facing screen-left, closed antique
   parasol used like a walking stick, gathered skirt, streaming hair and shawl,
   sparse cold rain.
4. **Chapel Candle White Lady** — Front-facing symmetrical idle, plain church
   dress and shoulder mantle, exactly one extinguished black candle held in both
   hands, exactly five cold-white spirit flames.
5. **River-Mirror White Lady** — Three-quarter pose facing screen-right, simple
   blouse-and-skirt silhouette, one blank oval hand mirror, silver mist ribbon,
   and one shallow water ripple around both feet.
