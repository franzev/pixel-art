## Interpretation

“Witch hunters” was interpreted as a hostile adult-human enemy family: an
austere Ashen Provinces tribunal equipped to pursue suspected supernatural
threats through conventional martial training and physical equipment. No
visible spells, effects, or folklore-creature anatomy were used.

## Positive Direction

The Processional Crucifer and Palm-Weave Arquebusier from Catholic Knights
Batch 41 were used as visual references only. Their deliberate pixel density,
wearable articulated armor, weathered black/ivory/bronze materials, Philippine
material cues, warm near-black isolation, and clean martial silhouettes were
preserved. Their exact poses, helmets, costumes, weapons, motifs, and
silhouettes were not copied.
