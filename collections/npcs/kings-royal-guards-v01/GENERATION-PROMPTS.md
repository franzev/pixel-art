## Shared direction

Ten separate human royal-guard concepts in two review waves of five. The Sable
Longsword Castellan is a visual-treatment reference only for deliberate pixel
density, connected clusters, restrained values, warm-near-black isolation, and
mature full-body proportions. Do not copy its face, sex, armor, pose, sword,
costume, heraldry, or silhouette.

Every concept is exactly one full-height adult human guard with one head, two
arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet. Each faction must differ through
helmet geometry, armor layout, cloth system, conventional weapon, stance,
silhouette, and implied defensive doctrine. No visible spells, auras, sigils,
orbs, smoke, droplets, threads, particles, scenery, text, animal anatomy,
oversized fantasy weapons, or green background.
