## Direction

Ten human royal guards, each representing a different Ashen Provinces faction.
The factions differ through helmet geometry, armor construction, cloth system,
weapon, stance, silhouette, and guard doctrine rather than palette alone.

The Sable Longsword Castellan is a visual-treatment reference only for crisp
pixel construction, connected clusters, mature full-body proportions,
restrained values, and warm-near-black isolation. Its face, costume, armor,
pose, sword, and silhouette must not be copied.
