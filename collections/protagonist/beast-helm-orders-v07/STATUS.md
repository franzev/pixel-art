# Beast-Helm Orders v07 — Status

## Goal

Create ten original animal-helm knights. Each helmet, armor geometry, palette,
cloth, and weapon role must express a different animal while remaining a
coherent armored character rather than a mascot costume.

## Concepts

1. Stag-Crown Lancer
2. Bear-Mantle Axeguard
3. Grey-Wolf Swordwarden
4. Iron-Boar Spear Knight
5. Pale-Owl Watcher
6. Black-Raven Cavalier
7. Red-Fox Scout Knight
8. Oxblood Bull Gatekeeper
9. Jade-Cobra Sentinel
10. Moon-Hare Duelist

## Review

- **Helmet readability:** all ten animals remain identifiable from the helmet
  silhouette alone: stag, bear, wolf, boar, owl, raven, fox, bull, cobra, and
  hare.
- **Matched roles and palettes:** forest stag lancer, brown bear axeguard,
  blue-grey wolf swordwarden, burgundy boar spear knight, ivory owl watcher,
  black-violet raven cavalier, russet fox scout, oxblood bull gatekeeper, jade
  cobra sentinel, and pale moon-blue hare duelist.
- **Armor variety:** fitted plate, heavy plate, layered watch armor, cavalry
  armor, lighter scout harness, lamellar, and ceremonial dueling plate appear
  across distinct weapon and shield configurations.
- **Character integrity:** every design remains a human knight with animal
  geometry translated into metalwork; none use mascot, furry-humanoid, or
  taxidermy treatment.
- **Originality and retention:** generated from text-only original briefs with
  no visual game references; all ten generated sources are retained.
