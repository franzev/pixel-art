# Beast-Helm Orders v08 — Status

## Goal

Create twenty new original animal-faction armored knights in four review waves
of five. Every design remains a fully human knight whose animal identity is
translated into forged helmet geometry, articulated armor, cloth, heraldry,
stance, and doctrine rather than biological animal anatomy, mascot styling, or
taxidermy.

## Active direction

- Use Beast-Helm Orders v07 only as the closest collection-level direction:
  crisp high-detail pixel art, readable animal helmets, wearable armor, restrained
  dark palettes, and one complete full-height knight per square render.
- Do not copy a v07 pose, helmet, armor layout, weapon silhouette, or palette.
- The helmet alone should identify the animal at 256 pixels, while every animal
  feature remains forged armor or heraldic construction around a human head.
- Each concept must differ through helmet profile, armor mass, cloth system,
  weapon role, stance, and negative space—not color alone.
- Keep all work as drafts until explicit user approval.
