## Goal

Create twenty high-quality original demon knights with strong variation in
helmet silhouette, armor construction, body profile, palette, weapon, and rank.
Infernal identity may be overt, but designs must not reproduce recognizable game
characters.

## Review

- **Palette range:** ember black/red, oil green, ash white, violet, marsh
  bronze, vermilion, prison brown, bone white, brass/soot, velvet red-black,
  old brass, salt white, green-black, funeral charcoal, obsidian, vermilion
  lacquer, coal black, ivory/crimson, dusk purple, and black-gold.
- **Silhouette range:** horned vassal, insect-crowned exactor, broad butcher,
  slim duelist, dragoon, crested lancer, gaoler, blind oracle, heavy templar,
  cavalier, legionary, wraith, idol guard, bell-helmed reaper, apostate,
  gatekeeper, furnace knight, lacquer duelist, wing-finned standarder, and
  asymmetric-horn captain.
- **Body profiles:** predominantly lean or medium human scale, with deliberate
  heavier profiles reserved for the butcher, gatekeeper, and furnace roles.
- **Originality:** generated from text-only original briefs with no visual game
  references and no deliberate reproduction of recognizable armor.
- **Retention:** all twenty generated sources are retained, including any
  designs that may later be rejected during selection.
