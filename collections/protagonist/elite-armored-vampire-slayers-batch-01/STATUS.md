## Interpretation

The terse brief is interpreted as five distinct mature adult human vampire
slayers in elite, wearable, Philippine-inflected gothic armor. They are
protagonist-class alternatives rather than vampire enemies. Vampire-hunting
identity is conveyed through protected throats, clear vision, disciplined
conventional weapons, matte capiz inspection details, abaca and palm-fiber
weather gear, and restrained sun-disc hardware. No visible spell or spell-like
effect is permitted.

- All ten renders are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG files.
- Every image contains exactly one complete mature adult human with one head,

- Heads, hands, feet, armor, cloth, and equipment remain fully inside the frame
  with safe padding.
- The ten silhouettes stay distinct at 256 pixels. Wave 02 adds a high

  overhead curved saber, and low compact axe without duplicating Wave 01.
- Focused equipment crops confirm visible hand contacts, straight sword and
  firearm axes, aligned mace head and shaft, readable matchlock lock clearance,
  a supported crossbow with one seated short bolt, separate two-handed grip
  contacts, a compact opposed hammer-and-beak head, an uninterrupted
  single-hand rapier blade, a coherent curved saber, and a visible axe collar.
- The five women have visibly attractive, unmistakably adult, structurally
  different faces. Same-scale portrait comparison shows differences in face
  length, jaw, chin, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, cheeks, hairline, and hairstyle.
- Draft 07 required one focused weapon correction after its first head read as

- Armor is wearable and articulated with clear sight, breathing, neck
