## Goal

burial damage, empty armor, and haunted textiles rather than simple blue
recoloring. Maintain grounded armor construction and distinct silhouettes.

## Review

- **Faction read:** spectral identity comes from empty visors, missing interior
  sections, burial cloth, corrosion, ash, frost dust, and fading edges rather
  than a uniform cyan recolor.
- **Silhouettes:** sallet wanderer, drowned bell knight, wick-crown watcher,
  gale lancer, mourning-mask duelist, chain warden, square frost templar,
  interrupted banner knight, pilgrim guard, and ring-crowned marshal.
- **Palette range:** pewter fog-grey, sea verdigris, soot white, storm blue-grey,
  silver charcoal, burial brown, pale steel-lilac, faded ochre, smoke and bone,
  and muted violet-antique silver.
- **Proportions:** predominantly lean or medium; the frost templar is the only
  intentionally heavier role and remains realistically human-scaled.
- **Originality and retention:** text-only original briefs with no visual game
  references; all ten generated sources are retained.
