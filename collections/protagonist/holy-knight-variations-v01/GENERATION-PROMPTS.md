# Holy Knight Variations v01

> **Current facing override (2026-08-01):** Any `right-facing` direction
> in this file means a mostly frontal, shallow front-three-quarter view.
> Keep the camera-facing facial plane visible; when unobscured, show both
> eyes, nose, mouth, chin, and expression. The gaze, leading action,
> locomotion, and equipment may favor screen-right, but never turn the face
> away into a rear-three-quarter, back-of-head, ear-only, far-cheek, or
> complete side-profile view unless explicitly requested. This block
> overrides older pose or facing wording below.

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per distinct character
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`
**Status:** Prompts prepared for generation

## Input references

- **Image 1 — canonical identity and pixel-treatment reference:**
  `public/art/protagonist/crimson-knight-player-character/drafts/01-crimson-knight-player-character-v01-reference-256.png`
- **Image 2 — supporting armor, cape, sword, and proportion reference:**
  `samples/silver-knight/silver-knight-left-facing-source.png`

Image 1 controls the recognizable player-character identity, chunky pixel
language, pose, facing direction, and warm-charcoal presentation. Image 2 is
supporting guidance only. Every output is a new generation, not an edit.

## Shared invariants

Every prompt preserves:

- one full-body holy knight, alone and centered
- tall 7–7.5-head proportions and long legs
- compact rounded closed sallet or armet
- upright three-quarter stance facing screen-left
- one long straight one-handed sword held low toward screen-left
- one empty off-hand visibly separated from the hip
- a long heavy cape trailing screen-right to the lower calf
- the same overall animation footprint as the Crimson Knight
- deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art, authored as though on a
  native 96 × 96 indexed-palette grid
- large connected square-pixel clusters, broad armor planes, restrained
  one-pixel highlights, approximately 12–16 meaningful colors
- a uniform warm charcoal `#171311` background

Every prompt avoids green, scenery, floor planes, cast shadows, text, logos,
watermarks, extra characters, shields, extra weapons, oversized halos, wings,
crowns, horns, plumes, glowing eyes, generic crusader crosses, copied
game-character details, anti-aliasing, blur, gradients, painterly rendering,
microtexture, excessive rivets, chain-link noise, and isolated noise pixels.
