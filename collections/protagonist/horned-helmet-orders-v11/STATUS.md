## Goal

Create five original knights whose helmets use clearly different horn
constructions while remaining wearable, human-proportioned, and coherent with
the rest of the armor. Horns must define silhouette without becoming oversized
fantasy clutter.

## Review

- **Horn readability:** compact ram coils, long rear-swept ibex ridges, short
  lateral bull horns, slim upright antelope spirals, and an asymmetric broken
  pair remain immediately distinct at 256 × 256.
- **Mounting and wearability:** every horn connects through reinforced helmet
  plates; shells remain skull-sized with readable sight, breaths, and neck
  clearance.
- **Armor coherence:** coil curves repeat in judicial shield edges, rearward
  ridges in lancer fluting, lateral mass in the gatekeeper's rectangular
  defense, vertical spirals in the duelist's narrow harness, and asymmetry in
  the apostate's cape and shoulders.
- **Proportions:** four knights are lean or medium. The bull gatekeeper is the
  only sturdy role and remains human-scale with visible waist and normal
  shoulders.
- **Role and palette diversity:** polehammer justiciar, unshielded lancer,
  mace-and-door-shield guard, estoc duelist, and sword apostate use separate
  equipment and material families.
- **Hard gates:** no oversized horns, giant pauldrons, biological horn growth,
  mascot heads, exposed monsters, glow, tank bodies, or recognizable copied
  armor.
- **Retention:** all five full generated sources are retained.
