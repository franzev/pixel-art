# Sacred Tribunal Canonesses v13 — Status

## Goal

Create five original adult female holy inquisitors that extend the approved
Sacred Tribunal v12 faction. Armor must be practical and non-sexualized while
still fitting realistically proportioned female bodies. Each design needs a new
helmet mechanism, role, armor system, cloth silhouette, and weapon profile.

## Review

- **Adult female representation:** all five use realistically proportioned
  female bodies beneath practical full-coverage armor, with subtly narrower
  shoulders and properly articulated waists and hips.
- **Non-sexualized construction:** no sculpted breast cups, cleavage, corseted
  waists, exposed thighs, heels, pin-up poses, or decorative vulnerability.
- **Faction continuity:** pale cloth, dark or aged metal, judicial equipment,
  fully controlled palettes, compact helmets, and disciplined posture place
  the canonesses clearly beside Sacred Tribunal v12.
- **New helmet systems:** convex mirror mask, interlocking diagonal plates, six
  punched windows, folded cheek-and-throat lames, and vertical command ribs do
  not repeat the five existing tribunal helmets.
- **Silhouette and role diversity:** longsword abbess, poleaxe prosecutor,
  mace-and-pavise auditor, staff-spear questioner, and shieldless two-handed
  canoness remain distinct at 256 × 256.
- **Wearability:** skull-sized helmets, readable neck clearance, restrained
  shoulders, visible waists, articulated joints, stable feet, and practical
  weapon grips pass the project rules.
- **Hard gates:** no bulk, generic gender-swapped copies, giant religious
  headgear, excessive gold, glow, smooth rendering, or recognizable copied
  armor.
- **Retention:** all five full generated sources are retained.
