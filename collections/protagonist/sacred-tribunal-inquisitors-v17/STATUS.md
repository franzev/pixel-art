# Sacred Tribunal Inquisitors v17 — Status

## Goal

Create five original adult male inquisitors that extend the Sacred Tribunal
faction through five conventional weapon profiles: greatsword, zweihander,
longsword, flail, and morning star. Each concept must remain mechanically
wearable, distinctly male without exaggerated bulk, readable at 256 pixels,
and visually separate through helmet, armor, cloth, stance, and weapon reach.

## Quality Gate

- Exactly one complete adult male humanoid and one weapon per render.
- Greatsword, zweihander, and longsword have straight aligned
  blade–guard–grip construction.
- Greatsword and zweihander use both hands on the handle.
- Flail has one straight haft, one short chain, and one attached striking head.
- Morning star has one compact spiked head on a short straight shaft and no
  chain.
- All five have stable feet, readable joints, plausible wearable armor, and
  clear weapon load paths.
- All five remain readable at 256 pixels.
- No scenery, green background, extra props, arrows, visible spells, glow,
  smoke, droplets, wisps, or ambient particles.

## Lifecycle

All five renders are **Drafts**. None are canonical, retained, approved, or
production-ready until the user explicitly approves them.
