# Sacred Tribunal v12 — Status

## Goal

Create five original holy knights with a severe inquisitorial identity. Each
must use a different compact ecclesiastical helmet mechanism, judicial role,
armor system, cloth silhouette, and weapon profile without becoming a generic
white-and-gold paladin or an implausibly bulky fantasy priest.

2. Split-Mitre Judicator

## Review

- **Faction cohesion:** pale liturgical cloth, dark or aged metal, disciplined
  posture, judicial equipment, restrained seals, and fully concealed faces
  establish one sacred tribunal without making the armor identical.
- **Helmet diversity:** triple lancet openings, compact split-mitre ridges,
  chapel hat with mail veil, stacked locking bars, and a pierced conical
  penitent crown remain separate constructions at 256 × 256.
- **Helmet-to-body coherence:** vertical lancet lines, paired mitre divisions,
  layered veil and coat, horizontal lock bands, and narrow pierced robe rhythm
  each continue through the matching body and equipment.
- **Proportions and wearability:** all five use lean or human-medium bodies,
  skull-sized helmets, visible waists, restrained shoulders, readable joints,
  practical weapons, and stable feet.
- **Silhouette and role range:** estoc examiner, polehammer judge, staff-spear
  field questioner, mace-and-pavise confessor, and two-handed penitent remain
  distinguishable without color.
- **Hard gates:** no generic shining paladins, giant crosses, oversized mitres
  or brims, excessive gold, glow, tank bodies, smooth rendering, or recognizable
  copied armor.
- **Retention:** all five full generated sources are retained.
