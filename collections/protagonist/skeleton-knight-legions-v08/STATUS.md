## Goal

well-fitted armor systems. Each must be immediately skeletal without becoming
an identical skull wearing a different color.

## Review

- **Faction read:** every character has clearly readable skeletal anatomy while
  varying which bones remain exposed and how the helmet frames the skull.
- **Armor eras:** segmented legionary plate, copper scale, fitted black plate,
  red-cord lamellar, salt-white crusader plate, moss brigandine, funeral brass,
  dueling harness, heat-darkened field armor, and command plate.
- **Silhouettes and roles:** spear legionary, crypt spearman, sword-and-shield
  ossuary knight, glaive retainer, mace crusader, hooked-axe reaver,
  polehammer bellguard, estoc duelist, two-handed axeman, and crowned marshal.
- **Proportions and anatomy:** thin human-scale skeletal frames, coherent skull
  and limb placement, stable stances, natural weapon grips, and no oversized
  armor or comedy treatment.
- **Originality and retention:** text-only original briefs with no visual game
  references; all ten generated sources are retained.
