# Spectral Foot Soldiers v09 — Status

## Goal

Create five original ghost foot soldiers whose spectral bodies match the
attached washerwoman reference: visible blue human forms, mournful faces,
layered translucent shading, bright cyan edge light, and cloth-like trailing
wisps. Each soldier must use a different infantry role and silhouette.

`archive/legacy-art/protagonist/spectral-foot-soldiers-v09/references/possessed-washerwoman-style-reference.png`

edit target and its washerwoman composition should not be reproduced.

## Review

- **Reference likeness:** all five use visible deceased human faces, layered
  blue spectral bodies, dark navy interior folds, restrained cyan edge light,
  and long hair or cloth strands that dissolve into wisps.
- **Composition:** each image contains one ghost soldier only. None reproduces
  the living host, washerwoman, laundry sheet, or second apparition from the
  reference.
- **Role diversity:** spear levy, sword-and-shield militia, town-watch
  halberdier, compact crossbow scout, and tall standard bearer.
- **Silhouette diversity:** vertical spear, round shield mass, long halberd,
  horizontal crossbow, and banner column remain distinguishable at reference
  size.
- **Proportions and equipment:** lean or ordinary human builds, light infantry
  clothing and armor, readable hands, practical weapon scale, and stable poses.
- **Retention:** all five full generated sources and the copied style reference
  are retained in the collection.
