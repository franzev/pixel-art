# Vampire Knight Courts v08 — Status

## Goal

Create ten original vampire knights with aristocratic menace, grounded armor,
distinct helmet and cape silhouettes, varied combat roles, and no repeated
base design. Keep proportions lean or human-medium rather than bulky.

## Review

- **Faction read:** all ten feel aristocratic and predatory through tailored
  armor, severe closed helmets, heraldic shields, and controlled cape shapes;
  none rely on exposed monsters, glowing eyes, or biological wings.
- **Silhouettes:** inverted chalice, swept dragoon fins, rose-petal armet,
  veil-crested lancer, flat-topped castellane helm, segmented leech jaw,
  coffin visor, abstract hound muzzle, pale fang points, and broken sun ring.
- **Armor and roles:** court plate, cavalry harness, dueling armor, lancer
  plate, castle guard armor, coat of plates, ceremonial paladin plate,
  inquisitorial armor, and command harness span distinct weapons and shields.
- **Proportions:** lean and human-medium throughout, with no oversized pauldrons
  or bulky heroic bodies.
- **Originality and retention:** text-only original briefs with no visual game
  references; all ten generated sources are retained.
