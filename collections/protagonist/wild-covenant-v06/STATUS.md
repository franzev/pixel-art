# Wild Covenant v06 — Status

## Direction

Ten original neutral knights and wardens shaped by local landscapes. Nature is
expressed through dyes, fibers, weathering, heraldry, and practical regional
equipment rather than implausible organic armor.

## Review

- Landscape identities are carried by practical materials and regional
  equipment: moss dye, woven reed, grey wool, pale shield strips, salt wear,
  orchard ochre, feather texture, root-like straps, and pale moth stitching.
- Armor systems span fitted plate, brigandine, padded jack, coat of plates,
  lamellar and mail, cavalry half-armor, workman gear, and light watcher plate.
- Helmet profiles include sallet, leaf-vent close helm, kettle hat, pointed
  bascinet, segmented nasal helm, burgonet, open sallet, crested burgonet,
  skullcap and coif, and low watcher sallet.
- All builds are lean or medium, with practical tools and human-scale weapons.
- All ten were generated from original text briefs without visual game
  references. Every generated source has been retained.
