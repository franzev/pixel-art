# Animal-Spirit Predators Batch 21 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-28
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design
**Status:** Draft-only

## Shared direction

Create original supernatural fauna rather than documented traditional beings.
Use crisp pixel art, complete screen-left silhouettes, a uniform warm
near-black background, charcoal scales or hide, mud brown, weathered bamboo,
abaca, dirty bone, slate blue, ice blue, silver, amber, and muted brass. Avoid
green, scenery, humanoid anatomy, gore, text, logos, and soft rendering.

## Roster

1. **Crocodile River Warden** — One crocodile head with two eyes, four legs and
   three-clawed webbed feet, one tail, one woven bamboo fish-trap shell, and
   three pale river-light bands.
2. **Moon-Horned Carabao** — One bovine head with two eyes and two crescent
   horns, four legs and cloven hooves, one tail, one loose wooden yoke, and
   three silver water ribbons.
3. **Lantern-Eyed Python** — One python head with two amber eyes and one closed
   mouth, one limbless body arranged in three coils, one tail, one abaca collar,
   and exactly three brass bells.
