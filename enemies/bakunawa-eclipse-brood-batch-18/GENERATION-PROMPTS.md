# Bakunawa Eclipse Brood Batch 18 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-28
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design
**Status:** Draft-only

## Shared direction

Create original lesser creatures inspired by Philippine eclipse and moon-eating
serpent traditions. None of these creatures is presented as the Bakunawa
itself or as a definitive cultural portrayal. Use crisp pixel art, complete
screen-left silhouettes, a uniform warm near-black background, soot black,
indigo, tarnished silver, dirty bone, and controlled pale ice-blue moonlight.
Avoid green, scenery, generic Western dragons, humanoid armor, gore, text,
logos, and soft rendering.

## Roster

1. **Eclipse Fang** — One serpent head with two eyes and a crescent jaw guard,
   four short legs with three claws per foot, one continuous body, one tail,
   seven moon-pale spine plates, and no wings.
2. **Moon-Fracture Serpent** — One limbless flying serpent with two eyes, one
   body and tail, exactly two crescent dorsal fins, exactly three cracked moon
   fragments, and no wings.
3. **Crescent-Shell Guardian** — One serpent-turtle head with two eyes, four
   legs and three-clawed feet, one tail, one enormous crescent shell with seven
   crater marks, and no wings.
