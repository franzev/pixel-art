# Balete Forest Court Batch 16 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-28
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design
**Status:** Draft-only

## Shared direction

Create original territorial entities associated with an otherworldly balete
forest court. These are game interpretations inspired broadly by Philippine
beliefs about powerful nature beings; they are not definitive engkanto, diwata,
or duwende portrayals and do not represent living cultural groups. Use crisp
pixel art, screen-left silhouettes, a warm near-black background, bark, abaca,
dry-leaf tan, ivory, bruised plum, and restrained amber. Avoid green, scenery,
human ceremonial insignia, caricature, gore, heroic armor, and soft rendering.

## Roster

1. **Root-Crown Bailiff** — One bark-mask head, two arms and four-fingered
   hands, two reverse-jointed root legs, two split feet, five pale crown prongs,
   one forked staff, and three trailing root ribbons.
2. **Leaf-Veil Duelist** — One masked head, two arms and hands, two root legs
   and feet, exactly two leaf-shaped wooden blades, and one long veil flowing
   screen-right.
3. **Mushroom Lantern Keeper** — One shelf-mushroom-capped head, two arms and
   three-fingered hands, two short root legs, one bamboo lantern with one flame,
   one ring holding exactly three wooden keys, and one dry-leaf mantle.
