# Banshees Batch 33 — Generation Prompts

## Shared direction

- Five separate isolated adult humanoid ghost-enemy concepts.
- Square 1:1 source canvas with one complete character and generous padding.
- Plain warm near-black background with no scenery, floor, cast shadow, text,
  frame, or watermark.
- Deliberate hard-pixel construction, connected clusters, broad value masses,
  restrained ramps, and readability at 256 pixels.
- Mature anatomy: one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Charcoal, dirty ivory, slate, silver, and controlled cold blue; no green
  background and no red-dominant body palette.
- Mournful, occult, non-graphic horror.
- Each design has a different encounter role, posture, silhouette anchor,
  clothing construction, primary tool or effect, and movement implication.

## Folklore research

The prompts use archival Irish folklore only for high-level banshee cues:
keening as a warning or lament associated with death, white clothing, long
flowing hair, and an occasional comb. The results are explicitly framed as
original game interpretations rather than definitive cultural portrayals.
