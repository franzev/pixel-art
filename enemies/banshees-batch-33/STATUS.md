## Interpretation

The request was treated as five separate isolated adult humanoid ghost-enemy
concepts. The designs are original Ashen Provinces game interpretations of the
banshee rather than claims of one definitive cultural portrayal.

The retained White Lady Variations Batch 05 guided mature mourning figures,
cold silver-blue manifestation, long dark hair, readable cloth masses, and
full-body pixel presentation. The Flesh-Veil Oracle guided only the fifth
design's statuesque severity, layered-cloth coherence, and occult restraint.
Exact poses, costumes, props, effects, and silhouettes were not copied.

## Latest user feedback

- Design 05, Hollow-Throat Matriarch, is the positive direction.
- New extensions should preserve its tall veiled, statuesque, layered
  black-and-ivory character treatment.
- Do not add weird spells, floating spell geometry, particles, rings, shards,
  projectiles, or magical objects.
- The newest wave may explore crimson and white, pure white, or other restrained
  surprise palettes.
- Dress trims should be elegant, coherent, and readable rather than noisy.
- Pointed dresses are disliked. Do not use triangular panels, tapered tails,
  spikes, jagged hems, torn points, forked cloth, or long pointed drapes.
- New banshees must not float. Both feet must be visible and planted on the same
  implied baseline in a weight-bearing standing pose.
- Designs 07–11 remain preserved drafts but their pointed and floating garment
  direction is not a positive reference for future extensions.

## Draft roster

1. **Milestone Cryer** — fast screen-left rush telegraphed by three horizontal
   broken sound crescents.
2. **Shawlbound Lamenter** — stationary area-control enemy sustaining four
   vertical lament threads.
3. **Comb-Reclaiming Widow** — narrow silent ambusher with one ivory comb and
   no magical effect.
4. **Wake-Ring Conductor** — broad interruption controller using two broken
   concentric sound rings.
5. **Hollow-Throat Matriarch** — hovering elite artillery enemy compressing a
   keen into one spindle with four acoustic shards.
6. **Pall-Veil Keener** — hovering close-range stagger enemy with the scream
   implied only by pose; no visible magic or external effect.
7. **Crimson-Edged Mourner** — diagonal gliding pursuer in crimson and ivory
   with a narrow dirty-gold scalloped mantle trim.
8. **Pearl Processional** — closed pure-white proximity-pressure silhouette
   with restrained pale-silver scalloped borders.
9. **Crimson-Lace Regent** — broad counter silhouette with black bell sleeves,
   crimson center, ivory panels, and braided trim.
10. **Violet-Hem Elegist** — evasive S-curve drifter in muted violet and ash
    with pearl-silver curved trim.
11. **Gilt-Ash Sentinel** — rigid high-collar guard in ash, aged bronze, and
    soot black with broad stepped gold trim.
12. **Crimson-Hem Wailer** — grounded advancing stance with a blunt crimson
    overcoat and smooth ivory semicircular gown hem.
13. **White-Hem Vigil** — grounded pure-white column gown with large scalloped
    silver trim and one continuous rounded hem.
14. **Black-Bell Caller** — broad grounded counter silhouette with rounded
    sleeves and a smooth bell-shaped black skirt.
15. **Indigo-Wrap Mourner** — grounded sidestep stance in a curved indigo wrap
    gown with rounded overlap and oval hem.
16. **Ash-Gold Court Wailer** — grounded elite guard in a blunt ash coat and
    broad ivory court skirt with stepped gold trim.

  nearest-neighbor resampling.
- Every image contains exactly one complete adult humanoid ghost with one head,
  two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Every defining element is readable and countable: three sound crescents; one
  shawl and four lament threads; one comb; two broken sound rings; and one sound
  spindle with four detached shards.
- Heads, hair, hands, feet, cloth, props, and effects remain fully inside each
  frame with usable padding.
- The five silhouettes remain distinct at 256 pixels: rushing diagonal, low
  wide grounded arch, narrow closed column, broad circular conductor, and tall
  hovering elite.
- Design 06 preserves Design 05's tall veiled family direction while changing
  the arm pose, drape construction, hem, and negative space.
- Design 06 has no external spell, magic geometry, particles, projectile, aura,
  glow object, or floating element.
- Designs 07–11 also contain no external spell, magic geometry, particles,
  projectiles, auras, glow objects, or floating elements.
- Wave 03 silhouettes remain distinct at 256 pixels: shallow gliding diagonal,
  closed white processional column, broad bell-sleeve regent, narrow violet
  S-curve, and rigid high-collar sentinel.
- Wave 03 trim systems remain elegant and readable through scalloped gold,
  pale-silver edging, crimson-and-ivory braid, pearl curved borders, and broad
  stepped gold geometry.
- Designs 12–16 are all grounded with two visible feet making contact on one
  implied baseline. None is hovering, airborne, levitating, or posed on tiptoe.
- Designs 12–16 use only smooth dress boundaries: blunt horizontal,
  semicircular, broad scalloped, bell-shaped, curved wrap, oval, and rounded
  hems. None uses pointed panels, tapered tails, spikes, forked cloth, or jagged
  torn edges.
- Wave 04 silhouettes remain distinct at 256 pixels: narrow crimson overcoat,
  pure-white column gown, broad black bell skirt, curved indigo wrap, and
  structured ash-gold court dress.
- The family stays in charcoal, dirty ivory, slate, silver, and controlled cold
  blue. No green field or red-dominant body palette appears.
- Backgrounds read as plain warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast
  shadow, text, frame, or watermark.
- Pixel treatment remains crisp at 256 pixels with connected clusters, broad
  value masses, restrained ramps, and no soft-focus painterly treatment.
- Horror remains mournful and non-graphic.
