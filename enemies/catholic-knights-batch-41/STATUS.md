## Interpretation

The briefs were treated as ten isolated mature adult human Catholic knights in
one austere devotional family. The first wave established five armored roles;
the second added five women without sexualized armor or palette-only variants.
Catholic identity is carried by processional, censer, scapular, capiz, votive,
and reliquary ideas rather than generic cathedral scenery. Abaca, capiz, palm
fiber, and tropical weathering connect the family to the Ashen Provinces.

## Positive Direction

The Holy Knight Realism Pass v05 was used as the closest accepted visual
direction for adult proportion, wearable armor, helmet scale, articulation,
weapon handling, restrained pixel density, and warm near-black isolation. It
was a visual reference, not an edit target. Its exact pose, helmet, cape,
weapon, costume, and silhouette were not copied.

For the female wave, the user-liked Warrior Nuns Batch 34 supplied the positive
direction for statuesque adult femininity and readable historical weapons. The
actual generation calls used the Processional Crucifer as a family reference
and the Crown of Thorns Briar-Point Justiciar v02 as a female-armor reference.
Both were visual references only, not edit targets, and exposed-skin markings,
sexualized construction, crowns, exact poses, faces, costumes, weapons, and
silhouettes were explicitly excluded.

## Draft Roster

1. **Processional Crucifer** — upright formation-control anchor using one iron
   processional cross-staff.
2. **Censer Penitent** — open circular area-denial fighter using one chained
   thurible and a restrained smoke trail.
3. **Capiz-Mantle Warder** — compact defensive stagger fighter using one short
   mace and a shieldless layered capiz shoulder mantle.
4. **Black-Scapular Auditor** — narrow approach-punishing duelist using one
   estoc and an empty command hand.
5. **Sunburst Relic Lancer** — broad low reach-control knight using one
   two-handed relic spear.
6. **Votive-Lamp Sabreur** — female patrol duelist using one curved sabre and
   one capiz votive lantern.
7. **Palm-Weave Arquebusier** — female horizontal overwatch knight using one
   early matchlock arquebus.
8. **Ivory-Arc Votary** — female single-shot lane archer using one longbow and
   one nocked arrow.
9. **Ash-Hatchet Penitent** — female forward-driving pursuit knight using one
   bearded hand axe and an empty balance hand.
10. **Veiled Relic-Pick Canoness** — female armor-breaking elite using one
    continuous two-handed war pick.

## Quality-Control Review

- All ten sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG files.
- All ten references are 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor RGB PNG files.
- Each review sheet is 1280 × 256 and presents one five-character wave in
  roster order.
- Every image contains exactly one complete adult human with one head, two arms,
  two hands, two legs, and two separated feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, cloth, smoke, and equipment remain within the frame with
  safe padding.
- Repeated equipment counts are readable across both waves: one cross-staff;
  one chained thurible; one mace; one estoc; one spear; one sabre and one
  lantern; one arquebus; one longbow and one nocked arrow; one hand axe; and one
  war pick.
- Armor remains human-proportioned with readable sight lines, breathing paths,
  neck clearance, joint articulation, and plausible grips.
- Backgrounds are warm near-black without scenery, floor planes, cast shadows,
  text, frames, logos, watermarks, or green fields.
- Crisp connected pixel clusters and broad values remain readable at 256.
- Silhouettes remain distinct at review scale: upright vertical staff, open
  chain arc, broad capiz mantle, narrow low estoc, wide spear diagonal,
  lantern-and-sabre asymmetry, horizontal firearm, tall bow arc, compact
  forward axe, and high-diagonal war pick.
- All ten use a consistent screen-left presentation.
- The female wave uses mature believable proportions, intact unmarked skin,
  functional armor coverage, continuous weapons, and plausible hand placement.
- No copied copyrighted character, long crimson cape, cone helmet, horns,
  animal anatomy, graphic gore, implausibly massive armor, or red-dominant
  palette was accepted.

## Internally Rejected Attempt

The first concept-03 attempt used a large capiz reliquary shield. The returned
image hid the requested interior grip and forearm strap, so it failed the
project's shield-mechanics rule and was not copied into the collection. It was
replaced by the shieldless Capiz-Mantle Warder.
