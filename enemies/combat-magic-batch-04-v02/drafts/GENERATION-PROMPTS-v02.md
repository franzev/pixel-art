# Combat and Magic Pixel-Art Redraw Tests — v02 Prompt Record

Generated separately through the built-in image-generation workflow. Original
Batch 04 sources supplied identity, anatomy, proportions, pose, costume, props,
effect layout, palette relationships, and mood only.

## Shared direction

Rebuild every subject from scratch as an actual native-resolution combat sprite,
not concept art and not a filtered, traced, edited, or downscaled copy of its
original. Work as though each sprite were authored directly on a 96 × 96 canvas
and enlarged through integer nearest-neighbor scaling.

Use visibly coarse, consistent square logical pixels; large connected clusters;
broad clothing, anatomy, weapon, and effect shapes; and clean negative space.
Retain approximately 16–20 meaningful colors with shared ramps and only two or
three shades per material. Preserve the original controlled combat accent
without allowing it to recolor the base character.

Use the established warm near-black background. Avoid green backgrounds,
gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, painterly shading, microtexture, excessive
dithering, isolated noise, palette expansion, altered anatomy, altered props,
text, labels, borders, and watermarks.
