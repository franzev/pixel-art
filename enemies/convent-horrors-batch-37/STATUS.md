## Interpretation and Reference Direction

The request was interpreted as twenty separate isolated enemy-character
concepts in four review waves of five. Every draft is one barefoot adult
convent-horror woman. The collection introduces color, vampire pallor and
fangs, restrained ritual marks, controlled blood traces, old burns, charred
cloth, realistic flails and other weapons, rosaries, and thorn crowns without
stacking all of those ideas onto every design.

Designs 21–25 form a dress-color extension requested after the user positively
described the first twenty as good. The extension varies both palette and
garment construction: fitted underdress with woven tapis-like overskirt;
wrap-front tunic with one-shoulder mantle; high-collar coat with pleated
overskirt; bell sleeves with a two-tail scapular; and pleated outer skirt with
short capelet. The new designs are not simple recolors.

`07-ivory-flail-votary-v02-source.png` from Convent Tormentors Batch 30
controlled the moderated mature proportions, long legs, barefoot readability,
convent identity, and isolated presentation. The Flesh-Veil Oracle controlled
the severe occult mood, broad value masses, premium crisp pixel construction,
and 256-pixel readability. The supplied figurine image remained an
anatomy-only reference from the previous collection. No exact reference pose,
face, costume, veil, weapon placement, effect, or silhouette was copied.

## Quality-Control Review

- All twenty-five source images are square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG files on warm
  near-black backgrounds.
- Every source contains exactly one complete adult woman with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two distinct bare feet.
- Hands and grips were inspected at source scale; no obvious extra finger,
  extra hand, extra limb, duplicate weapon, or disconnected weapon path was
  accepted.
- Both bare feet remain visible and readable in all twenty-five 256 × 256
  references.
- Every weapon is human-scale and mechanically recognizable. Long shafts,
  whip paths, flail joints, bow limbs, and weapon tips remain inside the canvas.
- The two flails use visibly different constructions and combat reads.
- Rosaries hang under gravity, have one cross each, and are not transformed
  into magical or hybrid weapons.
- Thorn crowns use natural branch construction and remain secondary to the
  character silhouette.
- Vampire traits are limited to pallor, eyes, subtle fangs, and sparse dark-red
  traces. Red and oxblood remain garment colors rather than repeated red skin.
- Burns and fire wear remain healed or garment-level; no exposed tissue,
  victim tableau, dismemberment, organs, or excessive blood appears.
- Upper-torso proportions remain moderated across the collection rather than
  returning to the rejected exaggerated volume.
- No draft contains scenery, a floor plane, cast shadow, typography, frame,
  green background, shoes, boots, magic clutter, animal anatomy, or a copied
  copyrighted character design.
- Five 1280 × 256 wave review sheets were created from nearest-neighbor
  256-pixel references. The original twenty-character 1280 × 1024 sheet was
  preserved, and a new twenty-five-character 1280 × 1280 v02 sheet was added.
- Designs 21–25 retain clear secondary and tertiary dress-color blocks at
  256 pixels rather than collapsing into single-color silhouettes.
