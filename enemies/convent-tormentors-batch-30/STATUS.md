## Interpretation and Reference Direction

This batch treats the request as five separate adult humanoid enemy-character
concepts in one review wave. Every character is a barefoot nun with one
gameplay-readable cruel weapon; the horror remains non-graphic.

The user described the initial five visible drafts as good, then requested more
with a white dress and body proportions like `/Users/franz/Desktop/images.jpeg`.
Because no extension quantity was given, the project default produced one pilot
as Design 06. The supplied image controlled only tall curvy adult proportions;
its identity, face, ponytail, bodysuit, colors, boots, pose, platform, futuristic
materials, and sword were not copied.

The user then requested five more white-dress concepts with only realistic
weapons, naming flail, mace, and whip as examples. Designs 07–11 form one review
wave using a plain flail, flanged mace, leather whip, compact war hammer, and
single-bit axe. All are human-scale, mechanically simple, non-magical, and free
of hybrid or torture-device additions.

The user then reported that some chests were too large. Designs 07, 09, and 11
were the clearest outliers and received non-destructive v02 edits with moderate
upper-torso proportions. Their weapons, poses, hands, feet, white dresses, and
silhouette logic were preserved. Designs 08 and 10 were left unchanged.

The retained Ash-Veil Processioner controlled the barefoot monastic identity,
mournful restraint, warm near-black isolation, and readable pixel clusters. The
Flesh-Veil Oracle controlled only the confirmed positive qualities of mature
statuesque proportions, severe veil logic, layered soot-dark cloth, pale intact
skin, controlled aged-metal accents, and premium pixel density. No reference
pose, exact veil, costume panel, anatomy, prop placement, spell arrangement, or
silhouette was requested.
