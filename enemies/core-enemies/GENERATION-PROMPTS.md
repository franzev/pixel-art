# Restrained Gothic Enemy Prompt Set

Generated with the built-in image-generation workflow. Each enemy was generated
separately to preserve its silhouette and concept.

## Shared direction

Create one original dark-fantasy pixel-art enemy sprite for a 2D gothic
Metroidvania on a square 1:1 canvas. Show one complete full-body creature centered
on a plain warm near-black background with generous padding, no scenery, text, or
border. Use crisp deliberate pixel clusters, a readable in-game silhouette, and a
restrained palette of corpse-pale skin, charcoal, faded brown, dirty grey, and
small controlled warm or spectral-white highlights. No green.

Keep the mood eerie and melancholic but not grotesque: no exposed organs, gore,
flayed skin, screaming faces, body fusion, or graphic wounds. Humanoids have one
head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet, naturally attached and clearly
readable. Use worn peasant, monastic, or funerary clothing. Avoid plate armor,
heroic posture, crimson capes, standard swords, shields, soldier uniforms, extra
limbs, duplicated hands, extra faces, smooth gradients, blur, painterly rendering,
anti-aliasing, watermarks, and text.
