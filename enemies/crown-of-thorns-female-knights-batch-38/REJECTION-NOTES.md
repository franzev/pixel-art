# Crown of Thorns Female Knights Batch 38 — Rejection Notes

Last updated: 2026-07-30

## User Feedback

The user identified two unacceptable construction problems in the visible work:

- One knight appeared to hold a sword by the blade.
- One thorn-crown treatment read as a weird halo.

The user did not identify the affected filenames, so do not invent an
asset-specific attribution. Keep the batch draft-only and do not use it as a
positive reference as a whole.

## Hard Corrections for Future Work

- Hands may touch only a weapon's intended grip, hilt, haft, stock, fore-end, or
  handle.
- No finger, glove, wrist, or empty hand may touch a blade, edge, point, ricasso,
  bowstring, muzzle, striking head, or other unsafe weapon surface.
- Two-handed swords require both hands visibly below the crossguard on the
  wrapped hilt.
- Pole weapons require continuous aligned shafts and visible load-bearing grips.
- Thorn crowns must be small physical circlets bound directly to a brow, hood,
  coif, cap, or helmet.
- No crown may float, sit behind the head, form a large open ring, or resemble a
  halo, aureole, horns, or antlers.
- Review every result at full resolution and 256 pixels. Reject and regenerate
  any bodily, anatomical, crown, weapon, or grip mistake before saving.

## Additional V01 Feedback

The user later rejected the wave's overall presentation as insufficiently
feminine, described the marshal's sword as giant and strange-looking, described
the halberd as bent, and explicitly prohibited pixel manipulation for the
correction.

Operational interpretation:

- Use softer, conventionally feminine adult faces, long styled hair, graceful
  poses, and elegant practical armor-dress construction.
- Do not infer or assign sexual orientation from visual appearance.
- Halberd and polearm shafts must be visibly straight and continuous.
- Swords must remain normal human scale unless supernatural scale is explicitly
  requested.
- Fix structural generation errors through fresh generation, never through
  retouching, compositing, repainting, resizing, or pixel manipulation.
