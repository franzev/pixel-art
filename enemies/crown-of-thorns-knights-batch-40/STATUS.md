Last updated: 2026-07-30

Ten new mixed-gender Crown-of-Thorns knight concepts are saved as unapproved
drafts in two review waves of five. The roster contains exactly five men and
five women.

Every source passed full-resolution and 256-pixel review for anatomy, complete

## User Corrections Applied

- Never place a hand or finger on a blade, edge, point, ricasso, striking head,
  muzzle, bowstring, or other unsafe weapon surface.
- Two-handed swords place both hands below the crossguard on the wrapped hilt.
- Polearms place both hands on one continuous aligned wooden shaft.
- One-handed weapons use one clearly readable grip; the empty hand stays away.
- Thorn crowns are small physical circlets touching the brow, hood, cap, or
  helmet.
- No floating rings, rear-of-head loops, halos, aureoles, horns, or antlers.
- Review full size and at 256 pixels; reject and regenerate any structural error.

## Quality-Control Summary

- Every image contains exactly one complete mature adult human.
- Every figure has one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Each crown is a single restrained thorn circlet in direct contact with the
  head covering or helmet.
- No crown forms a halo, aureole, floating ring, or rear loop.
- Falchion, mace, rondel, and pick hands remain on their wrapped grips.
- Lucerne hammer, spear, and axe hands remain on continuous wooden hafts.
- Longsword hands are both below the crossguard on the wrapped hilt.
- Matchlock hands are on the stock grip and wooden fore-end, not the barrel or
  muzzle.
- The gauntlet fighter has exactly two normal hands and no weapon.
- No source contains extra weapons, shields, magic, scenery, floor planes, cast
  shadows, text, frames, green backgrounds, tattoos, or graphic gore.
- All sources are 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG files.
- All website references are 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor RGB PNG files.
- Both review sheets are 1280 × 256.
