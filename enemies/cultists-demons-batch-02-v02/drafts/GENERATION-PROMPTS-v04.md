# Cultist Pixel-Art Redraw Tests — v04 Prompt Record

Generated separately through the built-in image-generation workflow. The
original batch sources were used only as identity, anatomy, proportion, pose,
costume, prop-layout, palette-relationship, and mood references.

## Shared direction

Rebuild each subject from scratch as an actual native-resolution game sprite,
not concept art and not an edited, filtered, traced, or downscaled copy of the
original. Work as though the sprite were authored directly on a 96 × 96 canvas
and enlarged with integer nearest-neighbor scaling. Use visibly coarse,
consistent square logical pixels, large connected clusters, flat colors, broad
cloth folds, solid prop shapes, and clean negative space.

Preserve the original silhouette, anatomy, pose, costume, props, proportions,
dominant colors, accent relationships, and mood. Use approximately 16 shared
flat colors, two or three shades per material, and a uniform warm near-black
background. Avoid gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, painterly shading,
microtexture, dithering, fragmented clusters, isolated noise pixels, palette
expansion, bright replacement colors, green, text, borders, and watermarks.

## 01 — Lantern-Faced Novice

Preserve the compact stocky body, oversized square wooden lantern-box head,
thick dark frame and fasteners, centered aged-brass shutter, single thin amber
light slit without a projected beam, charcoal hood, ragged shoulder capelet,
patched robe, broad sleeves, two clasped hands, belt ties/key, wrapped short
legs, brown shoes, and original front-facing slight three-quarter stance.

## 06 — Starless Astrologer

Preserve the tall symmetric hooded scholar, narrow aged-brass mask with one
black facial void, charcoal robe, pale marked stole, side-held control handles,
wrapped shins, and the exact astronomical apparatus: one outer wooden ring,
crossed braces, one horizontal front ring, and exactly three black brass-rimmed
eclipse discs arranged top-center, lower-left, and lower-right. Preserve the
hanging cords and small brass weight. Do not add a staff or replacement orbs.

## 10 — Blind Standard-Bearer

Preserve the tall lean three-quarter stance, pale lower face, layered taupe-grey
blindfold and left-streaming tails, charcoal and dusty-beige robes, short torn
mantle, wrapped limbs, sash, hanging brass ritual weight, split skirt, and both
hands gripping one tall crooked wooden pole. Preserve the exact empty square
iron frame, corner finials, torn cords, and connected ash-grey eye/spiral smoke
sigil. The frame is not a cloth flag and the sigil is not purple or luminous.
