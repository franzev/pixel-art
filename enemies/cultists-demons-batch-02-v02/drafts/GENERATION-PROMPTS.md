## Reference construction

The generated 1254 × 1254 source PNGs are preserved unchanged.

For each review reference:

1. Point-sample the source onto a virtual 96 × 96 grid.
2. Flatten the connected background to `#171311`.
3. Consolidate colors against a hand-defined material palette with dithering
   disabled.
4. Merge only detached 1–3-cell artifacts and tiny interior shade specks.
5. Fit the cleaned sprite within the 96 × 96 grid for safe padding.
6. Enlarge to exactly 256 × 256 with nearest-neighbor scaling.

order:
