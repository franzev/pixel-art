## Direction

Five separate isolated adult humanoid demonic astrologers were generated with the
Flesh-Veil Oracle as a visual reference rather than an edit target. The batch
preserves its mature proportions, severe occult mood, soot-dark cloth, pale
intact skin, worn bronze/copper, and controlled supernatural light without
copying its pose, costume, hands, horned hood, orb column, or silhouette.

The active demon diversity lock was followed: no animal anatomy, imps, bulky
brutes, or red-dominant bodies. All five assets use cool or neutral palettes and
distinct encounter roles, postures, clothing systems, props, and spell geometry.

## Rejected passes

The first two Eclipse-Ring Surveyor attempts were rejected before project save:

1. Attempt 1 introduced a third hand at the instrument's center.
2. Attempt 2 hid one arm into the hub and added a detached gripping hand.

The accepted third attempt made the celestial ring independent and kept the
character's exactly two hands visibly clasped at the chest.
