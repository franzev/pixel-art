## Active faction direction

The v02 faction is the **Order of the Eclipsed Maw**.

Shared visual locks:

- Hinged dragon-jaw visor with copper fang plates.
- Exactly two long swept-back black horn crests.
- Blackened bronze scale-shaped plate rows.
- Copper folded-wing rib pauldrons.
- Deep eclipse-indigo cloth.
- Exactly three pointed tail-shaped tassets.
- An ivory crescent clenched between two copper fangs as the faction badge.
- Controlled ember-orange magic.
