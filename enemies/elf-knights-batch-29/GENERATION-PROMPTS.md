## Interpretation and visual references

Create five separate adult humanoid elf-knight enemy concepts for the Ashen
Provinces. Elf identity comes from mature elongated features, exactly two
naturally attached pointed ears, disciplined equipment, and an ash-dark
moon-indigo family palette—not animal anatomy, bright green woodland styling,
or generic gold high-elf regalia.

These two retained Wild Covenant sources were supplied to the image generator
as visual references:

- `protagonist/wild-covenant-v06/drafts/01-mosscloak-marcher-source.png`
- `protagonist/wild-covenant-v06/drafts/02-grove-gate-warden-source.png`

They control only mature proportions, deliberate pixel construction, wearable
armor, restrained materials, and isolated presentation. Their exact poses,
helmets, armor layouts, cloaks, shields, spears, equipment, heraldry, palettes,
and silhouettes must not be copied.

The existing `forest-knights-batch-26` collection was inspected to avoid
repeating its sling, capture net, field horn, lantern staff, and pursuit axe
identities.
