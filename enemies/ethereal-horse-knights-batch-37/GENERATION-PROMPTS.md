## Review gate

Inspect every generation at full resolution and at 256 × 256. Reject malformed
human or horse anatomy, incorrect counts, missing padding, non-pixel rendering,
green backgrounds, scenery, ambiguous equipment, generic blue recolors, or
duplicate silhouettes. Save viable work only in the batch's `drafts/` paths and
keep every asset draft-only until the user explicitly approves it.
