## Quality-control review

- All five sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs on plain warm
  near-black backgrounds.
- Every asset contains one adult rider and one adult horse.
- Rider anatomy reads as one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet;
  far-side legs and feet are naturally partly occluded by the saddle and horse.
- Horse anatomy reads as one head, two ears, four coherent legs, four hooves,
  and one tail.
- Defining equipment is countable: one lance; one sabre; one chain and one
  censer; one bow and one nocked arrow; one standard with one capiz disc and one
  three-tailed banner.
- Horse muzzles, ears, legs, hooves, tails, riders, effects, and equipment remain
  inside the square with usable padding.
- Spectral identity comes from absent interiors, connected broken clusters,
  translucent bands, dissolution, and restrained cold edge accents rather than
  a uniform neon-blue recolor.
- The five silhouettes remain distinct at 256 × 256: low horizontal charge,
  high sabre diagonal, compact low censer patrol, open rearing bow triangle, and
  tall vertical standard.
- Five 256 × 256 references were created with nearest-neighbor resizing.
- The review sheet is 1280 × 256 and contains the five references in roster
  order.
- No scene, floor plane, cast shadow, green background, text, logo, watermark,
  second rider, second horse, skeletal horse, or graphic gore is present.
