## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated adult humanoid enemy knights from a
fictional corrupt Catholic-coded military order in the Ashen Provinces. The
designs use recognizable devotional material language without depicting real
people, a historical order, official insignia, or a living community as
monstrous.

## Reference role

The Pivot-Visor Penitent from Holy Knight Realism Pass v05 supplied the closest
accepted direction for adult proportion, wearable articulated armor, helmet
scale, weapon handling, and 256-pixel readability. The Flesh-Veil Oracle
supplied the strongest confirmed occult direction for soot-dark layering, worn
copper, dirty ivory, restrained supernatural light, and mournful menace. Both
were visual references, not edit targets; no exact pose, costume, prop
placement, symbol, or silhouette was to be copied.

## Draft roster

1. **Ash-Monstrance Pikeman** — tall reach sentinel using one pike with an
   integrated capiz-and-bronze ritual lattice.
2. **Black-Rosary Flail Justiciar** — forward chain controller using one
   continuous prayer-bead flail ending in a cruciform weight.
3. **Capiz-Reliquary Arbalist** — compact ranged overwatch elite using one
   loaded crossbow with an inset capiz reliquary window.
4. **Three-Seal Indulgence Pavise Knight** — defensive enforcer using one
   pavise with three sealed strips and one short mace.
5. **Black-Scapular Headsman** — tall execution elite using one two-handed
   broad sword beneath a two-tail black scapular.

## Quality-control review

- All five sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs.
- Each source contains exactly one complete adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two separated feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, cloth, weapon tips, shield, chain, and effects remain
  inside the frame with usable padding.
- Equipment remains countable: one pike and one integrated lattice; one chain
  and one terminal weight; one crossbow and one loaded bolt; one shield, one
  mace, and three sealed strips; and one two-handed sword.
- Armor remains lean or medium and mechanically wearable, with readable helmet
  sight lines, breathing paths, neck clearance, joint articulation, and
  plausible weapon grips.
- The pavise is shown from its interior with an aligned hand grip and forearm
  strap; the three sealed strips sit on its visible outer edge.
- Backgrounds remain warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast shadow,
  text, border, logo, watermark, or green field.
- Crisp connected pixel clusters and broad value masses remain legible on the
  1280 × 256 review sheet.
- Silhouettes remain distinct at review scale: narrow vertical pike, open chain
  diagonal, wide horizontal crossbow, closed shield brace, and bowed sword
  diagonal.
- No real insignia, real person, copied historical order, animal anatomy,
  graphic gore, red-dominant palette, cone helmet, crimson cape, or copied
  copyrighted silhouette was accepted.
