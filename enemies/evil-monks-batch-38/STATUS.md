## Interpretation and Reference Direction

The request was interpreted as five separate isolated adult-humanoid enemy
concepts in one review wave.

The retained Ash-Veil Processioner controlled only the barefoot monastic
austerity, weathered layered cloth, mournful occult menace, warm near-black
isolation, mature proportions, and crisp connected pixel clusters. Its exact
pose, veil, candle, garment panels, anatomy details, and silhouette were not
copied.

The five concepts differ through encounter role, posture, silhouette anchor,
primary tool or effect, garment construction, and movement implication:

- Low diagonal crook fighter.
- Closed vertical palm-script caster.
- Compact open-hand bolo stalker.
- Wide hunched cord controller.
- High diagonal pestle breaker.

## Quality-Control Review

- All five sources are square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG images on a warm near-black
  background.
- Every image contains exactly one complete adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two visible bare feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, robe hems, weapons, tools, cord, and glyphs remain fully
  framed with usable padding.
- Defining counts remain readable: one crook with one hook; one palm-leaf
  liturgy with exactly three separated glyphs; one bolo and one empty hand; one
  continuous cord with two knotted grips; and one continuous wooden pestle.
- Weapon and tool grips are mechanically coherent at full size and remain
  legible in the 256-pixel references.
- The five silhouettes remain distinct on the 1280 × 256 review sheet.
- No draft contains a second character, extra face, animal anatomy, horn, wing,
  halo, shoe, sandal, scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, text, green background,
  red-dominant body palette, victim, exposed organ, wet gore, or copied
  copyrighted costume.
- Five 256 × 256 references were created with nearest-neighbor resizing.
