## Interpretation

The request was treated as five separate adult humanoid forest/tree knight enemy
concepts with coherent elf anatomy, fair light ivory-white skin, clearly visible
green clothing, wearable weathered armor, and conventional swords only.

The retained Wild Covenant Mosscloak Marcher and Grove-Gate Warden were supplied
as visual references only for mature proportions, deliberate pixel construction,
wearable armor, restrained forest materials, visible green cloth, and isolated
presentation. Their poses, helmets, shields, polearms, equipment, emblems, and
silhouettes were not copied.

## Quality-control review

- All twenty-six sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 PNGs.
- Every figure is one complete fair-skinned adult humanoid elf with one head,
  two naturally attached pointed ears, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two
  separated feet.
- Designs 01–05 use only conventional swords. Designs 06–15 use only grounded,
  human-scale elvish arms: longbow, spear, sabre, paired short swords, longsword,
  recurve bow, pike, estoc, greatsword, and arming sword.
- No shields, fantasy weapon gimmicks, magic, scenery, text, or extra characters
  are present.
- Green garments are clearly visible through distinct mantle, sash, tabard,
  tunic, cloak, coat, and surcoat constructions.
- The twenty-six silhouettes remain distinct at 256 × 256 across six waves.
- Twenty-six 256 × 256 references were created with nearest-neighbor resizing.
- Three 1280 × 256 review sheets contain five references each in roster order.
- The 256 × 256 Wave 04 review sheet contains the single proportion study.
- The 1280 × 768 overview presents all fifteen drafts in a 5 × 3 grid.
- The updated 1024 × 1024 overview presents all sixteen drafts in a 4 × 4 grid.
- The 1280 × 512 female-longsword overview presents Designs 17–26 in two rows.
- The updated 1280 × 1536 overview presents all twenty-six drafts in a 5 × 6
  grid with four empty backing panels.
- Pixel treatment remains crisp and readable with connected clusters, broad
  values, and restrained color ramps.

## Internally rejected attempts

The first versions of Designs 01 and 02 were not saved because their visible
skin was too tan for the user's clarified fair/light/white-skin direction. Each
was corrected through a targeted color edit that preserved the approved armor,
green clothing, sword, pose, anatomy, and silhouette.

An initial dark-skinned attempt for Design 06 was rejected immediately after the
user clarified that all ten new women must be fair-skinned. It was never copied
into the project and was moved from generated assets to recoverable Trash.
