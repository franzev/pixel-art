# Forest Knights Batch 26 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-30  
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design

## Interpretation and visual references

Create five separate adult humanoid forest-knight enemy concepts for the Ashen
Provinces. Forest influence comes from prolonged duty, practical materials,
weathering, equipment, posture, and encounter role—not animal anatomy, living
tree armor, or decorative branches.

These two retained Wild Covenant sources were supplied to the image generator
as visual references:

- `protagonist/wild-covenant-v06/drafts/01-mosscloak-marcher-source.png`
- `protagonist/wild-covenant-v06/drafts/02-grove-gate-warden-source.png`

They control only mature proportions, deliberate pixel construction, wearable
armor, restrained forest materials, and isolated presentation. Their exact
poses, helmets, armor layouts, cloaks, equipment, heraldry, and silhouettes
must not be copied.

The in-progress `treant-knights-batch-25` collection was inspected to avoid its
shield, billhook, arbalest, censer, and lancer concepts.

## Review gate

Inspect every generation at full resolution and at 256 × 256. Reject malformed
anatomy, incorrect counts, missing padding, non-pixel rendering, green
backgrounds, scenery, ambiguous props, or duplicate silhouettes. Save viable
work only in the batch's `drafts/` paths and keep every asset draft-only until
the user explicitly approves it.
