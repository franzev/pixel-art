## Direction

These waves extend the retained Rootfoot Ghoul's gaunt, corpse-pale, rural
character direction into ten distinct rank-and-file encounter roles. The
Rootfoot Ghoul was used as a visual reference only for adult proportions,
intact pale flesh, weathered clothing, mournful menace, crisp pixel clusters,
and gameplay-scale readability. None of the drafts copies its root feet, pose,
clothing, or exact silhouette.

All ten drafts are complete adult humanoids facing screen-left on plain warm
near-black backgrounds. Wave 1 uses soot, charcoal, dirty ivory, ash grey,
weathered brown, and restrained cool accents with non-graphic decay. At the
user's direct request, Wave 2 explicitly adds stylized gore, visible scarlet
blood, and stronger saffron, blue-black, copper, cobalt, violet, and ember
accents. Blood remains localized rather than making every body red.

Both waves avoid animal anatomy, imp proportions, bulky brute builds, armor,
and protagonist signals.

## Validation

- Source files: pass; ten separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs.
- Reference files: pass; ten separate 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor PNGs.
- Review sheets: pass; both five-piece rows are 1280 × 256, and the combined
  overview is 1280 × 512.
- Anatomy: pass on visual inspection; every draft has exactly one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Equipment and effect counts: pass for Wave 1's pike, bolo, shield, spade, and
  net; pass for Wave 2's sickle, five-nailed board with three exposed rib ends,
  single jar, single continuous blood lash, and single torch flame.
- Facing: pass; all ten face screen-left.
- Silhouette and crop: pass; all subjects and equipment are complete with
  visible padding on every side.
- Family cohesion and diversity: pass; shared flesh, clothing, palette, and
  pixel treatment pair with ten distinct posture/equipment/gameplay reads.
- Background: pass; warm near-black borders vary by no more than four RGB
  levels across sampled edges, with no green field, scenery, floor, or cast
  shadow.
- Pixel treatment: pass; hard square pixels, connected clusters, restrained
  ramps, and broad value masses remain readable at 256 pixels.
- Gore and color direction: pass; Wave 2 uses five visibly different wound or
  blood treatments and five distinct non-blood color accents without red skin
  dominating the family.

One first attempt for the Abaca-Net Snarer faced screen-right and was rejected
before saving. The corrected draft was regenerated from scratch.

Wave 2 also rejected:

1. A Nailboarder pass with more than three exposed ribs.
2. A corrected Nailboarder pass whose board had insufficient top padding; it is
   preserved under `drafts/rejected/`.
3. A Torchbearer pass with detached floating sparks.

The final Nailboarder and Torchbearer were regenerated from scratch.
