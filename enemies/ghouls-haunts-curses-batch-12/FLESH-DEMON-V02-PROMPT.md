## Essential subject direction

Every design in this pass must visibly read as a flesh-bodied demon, ghoul, or
corpse-born creature.

Required visual balance:

- approximately 40–65% visibly exposed intact supernatural skin
- approximately 25–50% worn clothing, bindings, shrouds, aprons, or ritual cloth
- no full armor
- no creature whose main body is a cabinet, jar, wheel, board, lantern, cloud,
  mask, robe, or other object
- ghosts and magic may surround a flesh-bodied creature, but may not replace it
- masks and props are secondary details, never the entire identity

The skin may be corpse ivory, ash grey, muted flesh rose, dark bruised mauve,
blood burgundy, or restrained infernal red. The creatures should look
disturbing because of gaunt proportions, long limbs, strained posture, hollow
eyes, strange movement, and supernatural coloration.

Flesh must remain intact. Do not show exposed organs, intestines, flayed skin,
open muscle, missing body pieces, dismemberment, surgery, victims, or wet
photorealistic gore. Small controlled closed scars, dry curse marks, darkened
veins, blood stains, salt crust, ember seams, or pale spectral light are allowed.

## Pixel-art direction

Create genuine direct-to-grid game pixel art, not pixel-filtered concept
painting. Use hard square pixels, clean stepped edges, large connected color
clusters, two or three shades per material, approximately 14–18 meaningful
colors, strong negative space, and selective one-pixel highlights.

Use a square source with one complete centered creature and generous padding.
Use a perfectly plain warm near-black `#171311` background—never green. No
scenery, ground plane, vignette, decorative framing, text, UI, logos, or
watermarks.

Face each creature toward screen-left in a side or three-quarter gameplay pose.
Keep every head, hand, foot, horn, tail, weapon, and cloth end visible.

Implied native scale:

- small crouched creatures: 44–56 visible pixels high
- ordinary humanoid flesh demons: 60–76 visible pixels high
- tall or elite flesh demons: 78–90 visible pixels high

Avoid plate armor, heroic poses, crimson capes, sallet helmets, standard long
swords, shields, duplicated anatomy, oversized heads, smooth gradients,
excessive shading, painterly noise, blur, anti-aliasing, realistic anatomy
studies, and recognizable franchise creatures.
