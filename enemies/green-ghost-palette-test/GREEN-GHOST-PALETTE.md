# Green Ghost Palette Direction

## Working ramp

- Spectral outline: `#06120C`
- Deep ectoplasm: `#0A2A1C`
- Shadow green: `#0D4A2F`
- Mid emerald: `#147A48`
- Bright emerald: `#2FA968`
- Mint glow: `#62D990`
- Core highlight: `#B2F4C5`

The background remains warm near-black. Green is reserved for the ghost body,
weapons, mist, spell trails, and selective environmental light.

## Rendering rules

- Use 14–18 colors per ghost.
- Build transparency with broken pixel clusters and missing interior areas.
- Keep the brightest mint pixels around the face, hands, weapon edge, and magical
  focal point.
- Use darker blue-green around the silhouette and lower mist.
- Avoid smooth opacity gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, and full-body neon fill.
- Spectral weapons share the same green ramp as the ghost.
- Preserve a readable dark core so the ghost does not disappear against bright
  environments.
- Never use a green background.

## Test subjects

1. **Oathbound Spear Ghost** — Armed infantry silhouette testing spectral weapons.
2. **Drowned Net Ghost** — Wide water-and-net silhouette testing moving effects.
3. **Veiled Balete Mourner** — Narrow cloth silhouette testing transparency and
   trailing mist.

These are original ghost designs using a cinematic emerald spectral-army mood,
not reproductions of characters, costumes, or compositions from an existing film.
