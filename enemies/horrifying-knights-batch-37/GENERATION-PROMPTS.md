## 01 — Three-Nail Silence Executioner

```text
Primary request: The Three-Nail Silence Executioner, an elite punish-and-counter duelist who advances in small measured steps and retaliates after the player attacks.
Subject and silhouette: tall lean adult knight, narrow upright silhouette with a slight forward neck angle; right hand holds exactly one human-scale execution estoc low and diagonally forward; empty left hand raised beside but not touching the sealed visor in a rigid listening gesture. No shield and no other weapon.
Helmet/armor/clothing: close-fitting rounded armet with plausible side pivots and a single horizontal sight slit. Exactly three oversized square-headed black iron ritual nails cross the sight slit in a horizontal row: one left, one center, one right. These are the only nail-like or rivet-like marks on the helmet face. Small breathing holes sit well below them. Fitted dull-iron plate, soot-black padded coat, narrow divided tassets, and one short dirty-ivory abaca mourning sash at the waist.
Primary horror idea: the three large nails visibly seal the visor shut, and one narrow matte-black wax tear descends from the center nail to the bevor; no face, eyes, exposed flesh, or gore.
Secondary accent: one dirty-ivory sash, no magic.
Exact counts: three visor nails, one black wax tear, one estoc, one sash, zero shields, zero horns, zero flames, zero floating objects.
Lighting/mood: dim cold iron and terrifying stillness.
Hard count gate: show exactly THREE large visor nails, not two, not four, and no decorative rivets near them. Keep the empty left hand anatomically correct and separate from the helmet.
```
