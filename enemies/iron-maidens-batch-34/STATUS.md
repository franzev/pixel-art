## Quality-Control Review

- All five sources are square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG images.
- All five website references are 256 × 256 RGB PNG images made with
  nearest-neighbor resampling.
- Every source contains exactly one complete adult woman with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- All weapons, handles, hands, helmets, armor, legs, and feet are visible and
  uncropped with safe padding.
- The weapon set is readable and realistic: one longsword; one infantry spear;
  one bearded battle axe; one crossbow with one loaded bolt; one flanged mace
  and one plain shield.
- Every weapon uses a conventional straight grip, stock, shaft, or haft with
  coherent hand placement.
- No draft contains a fantasy weapon, weird weapon handle, secondary blade,
  chain weapon, magical mechanism, extra limb, duplicate person, scenery, text,
  green background, graphic gore, or long crimson cape.
- Armor is wearable, jointed, fully covering, and avoids exaggerated breast-cup
  plate or high heels.
- The five gameplay roles, postures, helmet profiles, armor weights, weapon
  silhouettes, and negative-space patterns remain distinct at 256 pixels.
