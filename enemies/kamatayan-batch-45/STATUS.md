The Flesh-Veil Oracle was used as the confirmed positive reference for mature
statuesque restraint, soot-dark layering, pale intact skin, worn copper, controlled
occult light, mournful menace, and visible pixel clustering. Its pose, horned
hood, garment panels, hands, spell layout, facial profile, and silhouette were
not copied.

The family expresses death through Philippine-inflected ritual obligations:
abaca binding, wake vigil, river crossing, unfinished burial labor, and capiz
funerary stillness. It does not rely on a Western skull-and-scythe archetype.
