# Living Houses and Roadside Mimics Batch 19 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-28
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design
**Status:** Draft-only

## Shared direction

Create animated rural structures that can be mistaken for ordinary roadside
objects. Use crisp pixel art, a uniform warm near-black background, readable
screen-left silhouettes, weathered bamboo, nipa, old rope, grey stone, dry tan,
clay red, amber interior light, and restrained violet curse accents. Avoid
scenery, human inhabitants, gore, green, text, logos, and soft rendering.

## Roster

1. **Stilt-Walking Bahay Kubo** — One house body and roof, one doorway with two
   eye slits, two shutter arms, exactly four bamboo stilt legs and four split
   feet, plus one hanging ladder.
2. **Well-Mouth Mimic** — One stone well body with one mouth and two eyes, four
   root legs and feet, two rope arms ending in three-fingered knot hands, one
   bucket, and one inner rope.
3. **Bamboo Bridge Serpent** — One slatted head with two eyes, one continuous
   bridge-plank body, exactly six bamboo legs and feet, one rope-bound tail,
   two back rails, and no arms or wings.
