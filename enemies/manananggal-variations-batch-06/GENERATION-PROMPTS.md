# Manananggal Variations — Prompt Set

Generated as five separate assets through the built-in image-generation workflow.
The designs use the commonly described flying form: a separated upper torso,
back-mounted bat wings, and a lower half hidden elsewhere. Accounts vary by
region and storyteller, so these are original game interpretations rather than a
definitive depiction of Philippine tradition.

## Shared direction

Create one flying Manananggal enemy for a dark-fantasy 2D Metroidvania. Show
exactly one separated upper torso, one head, two human arms, two hands, and two
bat wings attached to the back. The lower body is absent from the image. Keep the
figure centered on a plain warm near-black square background with generous
padding.

Use deliberately authored pixel art with hard square pixels, connected clusters,
restrained texture, selective highlights, and roughly 16–20 colors. Build the
base from charcoal, black-brown, corpse-pale skin, weathered cloth, and aged
metal. Deep crimson and vivid scarlet should occupy roughly 15–22 percent of each
sprite through blood, waist accents, wing veins, or magic so the designs do not
look muted.

The blood is conspicuous but stylized: clean drops, short trails, simplified
crimson ribbons, and controlled red OSL. Avoid photorealistic gore, detailed
organs, victims, entrail piles, extra limbs, arm-wings, missing wings, legs,
green, smooth gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, painterly rendering, text, borders,
and watermarks.

## Variations

1. **Roof-Hunter Manananggal** — Fast horizontal flying pose, worn indigo blouse,
   huge spread wings, reaching claws, four simplified crimson waist ribbons, and
   scarlet wing veins.
2. **Blood-Moon Matriarch** — Upright elite caster with crescent-shaped raised
   wings, silver-black braids, brass ornaments, crimson ceremonial cloth, and one
   small blood-moon orb.
3. **Thatch-Wing Prowler** — Low stalking posture, rural work clothes, abaca wrap,
   wings patterned like weathered nipa shingles, compact waist tendrils, and a
   diagonal blood trail.
4. **Bell-Tongue Ritualist** — Narrow hovering caster with folded wings, one
   cracked bronze bell, one long curled tongue, one blood-magic thread, and warm
   sound rings.
5. **Salt-Scarred Exile** — Asymmetric elite silhouette with one salt-burned but
   complete wing, dark-red mantle, bound waist ribbons, vivid blood drops, and a
   short curved blood lash.

## Saved format

Each design has:

- `*-source.png` — full generated source
- `*-reference-256.png` — 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor reference

`manananggal-variations-review-sheet.png` places all five references side by side.
