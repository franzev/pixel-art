# Mountain and Cave Horrors Batch 20 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-28
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design
**Status:** Draft-only

## Shared direction

Create subterranean enemies built around sound, roots, and darkness. Use crisp
hard-cluster pixel art, complete screen-left silhouettes, a uniform warm
near-black background, charcoal, cave brown, ash grey, dirty iron, dry root,
fiber tan, pale lavender, and chalk-white echo accents. Avoid green, scenery,
generic stone golems, gore, heroic armor, text, logos, and soft rendering.

## Roster

1. **Blind Cave Pilgrim** — One wrapped head with no eyes, two arms and hands,
   two legs and feet, one two-pronged tuning rod, one stone striker, and exactly
   three pale echo arcs.
2. **Hanging Root Lurker** — One gourd-shaped head with one eye slit, exactly
   four long root limbs ending in three-pronged claws, two ceiling-anchor
   tendrils, and no wings or tail.
3. **Echo-Mouth Hunter** — One eyeless head with one vertical mouth, two long
   arms and four-fingered hands, two reverse-jointed legs and three-toed feet,
   exactly four echo arcs, and no tail or wings.
