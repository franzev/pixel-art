## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated adult humanoid porcelain-knight enemy
concepts for one review wave. Porcelain is used as cracked, glazed, and repaired
armor plate mounted on flexible iron, leather, mail, cloth, and abaca
understructures rather than as a solid statue body.

The Porcelain Vowkeeper from `protagonist/pale-knight-orders-v16/` was used as a
visual reference only for pixel density, mature proportions, articulated armor,
restrained material rendering, and warm near-black isolation. Its exact helmet,
sword, shield, cape lattice, pose, costume, and silhouette were not copied.

The second wave responds to the user's request for helmetless women with
different hairstyles. It keeps the first wave intact and explores five new adult
female counterparts with fully visible faces, distinct hair construction, and
the same five broad encounter-role categories.

## Quality-control review

- All ten sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs.
- All ten references are 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor RGB PNGs.
- Each wave review sheet is 1280 × 256 and presents five drafts in roster
  order. The two-wave comparison sheet is 1280 × 512.
- Each image contains exactly one complete adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Helmets, hands, weapons, armor edges, cloth, legs, and feet remain inside the
  frame with useful padding.
- The backgrounds remain warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast
  shadow, text, border, logo, watermark, or green field.
- Crisp connected pixel clusters and broad material planes remain legible at
  256 pixels.
- Porcelain plates are visibly mounted to articulated understructures rather
  than replacing the body with a rigid ceramic statue.
- Weapon grips and armor joints are mechanically plausible, and every requested
  equipment count is correct.
- Wave 2 contains five helmetless adult women with clearly different hair:
  braided crown, blunt bob, high coil, long side braid, and loose waves.
- The five silhouettes and encounter roles remain distinct side by side in each
  wave, while the comparison sheet makes the intentional role pairings clear.
