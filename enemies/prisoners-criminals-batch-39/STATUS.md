## Direction

This wave depicts full-height adult prisoners and fugitives from the Ashen
Provinces. Their shared family signals are weathered local work clothing,
abaca materials, restrained sentence tags or penal iron, intact flesh, and
exhausted adult faces. The designs avoid modern orange uniforms, Western prison
stripes, generic fantasy armor, graphic gore, and depictions of any real
cultural group as monstrous.

The Flesh-Veil Oracle was used only as the strongest positive reference for
pixel discipline, mature proportions, restrained value structure, and
mournful menace. None of these drafts copies its pose, costume, horns, veil,
magic, or silhouette.
