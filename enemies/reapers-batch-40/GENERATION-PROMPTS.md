# Reapers Batch 40 — Generation Prompts

## Shared contract

- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- One isolated adult humanoid enemy per square image.
- Plain warm near-black background with no scenery, floor, text, frame, cast
  shadow, or vignette.
- Crisp hard square pixels, connected clusters, broad value masses, restrained
  color ramps, and no anti-aliasing, blur, painterly gradients, or microtexture.
- Exact humanoid anatomy: one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, two feet.
- Exactly one fully visible human-scale scythe per character.
- No animal-derived anatomy, imps, red-dominant bodies, bulky brutes, graphic
  gore, shields, sidearms, extra weapons, or copied copyrighted silhouettes.

## 01 — Rice-Wake Reaper

Create one original Ashen Provinces reaper called the Rice-Wake Reaper, a low
advancing lane-sweeper whose long harvesting scythe telegraphs a broad
ground-level crescent attack.

- Pose: three-quarter view facing left; low forward worker stance with bent
  front knee, extended rear leg, and pitched torso.
- Weapon: one long wooden harvesting scythe, two-handed grip, one broad iron
  crescent blade, dirty-ivory knot below the blade.
- Clothing: worn abaca work coat with split knee-length tails, narrow trousers,
  wrapped calves, bare feet, compact soot-dark mourning hood.
- Palette: soot black, charcoal, damp bark brown, ash grey, dirty ivory, dull
  iron, with minor cold moon-silver accents.
- No magic.

## 02 — Funeral-Lane Reaper

Create one original Ashen Provinces reaper called the Funeral-Lane Reaper, an
immobile vertical zone-controller who plants a hooked grave scythe and seals a
narrow passage.

- Pose: three-quarter view facing left; tall upright closed stance.
- Weapon: one vertical straight-shaft grave scythe with one short inward-hooked
  iron blade, gripped plausibly in two hands.
- Clothing: narrow high-collared soot-black jacket, long dirty-ivory underdress,
  asymmetric charcoal funeral veil, bronze throat clasp.
- Accent: three short braided black mourning cords at the belt.
- Palette: charcoal, soot black, dirty ivory, ash grey, worn bronze, pale skin,
  and subtle muted-indigo lining.

## 03 — Severed-Thread Reaper

Create one original Ashen Provinces reaper called the Severed-Thread Reaper, a
reactive counter-duelist who catches attacks on the crook of a compact war
scythe and answers with a diagonal hook.

- Pose: three-quarter view facing left; backward-leaning counter posture with
  open triangular negative spaces.
- Weapon: one compact war scythe with one hooked blade and one short ash-wood
  pole, held diagonally in two hands.
- Clothing: charcoal waistcoat, dirty-ivory shirt, narrow soot trousers, flat
  boots, split black overskirt, low asymmetric mouth-and-chin mask.
- Accent: one muted-violet binding cord wrapped three times around the shaft.
- No floating magic.

## 04 — Drowned-Road Reaper

Create one original Ashen Provinces reaper called the Drowned-Road Reaper, a
planted puller enemy who uses a boat-hook scythe to drag the player out of
position.

- Pose: three-quarter view facing left; wide planted stance with flexed knees
  and turned torso.
- Weapon: one horizontal bamboo-and-iron boat-hook scythe with one narrow
  inward-facing crescent blade and one blunt rear cap.
- Clothing: water-dark charcoal camisa, sleeveless layered-abaca coat, loose
  trousers, wrapped calves, sandals, compact dirty-ivory head cloth.
- Accent: one continuous slate-cyan spectral water ribbon following the lower
  half of the shaft.
- Palette: soot black, charcoal, dark brown, dirty ivory, dull iron, bamboo tan,
  slate blue, and pale cyan.

## 05 — Ember-Writ Reaper

Create one original Ashen Provinces reaper called the Ember-Writ Reaper, a slow
drifting executioner whose overhead crescent slash leaves one delayed ember arc.

- Pose: three-quarter view facing left; tall upright high-diagonal silhouette
  with one foot touching down and the other lifted.
- Weapon: one execution scythe held above and behind one shoulder, with one long
  iron shaft and one forward-curving crescent blade.
- Clothing: soot-dark sleeveless mantle, fitted dirty-ivory work tunic, wrapped
  black trousers, four broad charred cloth tails, copper waist ring, compact
  upper-face execution veil.
- Accent: one thin broken ember-orange arc made of five connected clusters.
- Palette: soot black, charcoal, dirty ivory, dull iron, aged copper, pale skin,
  and controlled ember orange.
