## Interpretation and Reference Direction

The initial request was interpreted as five separate isolated adult humanoid
enemy concepts in one review wave. The user then requested flails or weapons of
penitence without a quantity, so the project default produced one pilot
extension. The user said the wooden-beater pilot did not look like a flail. It
was replaced with an unmistakable compact one-handed ball-and-chain flail.
Each active image contains exactly one barefoot nun in a
crimson-and-weathered-white convent dress with one grounded human-scale weapon.
Horror is restrained and non-graphic.

`/Users/franz/Desktop/images.jpeg` controlled body proportions only: tall mature
adult anatomy, long legs, a defined waist and hips, and a statuesque silhouette.
The proportions were deliberately moderated. The reference's identity, face,
hair, bodysuit, colors, boots, pose, sword, platform, and futuristic design were
not copied.

The rejected Warrior Nuns Batch 32 was not used as a positive visual reference.

## Quality-Control Review

- All six sources are square 1254 × 1254 PNG files on a warm near-black
  background.
- Each image contains one complete adult woman with one head, two arms, two
  hands, two legs, and two distinct bare feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, clothing, weapon shafts, blades, bow tips, lashes, and
  quarrel tips are fully visible with safe padding.
- All weapons are human-scale and use mechanically plausible hand placement:
  a leather scourge, mancatcher, executioner's sword, war hammer, crossbow, and
  compact one-handed ball-and-chain flail.
- Crimson and white are carried by the clothing rather than the skin.
- The figures remain mature and statuesque without copying the reference pose or
  exaggerating its anatomy.
- The six silhouettes differ through open fan, diagonal pole, vertical sword,
  low hammer, horizontal crossbow, and hanging chain-flail shapes.
- All 256 × 256 references use nearest-neighbor resizing and remain readable on
  the 1536 × 256 V03 review sheet.
- No source includes a second character, footwear, scenery, floor plane, text,
  green background, magic, victim imagery, exposed organs, or graphic gore.
