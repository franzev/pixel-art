## Reference direction

The Flesh-Veil Oracle was used as the confirmed positive reference for adult
statuesque restraint, soot-dark layering, controlled occult light, and visible
pixel clustering. Its pose, veil, costume panels, spell layout, and silhouette
were not copied.

The family expresses shadow through absorbed highlights, lightless visor gaps,
flat-black cloth folds, alternating charcoal value bands, and restrained
indigo or violet accents. No detached shadow doubles, smoke bodies, or ground
shadows were used.
