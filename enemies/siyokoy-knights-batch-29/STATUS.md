## Interpretation and reference direction

This batch treats the siyokoy as a full-height adult humanoid aquatic order with
cold slate skin, neck gills, webbed extremities, water-dark abaca, corroded
metal, and restrained pale-cyan effects. It is an original Ashen Provinces game
interpretation rather than a definitive cultural portrayal.

The Mosscloak Marcher and Grove-Gate Warden controlled only mature proportions,
wearable armor, restrained materials, deliberate pixel construction, and
isolated presentation. The Drowned Net-Caster controlled only the cool
water-effect palette. No exact reference pose, helmet, costume, equipment,
anatomy, effect arrangement, or silhouette was requested.
