# Skeleton Feudal Court Batch 39 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`  
**Execution:** One fresh generation call per distinct asset

## Shared direction

Create exactly one complete adult human skeleton on a square 1:1 canvas with a
perfectly uniform warm near-black `#171311` background. Use coherent skeletal
anatomy: one skull and jaw, one rib cage, two arms, two bony hands, two legs,
and two separated feet. Keep all body parts, crowns, weapons, props, veils,
cloth, and effects fully inside the frame with generous padding.

Render genuine high-density game pixel art on an implied low-resolution grid:
hard square pixels, connected clusters, broad readable value masses, restrained
color ramps, consistent pixel density, and selective highlights. Do not use
anti-aliasing, blur, smooth painterly gradients, pixel-filtered painting,
excessive dithering, noisy microtexture, or mixed pixel sizes.

Use mature 7-to-7.5-head proportions, lean or believable medium builds, a
consistent screen-left three-quarter direction, fitted weathered equipment,
and a severe mournful occult presence. Favor soot black, charcoal, old ivory
bone, dull iron, tarnished bronze or copper, weathered brown, dirty gold, and
one controlled family accent. Use Philippine-inflected abaca, piña, banig,
rain-road, river-crossing, and funerary construction where relevant without
generic Western cathedral-fantasy excess.

Never add scenery, floor planes, cast shadows, vignettes, gradients, frames,
typography, pseudo-writing, logos, watermarks, extra characters, extra anatomy,
glowing eyes, graphic gore, chibi proportions, giant armor, green backgrounds,
red-dominant bodies, religious crosses, fleur-de-lis, or recognizable copied
game designs.

Visual references were used only for high-level family treatment:

- `protagonist/skeleton-knight-legions-v08/`
- `npcs/sovereigns-of-the-provinces-v01/drafts/05-ossuary-procession-king-source.png`
- `npcs/princesses-v01/drafts/01-mourning-seal-bearer-source.png`

The references were not edit targets. No exact pose, crown, prop arrangement,
garment system, or silhouette was copied.

## Corrective generations

- The Broken-Circlet Key Prince was replaced by the Broken-Circlet Exile Prince
  after the key and magic ring produced forbidden cross/halo imagery.
- The Ember-Vow Princess was rerendered with a completely plain collar after
  the first attempt produced a cross-like chest clasp.
