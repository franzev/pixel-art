# Spanish Colonial Corruption Batch 09 — Generation Prompts

**Workflow:** Built-in image generation, one separate call per active design  
**Status:** Unapproved replacement drafts  
**Date:** 2026-07-28

## Shared direction

Create a completely new transformed Spanish colonial soldier from scratch with
no input image. Do not edit, trace, recolor, or reuse an existing asset, pose,
or silhouette.

Corruption must be intrinsic to the body's anatomy, posture, face, hands, gait,
torn or reshaped uniform, and weapon handling. The transformed body itself must
create the new silhouette. Do not merely add orbiting particles, floating
symbols, detached shadows, halos, echo rings, puddles, roots around the feet,
extra flames, or decorative effects around an otherwise normal soldier.

Use one square `1:1` source with exactly one complete full-body enemy, centered
with safe padding on a warm near-black `#171311` backdrop. No scenery, floor,
cast shadow, text, border, logo, watermark, vignette, or green background.

Draw direct-to-grid game pixel art with hard square pixels, large connected
clusters, clean stepped edges, broad folds, strong negative space, restrained
highlights, and `16–22` meaningful colors. Avoid gradients, blur,
anti-aliasing, painterly microtexture, and isolated noise. The intended native
visible height is `58–78` pixels at an adult body scale comparable to the
Crimson Knight.

Keep the historical base recognizable as Spanish colonial Philippines,
circa 1896–1898: pale-blue/off-white rayadillo or specified dark-blue tropical
uniform, straw campaign hat or white capacete, leather cartridge gear, Model
1893 Mauser, period revolver, sabre, or entrenching tool. No conquistador armor,
morion, breastplate, Napoleonic, First World War, modern, American khaki,
fantasy plate, or hero cape.

Use integrated supernatural damage and mutation without exposed organs,
entrails, victims, pregnancy imagery, or graphic gore. Keep one head, two arms,
two hands, two legs, and two feet unless an explicit body mutation changes the
shape of one existing part. Never duplicate hands, faces, weapons, straps,
wounds, or equipment.

## Individual concepts

### 01 — Feral Aswang-Bitten Cazador

Redraw a rayadillo rifleman in a wide prowling stance. Integrate neck punctures,
a lengthened jaw with two fangs, stretched neck, clawlike five-fingered hands,
torn sleeves, and a low animal gait. He controls one Mauser across his body; no
attacker, blood spray, aura, or detached effects.

### 02 — Starved Jungle Ghoul Rifleman

Redraw an emaciated rifleman in a deep crouch with a sunken face, hanging
rayadillo, long forearms, bony knees, and boots loose around wasted legs. His
body hangs over one low Mauser; no grave scenery, insects, or floating decay.

### 03 — Inverted-Sigil Possessed Rifleman

Redraw a rifleman in a severe backward arch with violet eyes, clenched joints,
one burned reversed forehead sigil, and a straw hat hanging from its own strap.
He grips one vertical Mauser as his body twists around it. The symbol is part of
the forehead, never floating.

### 04 — Night-Listener Cazador

Redraw a tracking cazador with sealed eyes, enlarged ears, a forward-thrust
head, hunched shoulders, and a careful heel-to-toe gait. He holds one Mauser
horizontally while listening through his altered anatomy. No sound rings or
external sonic graphics.

### 05 — Zombified Bayonet Charger

Redraw a dead, twisted bayonet attacker with a collapsed shoulder, bent spine,
slack jaw, torn rayadillo, and uneven charging legs. Use exactly one Mauser with
one fixed bayonet and no detached gore.

### 06 — Carapace-Mutated Charger

Redraw a charging cazador whose existing shoulder, ribs, arm, thigh, and boot
have become burgundy-black chitin. The altered side carries several integrated
shell spines and changes the center of balance. Use one Mauser and one fixed
bayonet; no flying insects or loose shell pieces.

### 07 — Night-Vampire Bayonet Cazador

Redraw a thin, long-limbed vampire cazador in an overhead pounce. Give him an
elongated face, two fangs, long five-fingered hands, narrow shoulders, and
stretched legs. He raises one Mauser with one fixed bayonet. No cape, wings,
bats, blood spray, or mist.

### 08 — Bandage-Bound Wounded Charger

Redraw a severely wounded but coherent soldier with torso bandages, a rigid leg
splint, one dropped shoulder, and a dragging gait. Make the binding and injury
control his silhouette while he uses one bayoneted Mauser as both support and
weapon. No open organs or detached medical props.

### 09 — Inverted-Sigil Possessed Guardia

Redraw a dark-guingón Guardia under the same curse family as 03: white
capacete, backward arch, violet eyes, one reversed forehead mark, and one
vertical rifle. Use a distinct upright patrol-derived silhouette and no
floating symbols.

### 10 — Waterlogged Guardia Revenant

Redraw a Guardia whose soaked body and heavy dark uniform sag downward. Make
the face, sleeves, shoulders, trouser cuffs, and gait visibly water-heavy while
he carries one rifle across the body. No puddle, rain, dripping particles, or
external water effect.

### 11 — Gaunt Guardia Ghoul

Redraw an emaciated Guardia with an oversized capacete caused by wasting,
sunken cheeks, narrow torso, long forearms, and a low hungry stance. He holds
one rifle crosswise. No detached grave elements or gore.

### 12 — Fire-Charred Guardia

Redraw a Guardia with one half of the existing body burned into charcoal-black
skin and cracked dark cloth with restrained internal ember color. The charred
side folds inward while he braces one rifle upright. No external flame, smoke,
sparks, or burning scenery.

### 13 — Root-Mutated Colonial Sapper

Redraw an engineer whose existing tool arm and both lower legs have become
woody, rootlike anatomy. Rayadillo splits around the changed limbs and the new
weight creates a broad dragging stance. He holds one short spade; one Mauser is
slung behind. No roots growing into the floor.

### 14 — Grave-Hungry Sapper Ghoul

Redraw a sapper ghoul with a starved face, hanging campaign clothing, long
working arms, and a violent overhead tool swing. Use one short spade, one slung
Mauser, and one satchel. No grave, corpse, soil spray, or detached debris.

### 15 — Beetle-Carapace Colonial Sapper

Redraw a sapper whose left cheek, shoulder, ribs, arm, hand, and part of one
boot have become amber-violet beetle shell. Split the rayadillo around the
integrated armor and make its weight pull him into a low side stance. He holds
one short spade in both hands; one Mauser is slung behind. No insects, wings,
antennae, or particles.

### 16 — Aswang-Swollen Colonial Sapper

Redraw an infected sapper with two neck punctures, two restrained fangs, and
one massively swollen corded tool arm tearing through its sleeve. Bend the rest
of the body to counterbalance the altered limb. He grips one low spade in both
hands; one Mauser is strapped behind. No visible attacker or aura.

### 17 — Blood-Starved Vampire Officer

Redraw a dark-uniform colonial officer as a long-limbed stalking vampire with
an elongated face, two fangs, crimson eyes, narrow shoulders, bowed spine, and
long five-fingered hands. He holds one period revolver low and keeps one sabre
fully sheathed. No cape, wings, bats, mist, or victims.

### 18 — Marionette-Possessed Officer

Redraw an officer whose own joints are seized into a rigid crossing step:
closed eyes, uneven shoulders, sharp but coherent elbow and wrist angles,
crooked buttons, and one reversed forehead sigil. One revolver hangs loosely
and one sabre stays sheathed. No strings, puppeteer, cross, smoke, or aura.

### 19 — Hollow-Chested Cursed Officer

Redraw an officer whose sternum and abdomen collapse deeply inward beneath the
uniform, bowing the buttons and cross-strap into a narrow midsection. Cave one
side of the face and fold the posture forward. He holds one revolver close to
the chest and one sabre remains sheathed. No body hole, exposed ribs, smoke, or
mist.

### 20 — Mirror-Mutated Officer

Redraw an officer whose left face, neck, shoulder, arm, hand, and one strip of
the leg have become angular silver-blue crystalline flesh. Use a rigid
fractured zigzag posture. The normal hand aims one revolver; the altered hand
holds the hilt of one fully sheathed sabre. No floating shards, mirrors,
sparkles, or detached effects.

the user clarified that the soldiers themselves must be redrawn. At the user's
request, every source is preserved in `rejected/effects-first-pass/` together
with a 256 reference and separate archive review sheets. They may be inspected
or reused as exploratory concepts, but must not be counted, promoted, or
referenced as canonical assets unless the user explicitly changes their status.
