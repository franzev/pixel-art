# Spanish Colonial Corruption Batch 09 — Generation Prompts

**Workflow:** Built-in image generation, one separate call per generated design  
**Status:** Unapproved replacement drafts with two preserved rejected passes  
**Date:** 2026-07-28; specialist and support/siege extensions added 2026-07-29

## Shared direction

Create a completely new transformed Spanish colonial soldier from scratch with
no input image. Do not edit, trace, recolor, or reuse an existing asset, pose,
or silhouette.

Corruption must be intrinsic to the body's anatomy, posture, face, hands, gait,
torn or reshaped uniform, and weapon handling. The transformed body itself must
create the new silhouette. Do not merely add orbiting particles, floating
symbols, detached shadows, halos, echo rings, puddles, roots around the feet,
extra flames, or decorative effects around an otherwise normal soldier.

Use one square `1:1` source with exactly one complete full-body enemy, centered
with safe padding on a warm near-black `#171311` backdrop. No scenery, floor,
cast shadow, text, border, logo, watermark, vignette, or green background.

Draw direct-to-grid game pixel art with hard square pixels, large connected
clusters, clean stepped edges, broad folds, strong negative space, restrained
highlights, and `16–22` meaningful colors. Avoid gradients, blur,
anti-aliasing, painterly microtexture, and isolated noise. The intended native
visible height is `58–78` pixels at an adult body scale comparable to the
Crimson Knight.

Keep the historical base recognizable as Spanish colonial Philippines,
circa 1896–1898: pale-blue/off-white rayadillo or specified dark-blue tropical
uniform, straw campaign hat or white capacete, leather cartridge gear, Model
1893 Mauser, period revolver, sabre, or entrenching tool. No conquistador armor,
morion, breastplate, Napoleonic, First World War, modern, American khaki,
fantasy plate, or hero cape.

Use integrated supernatural damage and mutation without exposed organs,
entrails, victims, pregnancy imagery, or graphic gore. Keep one head, two arms,
two hands, two legs, and two feet unless an explicit body mutation changes the
shape of one existing part. Never duplicate hands, faces, weapons, straps,
wounds, or equipment.

## Specialist extension

Assets 21–30 use the same full-redraw, anatomy, backdrop, pixel-treatment, and
historical restrictions above. An additional uniqueness constraint prohibited
the familiar straw-hat rifleman, bayonet charger, white-capacete Guardia,
shovel sapper, and dark revolver-and-sabre officer silhouettes. Each prompt
instead specified a different service branch, uniform cut, headgear, tool,
posture, and integrated transformation.

cheek, neck, shoulder, arm, side ribs, hand, and lower leg with dirty
ivory-and-coral-red branching reef anatomy that makes the whole body list

secured across the back. No water, fish, bubbles, seaweed, floor reef, or
floating coral.

### 23 — Bridle-Faced Cavalry Trooper
