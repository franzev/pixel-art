This batch redraws late-nineteenth-century Spanish colonial soldiers as complete
supernatural enemy variants. The transformation is built into each new body's
anatomy, posture, uniform damage, silhouette, and weapon handling. These are not
edits or effect layers applied to Batch 08.

The first effects-led pass was rejected after review and moved to
`rejected-effects-first-pass/`. At the user's request, all 15 sources are
preserved in the workspace with their own 256 references and archive review sheets.
They remain excluded from the active draft count, active review sheets, and
promotion gate unless the user later changes their status.

## Research boundaries

- [Rayadillo: The Spanish Overseas Colonial Uniform](https://www.1898miniaturas.com/en/article/rayadillo-spanish-overseas-uniform/)
  informed rayadillo, straw campaign hats, leather equipment, and Model 1893
  Mauser cues.
- [Kabihisnan 2025](https://renacimientomanila.org/2025/06/05/kabihisnan-2025/)
  informed the Guardia Civil/Guardia Civil Veterana dark guingón, white
  capacete, and mixed force context.
- [The Aswang Project: Sonic Categories as Technology of the Self](https://journals.upd.edu.ph/index.php/Lirip/article/download/8824/7784/)
  was used only as a reminder that aswang and tiktik traditions vary by region
  and that sound is central to some tellings. The batch therefore treats the
  named infections as game interpretations, not a definitive folklore taxonomy.
- [NCCA: Intangible Heritage](https://ncca.gov.ph/about-culture-and-arts/culture-profile/intangible-heritage/)
  informed the decision to avoid presenting one invented monster anatomy as a
  fixed historical account of living oral traditions.
