**Status:** Unapproved draft batch  
**Generated:** 2026-07-28  
**Retention:** 0 retained; 5 draft designs  
**Lore/count changes:** None

This batch depicts Spanish colonial military opponents in the Philippines,
circa 1896–1898. The expeditionary infantry, engineer, and officer are
specifically presented as Peninsular Spaniards. The Guardia Civil Veterana
design is specifically a Peninsular Spanish veteran NCO in a force that
historically included both Spaniards and Filipinos under Spanish leadership.
The batch must not be used to imply that all colonial enlisted troops were
ethnically Spanish.

  — rayadillo clothing, leather cartridge boxes, black-ribbon straw hats,
  national-color cockades, officer rank placement, and Model 1893 Mauser
  equipment.
- [Kabihisnan 2025](https://renacimientomanila.org/2025/06/05/kabihisnan-2025/)
  — Army of the Philippines composition; Guardia Civil and Guardia Civil
  Veterana guingón uniforms, maroon trim, hombreras, forrajera, white
  capacete, and mixed Spanish/Filipino personnel.

  — the limited Peninsular component before the uprising, expeditionary
  reinforcement, and the 1896 expansion of engineer units.
