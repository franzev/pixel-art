## Interpretation

The brief was treated as five isolated late-medieval or early-modern
Iberian-inspired adult humanoid knight orders rather than another extension of
the existing 1896–1898 Spanish colonial riflemen. Spanish identity is carried
through plausible armet, sallet, brigandine, half-armor, adarga, montante,
lance, crossbow, and mace construction. Abaca and tropical weathering connect
the family to the Ashen Provinces without turning it into a generic European
cathedral-fantasy faction.

## Reference role

The retained Holy Knight Realism Pass v05 was used as the closest accepted
direction for adult proportion, wearable armor, helmet scale, articulation,
weapon handling, and 256-pixel readability. It was a visual direction
reference, not an edit target; its exact poses, helmets, clothing, props, and
silhouettes were not copied.

## Draft roster

1. **Black Montante Processional** — tall area-control elite with one
   two-handed montante in a low diagonal guard.
2. **Ash-Adarga Counterduelist** — compact defensive elite with one side sword
   and one heart-shaped adarga using a visible interior grip and forearm strap.
3. **Reed-Sash Dismounted Lancer** — wide reach-control elite bracing one short
   war lance.
4. **Soot-Sallet Crossbow Castellan** — compact ranged overwatch elite with one
   steel crossbow and one loaded quarrel.
5. **Ivory-Gorget Mace Penitent** — narrow close-range stagger elite carrying
   one short-hafted flanged mace.

## Quality-control review

- All five sources are separate square 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs.
- Each image contains exactly one complete adult humanoid with one head, two
  arms, two hands, two legs, and two separated feet.
- Heads, hands, feet, cloth, shield, and weapon tips remain inside the frame
  with safe padding.
- Repeated equipment counts are readable: one montante; one side sword and one
  adarga; one lance; one crossbow with one loaded quarrel; and one mace.
- The adarga is shown from its interior with an aligned hand grip and forearm
  strap rather than an implausible exterior handle.
- Armor remains human-proportioned with readable sight lines, breathing paths,
  neck clearance, joint articulation, and plausible weapon grips.
- Backgrounds are warm near-black with no scenery, floor plane, cast shadow,
  text, border, logo, watermark, or green field.
- Crisp connected clusters and broad value masses remain legible at 256 × 256.
- Silhouettes remain distinct at review scale: tall sword diagonal, compact
  shield guard, wide lance brace, horizontal crossbow, and closed mace carry.
- All five use a consistent three-quarter presentation facing screen-left.
- No copied historical-order insignia, Crimson Knight costume, bulky fantasy
  armor, animal anatomy, graphic gore, or red-dominant palette was accepted.
- The first Montante Processional attempt was rejected because it returned a
  portrait canvas instead of the required square. It was not copied into the
  project.
