# Tiyanak Variations Batch 17 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-28
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design
**Status:** Draft-only

## Shared direction

Create three non-graphic, clearly supernatural tiyanak variations for a gothic
2D Metroidvania. Regional accounts differ, so these are original game
interpretations rather than definitive portrayals. Keep the creatures
child-sized but avoid realistic infant anatomy, victims, wounds, gore, or
photorealistic distress. Use crisp pixel clusters on a warm near-black
background with bamboo, reed, ash cloth, corpse-pale grey, bruised violet,
slate blue, and controlled ember-orange accents.

## Roster

1. **Bamboo-Cradle Tiyanak** — One mask head with two eyes and one mouth, two
   long arms and four-fingered hands, two bent legs and clawed feet, one broken
   woven bamboo cradle shell, and one trailing swaddling strip.
2. **River-Cry Tiyanak** — One mask head with two eyes and one circular mouth,
   two long arms and webbed four-fingered hands, two frog-like legs and webbed
   feet, and one wet reed sash trailing screen-right.
3. **Ash-Swaddled Tiyanak** — One soot-dark mask head with two ember eyes and
   one mouth, two arms and four-fingered hands, two small legs and clawed feet,
   one ash-grey swaddling bundle, and one crescent ash plume.
