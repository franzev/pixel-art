# Treant Knights Batch 25 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-30
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design
**Status:** Ten-design draft collection in two five-design review waves

## Interpretation and reference

Create five separate adult humanoid Treant knight enemy concepts for the Ashen
Provinces. “Treant” means a person-like arboreal body with bark, root, and
heartwood anatomy—not an animal-headed creature and not a normal human knight
with decorative branches attached.

The strongest confirmed liked character reference is:

`enemies/infernal-demons-batch-13/drafts/03-flesh-veil-oracle-source.png`

Use it as a visual treatment reference only for mature statuesque proportions,
severe melancholy presence, connected pixel clusters, broad value masses,
restrained materials, and controlled warm light. Do not copy its pose, veil,
costume, facial profile, spell arrangement, or silhouette.

The existing Balete Forest Court drafts were inspected only to avoid duplicating
their crown, leaf-duelist, and mushroom-lantern silhouettes. They are not
approval anchors.

## Shared production direction

- Use case: `stylized-concept`.
- Asset type: isolated enemy concept master for a 2D gothic Metroidvania.
- Generate exactly one complete character on each square 1:1 canvas.
- Face the gameplay silhouette toward screen-left in a readable three-quarter
  view.
- Use a uniform warm near-black background, preferably `#171311`.
- Show a full-height adult humanoid tree-being: exactly one head, two arms, two
  hands, two articulated legs, and two clearly separated feet.
- Build a lean or believable medium body from coherent bark plates and heartwood
  joints. Do not add branch arms, root tentacles, extra faces, or extra limbs.
- Armor must fit the humanoid body and articulate at the neck, shoulders,
  elbows, wrists, hips, knees, and ankles.
- Use Philippine-inflected materials where functional: balete wood, abaca
  bindings, split bamboo, damp iron, woven plant fiber, and restrained bronze.
- Palette: charcoal, bark brown, soil, ash, dirty ivory, dull iron, and moss-dark
  olive kept subordinate; controlled amber only where specified.
- Use deliberate crisp medium-density pixel art with hard square pixels,
  connected clusters, stepped contours, broad values, restrained ramps, and
  sparse highlights.
- Keep every head, hand, foot, weapon, shield, cloth tail, smoke accent, and
  silhouette extension comfortably inside the frame with generous padding.
- Mood: severe, mournful, occult, weathered, grounded, and non-graphic.
- No scenery, floor, cast shadow, gradient, vignette, frame, typography,
  watermark, extra character, animal anatomy, bright green dominance, green
  background, leafy fantasy costume, red-dominant body, oversized weapon,
  graphic gore, antialiasing, blur, painterly rendering, mixed pixel sizes, or
  noisy microtexture.
- Do not copy a copyrighted character or reuse one armor body with different
  branches.

## Wave 2 — Visible living vegetation

The user requested five additional Treant knights and explicitly asked to see
greens from vegetation. For Designs 06–10, natural living vegetation must be a
clearly visible 20–30% of the character silhouette. Use readable clusters of
fern, moss, vine, orchid leaf, or seed-pod green rather than tiny scattered leaf
specks.

This directly supersedes Wave 1's more restrictive green coverage. The plain
background remains warm near-black, and green must not become a background,
fog, magical wash, reflected light, metal tint, or whole-body tint. Preserve
bark, charcoal, iron, soil, dirty ivory, abaca, and bronze as the structural
palette.

## Review gate

Inspect every generation at full resolution and at 256 × 256. Reject malformed
anatomy, incorrect counts, missing padding, non-pixel rendering, green
backgrounds, scenery, ambiguous props, or duplicate silhouettes. Save viable
work only in the batch's `drafts/` paths, assemble a five-panel 1280 × 256
nearest-neighbor review sheet, and keep every asset draft-only until the user
explicitly approves it.
