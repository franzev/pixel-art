# Typhoon and Flood-Born Batch 14 — Generation Prompts

**Prepared:** 2026-07-28
**Workflow:** Built-in image generation; one fresh call per design
**Status:** Draft-only

## Shared direction

Create original typhoon- and flood-born enemy concepts for a 2D gothic
Metroidvania. Use genuine crisp pixel art with hard square pixels, connected
clusters, stepped edges, strong negative space, and restrained color ramps.
Present exactly one complete enemy on a uniform warm near-black background with
generous padding. Keep every gameplay-defining prop, limb, cloth trail, and
effect visible.

The palette uses soot black, charcoal, weathered storm-damaged materials,
rain-dark brown, slate blue, pale cyan, and spectral white. Green backgrounds,
smooth gradients, blur, painterly rendering, text, logos, scenery, and cropped
silhouettes are forbidden.

## 01 — Wind-Torn Processioner

**Primary request:** A storm revenant that advances against typhoon winds and
attacks with compressed gusts.

Exactly one tall gaunt humanoid enemy facing screen-left, with one cloth-covered
head and no visible face, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two bare feet. Both
hands grip one long weathered bamboo processional pole held diagonally forward.
A shredded abaca mourning shroud and exactly three long cloth streamers blow
violently toward screen-right. One narrow crescent of pale wind curls behind the
pole, clearly separated from the body.

Use soot black, charcoal, rain-dark brown, faded abaca tan, and blue-grey with
controlled pale cyan and spectral-white wind accents. Avoid extra anatomy,
cropping, plate armor, a crimson cape, a heroic knight stance, gore, green,
soft rendering, scenery, and shadows.

## 02 — Flood-Debris Colossus

**Primary request:** A slow siege-sized creature assembled by supernatural
floodwater from wrecked village materials.

Exactly one large hunched nonhuman construct facing screen-left. It has one
cracked clay-jar head cavity with a single narrow cyan glow and no human face,
two massive asymmetrical arms, two broad hands with four countable driftwood
digits each, two short braced legs, and two wide timber feet. Its body is formed
from lashed bamboo, broken nipa roof slats, one overturned banca plank across
the back, rope knots, and a cracked wooden door forming the chest. Spectral
floodwater circulates through one open chest gap and pours in one controlled
ribbon behind it.

Use soaked charcoal, weathered bamboo brown, faded nipa tan, dirty clay, and
black rope with controlled slate-blue, pale-cyan, and spectral-white water.
Avoid extra anatomy, a human face, bodybuilder proportions, a generic stone
golem, armor, weapons, gore, bones, green, soft rendering, scenery, and shadows.

## 03 — Lightning Santelmo

**Primary request:** An original game interpretation of a wandering Philippine
fire spirit transformed by a typhoon into a fast aerial lightning enemy.

Exactly one hovering nonhumanoid storm-fire entity leaning toward screen-left.
It has one large central teardrop-shaped blue-white flame core containing a

Use deep indigo, storm blue, pale cyan, spectral white, and a very small
ember-orange heart. Avoid extra flames or eyes, humanoid anatomy, bird or dragon
forms, wings, cropping, photorealistic fire, diffuse glow fog, green, soft
rendering, scenery, and shadows.
