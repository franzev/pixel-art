# Vampire Brides Batch 35 — Replacement Generation Record 06–25 v02

## Reason for replacement

The first 06–25 extension pass was rejected because its figures looked like
copies differentiated mainly by color. Replacement v02 treats pose identity as
the first design decision.

## Shared generation contract

- Use the approved 01–05 review sheet as the positive character-family and
  pixel-treatment reference.
- Use the rejected 06–25 grid only as a negative reference. Never copy its
  generic upright or forward-lunge templates.
- Generate one mature fictional vampire bride per call.
- Give every figure an unmistakably different pose, action line, center of
  gravity, arm arrangement, leg arrangement, facing, and implied motion.
- Show murderous intent through red eyes, fangs, predatory gaze, and decisive
  attack mechanics.
- Use ordinary swords, daggers, spears, bows, one bolo, or empty hands only.
- No ropes, loose cords, chains, shields, whips, strange handles, novelty
  weapons, extra anatomy, animal traits, graphic gore, scenery, text, or green
  backgrounds.
- Keep the full silhouette and equipment inside a warm near-black frame with
  generous padding.
- Render crisp intentional pixel art with hard clusters and a limited palette.
