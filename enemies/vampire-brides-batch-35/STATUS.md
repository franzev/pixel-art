## User correction applied

The governing requirement for v02 is that pose identity must remain obvious
even if every character is reduced to a black silhouette. Palette, faction,
weapon, and dress styling are secondary.

- Every replacement has a different action line, center of gravity, leg
  arrangement, arm arrangement, and orientation.
- Murderous intent is communicated through attack mechanics, gaze, bared
  fangs, and predatory body language.
- New designs contain no ropes, loose cords, chains, shields, whips, or
  novelty weapons.
- “Different races” is implemented as fictional vampire bloodlines and
  factions, not real ethnic groups.

## Quality-control review

- All 20 v02 sources are separate RGB PNGs. Their canvases adapt to the
  silhouette: square, landscape, or portrait as needed.
- All 20 matching references are 256 × 256 nearest-neighbor RGB PNGs.
- The combined 1280 × 1024 sheet shows 20 distinct silhouettes with no reused
  pose template.
- Weapons are limited to ordinary swords, daggers, spears, bows, one bolo, or
  empty hands.
- All characters are complete adult humanoid vampire brides with readable
  faces, red eyes, fangs, and murderous intent.
- Backgrounds remain warm near-black with no scenery, text, border, watermark,
  or green field.
- Dress construction changes within and across factions rather than relying on
  recoloring alone.
