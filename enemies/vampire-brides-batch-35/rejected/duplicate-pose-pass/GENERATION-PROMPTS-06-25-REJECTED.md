## User direction

Create twenty more vampire brides after approval of designs 01–05. Give them
murderous intent, different dress construction and colors, and distinct
fictional factions or supernatural bloodlines. Do not use ropes or weird
weapons.

## Shared generation contract

Every prompt requested exactly one complete adult humanoid vampire woman on a
square canvas, facing screen-right in a clear attack-ready pose. Each required
one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet; intact supernatural skin;
two short fangs; small hostile red or scarlet irises; and unmistakable intent to
kill without a victim or graphic gore.

Every image used a uniform warm near-black background with no scenery, floor,
cast shadow, gradient, text, frame, logo, or watermark. Rendering required
deliberate crisp pixel art with hard square pixels, connected clusters, broad
value masses, restrained ramps, consistent pixel density, clean edges, and
readability at 256 pixels. Anti-aliasing, blur, painterly gradients, noisy
microtexture, and pixel-filter rendering were prohibited.

Weapon prompts permitted only familiar ordinary forms with conventional grips:
plain straight swords, plain daggers, straight-shaft leaf-point spears, simple
recurve bows with one string and one nocked arrow, and one plain Filipino bolo.
Ropes, cords, chains, whips, shields, hooks, forks, serrations, split blades,
sculpted handles, curved shafts, fantasy attachments, extra weapons, bat
anatomy, animal traits, horns, bulky armor, crowns, green backgrounds, cropped
elements, and extra anatomy were prohibited. Weaponless concepts required zero
weapons, props, or magic and ordinary human hands without claws.

“Different races” was interpreted as fictional vampire bloodlines rather than
real ethnic groups.
