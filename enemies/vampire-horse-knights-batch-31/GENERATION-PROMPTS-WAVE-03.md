## Correction direction

This wave follows `REJECTION-NOTES.md`. Drafts 01–10 remain positive references
only for render quality, deliberate pixel clustering, mounted anatomy, framing,
and near-black isolation. Their weak vampire identity and underscaled or
ambiguous weapons are negative references and must not be copied.

The Blood-Starved Vampire Officer and Night-Vampire Bayonet Cazador sources
guide only pallid skin, red irises, and restrained readable fangs. Their
uniforms, poses, anatomy, weapons, and feral body language must not be copied.

Every viable design must pass two tests at 256 pixels:

1. It is unmistakably a mounted vampire knight.
2. Its weapon is long enough, ordinary, and mechanically understandable.

## Review gate

Inspect every generation at full resolution and at 256 × 256. Reject any draft
that does not immediately read as a mounted vampire knight, has a visibly short
or ambiguous weapon, has a strange handle or shaft, loses counted anatomy, or
fails the master render contract. Save viable work only under `drafts/`.
