# Vampire Knights Batch 37 — Rejection Notes

Last updated: 2026-07-30

## Direct user feedback

Visible lower legs look weird for this family. Future continuations must expose
skin through the arms or controlled upper-thigh openings instead.

## Active correction lock

- Do not create new vampire knights with bare calves, shins, ankles, open
  sandals, or other exposed lower-leg construction.
- Cover both legs from the knee down with fitted greaves and closed flat boots.
- Prefer clearly visible bare arms or narrow outer upper-thigh mobility panels.
- When thighs are visible, keep the groin, buttocks, inner thighs, and lower
  legs protected.
- Skin exposure remains non-sexualized: no cleavage, lingerie construction,
  garters, stockings, high heels, pin-up poses, or exaggerated anatomy.

Draft 09 remains an unapproved historical draft. This feedback does not approve
it, promote it, or request its deletion.

## Repeated identity problem

After reviewing Drafts 01–15, the user said the characters had:

- The same hairstyles.
- The same faces.
- The same hair color.
- The same body muscle.
- Insufficient variety and insufficiently distinct characteristics.

This means weapon, stance, armor, and exposed-skin changes were not enough.
Future concepts must also differ visibly through face shape, facial features,
hair construction, hair value or color, age impression, height, shoulder width,
torso mass, limb thickness, and muscle distribution.

Do not default every vampire knight to:

- A young symmetrical oval face.
- Black hair in a bun or ponytail.
- The same narrow-waisted athletic body.
- The same shoulder width, arm definition, bust construction, and leg mass.
- A new costume or weapon placed on an otherwise cloned character.

## Wave 4 full rejection

The attempted identity-diversity correction was stopped during Wave 4. The user
rejected every Wave 4 attempt and said:

> “you've become WOKE!”

No Wave 4 source, 256 reference, prompt entry, manifest entry, or review sheet
was saved to the project.

The phrase above is recorded verbatim as direct feedback. It does not identify
which specific visual traits caused the reaction. Do not infer from it that the
user rejected a particular age, ethnicity, body type, face, hairstyle, or hair
color individually. The only safe conclusions are:

- The complete Wave 4 direction was unacceptable.
- Its visible renders are rejection references, never positive references.
- A checklist-driven attempt at differentiation did not satisfy the requested
  visual variety.
- “Distinct characteristics” must remain cohesive with the user's intended
  vampire-knight aesthetic rather than becoming variety for its own sake.

Before another continuation, do not reuse the Wave 4 identity matrix or its
generated designs. Present a compact visual-direction interpretation that
explains how variety will stay within one cohesive family.
