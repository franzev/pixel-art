- Generate one fresh source per design.
- Exactly one complete character per square image.
- Face the gameplay silhouette toward screen-left.
- Keep the entire hood, hands, cloth tails, feet, and spell effects uncropped.
- Use a uniform warm near-black background with no green cast.
- No ground plane, scenery, shadow, vignette, border, text, or additional figure.
- Use genuine crisp low-shade pixel art with hard square pixels, connected
  clusters, stepped contours, two or three shades per material, and sparse
  highlights.
- Keep the body adult, lean, fully clothed, nonsexualized, and anatomically
  coherent: one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Flesh remains intact and stylized. No open wounds, organs, flaying, or gore.
- Use original broad grimdark design language without recognizable franchise
  costumes, symbols, armor, silhouettes, or weapons.
- Regenerate anatomy failures from scratch; do not paint over or composite.

## Roster

### 01 — Ember-Triad Veilseer

Tall narrow artillery caster with a split two-pronged soot-black veil, a fitted
burgundy shoulder mantle, one high hand and one low hand, and exactly three
ember spheres arranged in a descending diagonal from upper-left to lower-right.
The three spheres differ in size and are connected by no line. Long split skirt
and one trailing cloth ribbon create a narrow S-shaped silhouette. No weapon.

Filename slug: `ember-triad-veilseer`

### 02 — Cinder-Thread Binder

Wide horizontal control caster with a short double-horn hood, layered black
apron panels, and both pale arms extended far apart. Exactly three thin
ember-orange threads stretch between the hands and cross once into one
triangular snare. No orb, staff, mirror, weapon, or floating object. One rear
burgundy mantle tail flows toward screen-right.

Filename slug: `cinder-thread-binder`

### 03 — Pyre-Sleeve Channeler

Broad defensive caster with a smooth blindfold veil, two short backward horns,
and very wide layered burgundy sleeves held outward like a barrier while
remaining clearly attached to two arms. Exactly one compact ember flame hovers
above each open palm, two flames total. The lower garment is narrow and straight
to contrast the wide sleeves. No wings, orb, weapon, or staff.

Filename slug: `pyre-sleeve-channeler`

### 04 — Ash-Crown Augur

Extremely narrow upright caster with a long pointed soot-black hood, one broken
horn stump and one intact horn, hands folded low at the waist, and exactly five
small ember motes forming an uneven broken arc above and behind the hood.
Layered charcoal cloth dominates; burgundy appears only in the inner lining.
No handheld spell, orb, mirror, staff, or weapon.

Filename slug: `ash-crown-augur`

### 05 — Furnace-Veil Matriarch

Compact commanding caster with a broad triangular horned veil, short layered
black-and-burgundy skirt panels, and both pale arms forming a protective circle
around exactly one large contained ember sphere at the sternum. The hands do
not touch the sphere. Two long cloth tails sweep backward to screen-right. No
additional motes, weapon, staff, mirror, or armor.

Filename slug: `furnace-veil-matriarch`
