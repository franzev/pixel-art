# Warrior White Ladies Batch 33 — Rejection Notes

Last updated: 2026-07-30

## Verdict

Full rejection. Do not retain, promote, add to lore, or use any Batch 33 output
as a positive visual reference.

## Explicit User Feedback

The user asked to avoid shields when they are not appropriate and identified
weird shield-handle placement.

Future concepts must therefore treat a shield as functional equipment rather
than a generic silhouette device. Its interior grip or straps, hand placement,
wrist angle, forearm alignment, and load-bearing logic must all be readable and
mechanically plausible.

The rejection applies to the whole visible wave. The shield critique is the only
asset-specific reason recorded, so no additional reasons should be inferred for
the other rejected designs.
