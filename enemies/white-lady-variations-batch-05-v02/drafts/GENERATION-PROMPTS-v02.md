# White Lady Low-Resolution Redraw Test — v02

Generated separately through the built-in image-generation workflow. The
retained Balete Bride source was used only as an identity, anatomy, pose,
costume, prop, silhouette, and palette reference.

## 02 — Balete Bride

Redraw the Balete Bride from scratch as genuine native game pixel art authored
as though directly on a virtual 96 × 96 canvas.

Preserve the exact tall adult feminine proportions, compact head, quiet
three-quarter pose facing screen-right, long black hair, broad bridal veil
flowing strongly screen-left, restrained layered white bridal dress, wide
sleeves, woven waist sash, one loop of dark remembrance beads hanging from the
screen-right hand, and exactly three separate pale root wisps beside the feet.
Preserve one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two clearly readable bare
feet with natural attachment points. Preserve the balance between the large
left-flowing veil and upright body.

Use visibly coarse, consistent hard square logical pixels, large connected
clusters, broad cloth shapes, and clean negative space. Use no more than 16 flat
colors total, with only shadow, midtone, and an optional highlight per material.
Build the veil from a few broad flat shapes, the dress from a few large folds,
the hair from two solid dark masses, the beads from one simple loop, and each
root wisp from one thick stepped silhouette.

Preserve pearl white, aged ivory, silver grey, cold blue-grey, charcoal, and
black hair with tiny icy-cyan highlights. Do not introduce green, emerald, teal,
or lime into the White Lady.

Avoid high-resolution illustration treatment, lace patterns, fabric hatching,
diagonal microstripes, tiny edge ornament, transitional shades, fragmented
strands, gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, painterly shading, dithering, glow,
isolated noise, scenery, floor planes, text, borders, logos, and watermarks.

The result is a draft redraw test and is not yet canonical.
