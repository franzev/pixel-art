# The Balete Moon Garden — Green-Biome White Lady Fit Test

## Workflow

1. Generate a completely empty environment plate with no people, enemies,
   ghosts, statues, or supernatural silhouettes.
2. Treat the environment as a 384 × 216 native pixel-art canvas.
3. Reduce the empty environment to 20 flat colors without dithering.
4. Strengthen only the natural vegetation ramp at native resolution so forest,
   fern, moss, and leaf greens remain visibly distinct at game scale.
5. Prepare a transparent 96 × 96 mockup cutout from the separate Balete Bride
   v02 redraw.
6. Composite that exact cutout over the native environment. Do not ask the
   image-generation model to recreate the White Lady inside the room.
7. Review the result against a 24 × 24 gameplay grid.

## Empty environment prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: empty side-scrolling game environment plate and 24 × 24 tile-scale
mockup base

Primary request: Create a completely character-free 16:9 side-view room called
the Balete Moon Garden for a dark gothic Metroidvania. It must feel noticeably
greener, more alive, and more colorful than the previous muted stone and flooded
market rooms while remaining eerie and playable.

Scene/backdrop: A humid overgrown Philippine balete grove at night. Two enormous
balete trunks frame the far left and far right, with thick descending roots and
broad root platforms. A moss-covered stone causeway forms the main combat floor
across the lower middle with one short jumpable gap exposing shallow dark water.
At left, three large fern clumps and broad tropical leaves grow around a broken
roadside shrine. At center, a clear open combat space sits beneath an arch formed
by one thick balete root. At right, a raised root-and-stone platform stands above
the main floor and is reached by three large root steps. The background contains
layered leaf masses, hanging vines, and one distant moonlit clearing—not a flat
green field. Include two golden firefly clusters, one small white flower patch,
broad water reflections, and visible moss bands. No people, ghosts, White
Ladies, creatures, statues, faces, or body-like silhouettes.

Style/medium: Native low-resolution pixel art authored as though directly on a
virtual 384 × 216 canvas. Use coarse, consistent hard square pixels, large
connected clusters, stepped silhouettes, broad leaf shapes, and tile-friendly
construction on an implied 24 × 24 grid. Use about 18–20 meaningful flat colors,
with two or three shades per material. Do not render foliage leaf by leaf.

Composition/framing: Strict orthographic side view with clear horizontal
traversal, a main floor baseline at y=168, one short gap, one raised right
platform, large uncluttered spaces for a 96 × 96 actor, and no perspective floor
plane.

Lighting/mood: Humid moonlit night with stronger color separation and clearer
midtones. Natural foliage should be visibly green at gameplay scale. Use small
warm-gold fireflies and pale moonlight without bloom.

Color palette: Warm near-black and brown-black remain only in the deepest sky
and shadows. Vegetation uses a clearly visible natural green ramp: deep forest
green, rich leaf green, moss green, fern green, and restrained yellow-green
highlights. Add wet bark browns, warm grey stone, pale moonlit silver, muted
gold, and a small white flower accent. Greens occupy roughly 35–45 percent of
the room and are more saturated than previous environments. These greens belong
only to living vegetation and moss, not supernatural energy. Never use a flat
green background.

Constraints: Environment only. Preserve strong value separation behind a future
pearl-white character. Keep foliage in large readable masses. Align floors and
ledges naturally to 24-pixel modules. The room must remain readable at actual
384 × 216.

Avoid characters, enemies, human silhouettes, pale ghost-like tree shapes,
green fog, green magical glow, a uniform green wash, monochrome treatment,
muddy all-brown palettes, microtexture, hundreds of tiny leaves, noisy grass
pixels, individual bark scratches, painterly rendering, smooth gradients, blur,
anti-aliasing, soft edges, excessive dithering, isolated noise pixels, dense
rain, UI, text, labels, borders, and watermarks.

## Mockup note

The populated room and transparent cutout are scale and readability studies.
They are not a finalized production room, animation set, or canonical tileset.
