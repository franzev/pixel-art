# The Drowned Market — Low-Resolution Correction

## Corrected workflow

1. Generate a completely empty environment plate with no people, creatures, or
   supernatural silhouettes.
2. Treat the environment as a 384 × 216 native pixel-art canvas.
3. Reduce the empty environment to 14 flat colors without dithering.
4. Create separate approved redraws for the Possessed Fishmonger, Drowned
   Net-Caster, and Rain Apparition.
5. Prepare 96 × 96 transparent mockup cutouts from those redraws.
6. Composite the exact cutouts over the native environment. Do not ask the
   image-generation model to recreate characters inside the scene.

## Empty environment prompt

Create a character-free 16:9 side-view environment plate for a gothic
Metroidvania. The Drowned Market is a flooded Philippine riverside marketplace
during a supernatural monsoon. It must resemble an actual playable
low-resolution game room rather than cinematic concept art.

Work as though the environment were authored directly at 384 × 216. Use coarse,
consistent square pixels, large connected clusters, stepped silhouettes, and
approximately 14 flat colors.

A raised boardwalk forms the main combat floor and contains one short jumpable
gap. A simple fish stall stands at left with one cutting table, two large
baskets, a hanging scale, and three hooks. A collapsed stall stands at right
with three large hanging traps, two jars, and one awning platform. Three simple
stilt-house silhouettes, two narrow boats, and three distant roofs occupy the
background. Floodwater fills the lower quarter with a few broad reflections and
ripple rings. Rain appears only as sparse, deliberate vertical clusters.

Use warm near-black, charcoal, rain-dark brown, muted wood, dull beige, slate
water, and tiny dull-amber navigation lights. Avoid characters, ghosts,
creatures, green atmosphere, painterly rendering, anti-aliasing, gradients,
blur, bloom, fog, smooth water, detailed roof tiles, individual wood grain,
dense rain, debris carpets, microtexture, UI, text, labels, and watermarks.

## Mockup note

The 96 × 96 cutouts and 384 × 216 populated image are scene-validation assets,
not finalized production sprites or a final tileset.
