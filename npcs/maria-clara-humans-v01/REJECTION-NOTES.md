# Maria Clara Humans v01 — Rejection Notes

Last updated: 2026-07-30

## Rejected set

The complete ten-character Maria Clara human NPC pass is rejected.

## Explicit user feedback

> all rejected, looks cartoony

## What failed

- The faces are too simplified, soft, friendly, and animation-like.
- Expressions and poses read as charming civilian illustrations rather than
  severe adults belonging to the Ashen Provinces.
- Fabric rendering is clean and polished instead of worn, weighty, restrained,
  and materially grounded.
- Several palettes are bright and cheerful enough to intensify the cartoon
  impression.
- The pixel construction is technically crisp but lacks the mature anatomical
  planes, harsh value grouping, gothic severity, and weathered finish of the
  project's strongest references.

## Mandatory correction for any future attempt

- Solve the rendering treatment before exploring more colors or poses.
- Use mature, longer adult proportions and more specific facial planes.
- Use a harsher, lower-key value structure with broader shadow masses.
- Make cloth feel heavier, less pristine, less decorative, and more historically
  worn.
- Keep expressions restrained, serious, weary, guarded, or mournful.
- Avoid cheerful smiles, storybook styling, soft doll-like faces, clean costume
  illustration, bright pastel coordination, and animation-film polish.
- Compare every pilot at full size and 256 pixels with the strongest approved
  severe project references before expanding beyond a small review wave.

These rejected images are negative evidence only. Do not use them as positive
visual references.
