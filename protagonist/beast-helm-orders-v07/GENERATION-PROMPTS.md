# Beast-Helm Orders v07 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per character  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Shared direction

Render one full-body original animal-helm knight centered on uniform warm
charcoal `#171311`. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art with
hard square pixels, large connected clusters, broad material planes, restrained
highlights, and approximately 12–18 meaningful colors.

The helmet may clearly evoke the animal through metal ears, muzzle, beak, brow,
antlers, tusks, scales, or eye geometry. Match the animal through palette,
armor lines, cloth texture, stance, and weapon role. Keep a human armored body;
avoid furry humanoids, taxidermy heads, mascot costumes, scenery, glow, text,
logo, watermark, or recognizable copied game designs.

7. **Red-Fox Scout Knight** — narrow fox-muzzle helm and tall ear plates,
   russet-bronze light armor, cream throat cloth, arming sword and buckler.
8. **Oxblood Bull Gatekeeper** — broad brow helmet with short outward horns,
