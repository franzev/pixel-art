# Demon Knight Legions v07 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per character  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Shared direction

Render one full-body original demon knight centered on uniform warm charcoal
`#171311`. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art with hard
square pixels, large connected clusters, broad material planes, restrained
highlights, and approximately 12–18 meaningful colors.

Keep human-scale lean, medium, or deliberately heavy proportions. Helmets may
use horns, maws, blind masks, crests, and infernal geometry, but the armor must
remain readable and articulated. Vary palettes, armor eras, cloth, weapons, and
ranks. No scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, glowing eyes, text, logo, or
watermark. Never copy a recognizable game design.
