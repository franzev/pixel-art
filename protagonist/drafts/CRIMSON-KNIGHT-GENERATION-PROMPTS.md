# Crimson Knight Player-Character Redraw

**Status:** Draft, non-canonical  
**Current test:** v01  
**Identity reference:** `pixel-art/samples/silver-knight-left-facing-source.png`

The player character was redrawn separately through the built-in image-generation
workflow. The saved Silver Knight source was used only as an identity, palette,
pose, armor, sword, and crimson-fabric reference. It was not edited, traced,
filtered, pixelated, or downscaled.

## v01 direction

Redraw the canonical Crimson Knight from scratch as deliberately chunky
low-resolution game pixel art. Work as though the sprite were authored directly
on a virtual 96 × 96 indexed-palette canvas and enlarged by exact integer
nearest-neighbor scaling.

Preserve the tall 7–7.5-head silhouette, compact rounded sallet, dark weathered
medium plate, long legs, narrow waist, upright three-quarter screen-left stance,
one long straight one-handed sword held low toward screen-left, and one empty
off-hand separated from the hip.

Preserve the defining crimson scarf, short front tabard, and long heavy cape
flowing toward screen-right to the lower calf with three or four broad torn
tails.

Use large connected clusters, hard square pixels, broad armor planes, broad cape
folds, strong negative space, and approximately 12–16 meaningful colors. Use a
uniform warm charcoal background. Avoid gradients, blur, anti-aliasing,
painterly shading, microtexture, tiny rivets, individual chain links, excessive
metal reflections, fragmented cape strands, isolated noise pixels, green,
shields, extra weapons, and copied game-character details.
