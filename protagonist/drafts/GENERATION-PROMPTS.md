# Protagonist Draft Pixel-Art Treatment Tests

**Status:** Draft, non-canonical  
**Selected visual pass:** v02  
**Reference batch:** `enemies/cultists-demons-batch-02/`

Each subject was redrawn separately through the built-in image-generation
workflow. The retained enemy sources were used only as identity, palette, pose,
costume, anatomy, and prop-layout references. They were not edited, filtered,
traced, pixelated, or downscaled.

## Selected v02 shared direction

Redraw the subject from scratch as extremely clean, deliberately chunky
low-resolution game pixel art. Work as though the sprite were authored directly
on a virtual 96 × 96 indexed-palette canvas and enlarged by exact integer
nearest-neighbor scaling. Use one consistent coarse square logical pixel size,
large connected clusters, hard stair-step contours, broad cloth folds, solid
prop shapes, and clear negative space.

Use approximately 12–16 meaningful flat colors including a uniform warm
near-black background. Give each material only a shadow, midtone, and optional
highlight. Preserve the original palette relationships while merging redundant
shades.

Avoid gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, painterly texture, microtexture,
dithering, fragmented clusters, isolated noise pixels, transitional shades,
mixed pixel sizes, green, scenery, floor shadows, text, borders, logos, and
watermarks.

## 01 — Lantern-Faced Novice

Preserve the compact stocky body, oversized square wooden lantern-box head,
centered aged-brass shutter, one narrow amber light slit, charcoal hood, ragged
capelet, broad sleeves, two clasped hands, patched robe, belt tie and simple
weight, two wrapped short legs, and two brown shoes. Reduce the mask to broad
wooden panels and large brass shapes. Use only a few broad robe folds and large
solid patches.

## 06 — Starless Astrologer

Preserve the tall symmetric hooded scholar, narrow aged-brass mask with one black
facial void, charcoal robe, broad pale stole, wrapped shins, and both hands on
side handles. Preserve one thick outer wooden ring, two broad crossed braces,
one thick horizontal front ring, and exactly three black brass-rimmed eclipse
discs at top-center, lower-left, and lower-right. Keep one simple cord and one
brass counterweight. Do not add a staff or replacement orbs.

## 10 — Blind Standard-Bearer

Preserve the tall lean three-quarter stance, pale lower face, broad taupe-grey
blindfold with two left-streaming tails, charcoal and dusty-beige robes, short
torn mantle, wrapped limbs, sash, one brass ritual weight, split skirt, and both

four simple corner finials, three broad torn cords, and one connected ash-grey
eye-and-spiral smoke sigil. The frame is not a cloth flag, and the sigil is not
purple or luminous.
