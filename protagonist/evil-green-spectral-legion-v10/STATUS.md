## Interpretation

The request continues the evil-green skull-faced spectral family established by
the preceding foot-soldier pass.

- Only exposed ghost anatomy and attached body wisps are bright green and
  spectral.
- Armor, clothing, shields, weapons, tack, barding, capes, and other equipment
  remain solid physical materials.
- Weapons use familiar historical forms with ordinary handles and mechanically
  coherent construction.
- Horse knights use one spectral rider on one believable physical horse.

The nine requested concepts are being produced in review waves of no more than
five. Wave 01 contains both bosses, both captains, and the first horse knight.

## Wave 01 quality review

- **Boss identity:** one tall greatsword duelist and one broad shield-wall
  commander read as different boss encounters rather than palette variants.
- **Captain identity:** the poleaxe and longbow silhouettes remain immediately
  distinguishable from each other and from the bosses.
- **Material separation:** green is confined to exposed spectral anatomy and
  attached wisps; all armor, cloth, weapons, shield, horse, and tack remain
  physical.
- **Weapon realism:** plain greatsword, straight arming sword, wooden pavise,
  historical poleaxe, wooden longbow, ordinary arrow, and straight cavalry
  lance remain mechanically understandable at 256 pixels.
- **Mounted anatomy:** the Green-Rein Lancer contains one coherent rider and one
  natural horse with one head, two ears, four legs, four hooves, and one tail.
- **Framing:** all complete silhouettes and equipment tips remain inside square
  sources with usable padding and no scenery or text.
- **Pixel treatment:** connected clusters, broad value masses, and distinct
  silhouettes remain readable in the nearest-neighbor review references.
