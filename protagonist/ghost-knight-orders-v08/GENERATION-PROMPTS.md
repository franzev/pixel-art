# Ghost Knight Orders v08 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per character  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Shared direction

Render one full-body original ghost knight centered on uniform warm charcoal
`#171311`. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art with hard
square pixels, large connected clusters, broad readable material planes,
restrained highlights, and approximately 12–18 meaningful colors.

Make the knight unmistakably spectral through empty visor darkness, missing or
fading armor sections, burial cloth, corrosion, mist-like edges, and impossible
absence inside the harness. Do not make every knight cyan or transparent. Keep
grounded human-scale lean or medium proportions, readable joints, practical
weapons, and varied armor eras. No scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, glowing
eyes, glowing aura, text, logo, watermark, recognizable copied game designs, or
oversized armor.

## Character briefs

1. **Fog-Sallet Wanderer** — long-tailed sallet, incomplete shoulder plate,
   muted pewter and fog-grey cloth, narrow longsword and buckler; lower cloak
   softly dissolves but boots remain readable.
2. **Drowned Bell Knight** — deep bell-shaped helm dripping with dark verdigris,
   sea-green corroded brigandine, torn rope sash, boarding axe and round shield.
3. **Wick-Crown Watcher** — compact kettle helm with cold unlit candle-spike
   crown, soot-white robes over mail, halberd; completely black empty visor.
4. **Gale-Riven Lancer** — wind-bent plume and split plate skirts, storm-grey
   fitted plate, desaturated blue streaming scarf, very long spear.
5. **Mourning-Veil Duelist** — smooth featureless mask under translucent black
   mourning veil, silver-charcoal slim plate, rapier and small shield.
6. **Barrow-Chain Warden** — old nasal helm, burial-brown lamellar, loose
   spectral chain loops passing through missing torso sections, flanged mace
   and coffin-plank shield.
7. **Frost-Rime Templar** — square crusader helm rimed in chalky frost, pale
   steel and faded lilac cloth, two-handed warhammer; no blue glow.
8. **Hollow Banner Knight** — narrow visor and empty abdominal gap between
   chest and fauld, faded ochre-black armor, banner-halberd and torn pennant.
9. **Ashen Pilgrim Guard** — pointed chapel-de-fer, smoke-grey scale armor,
   off-white pilgrim mantle, staff-spear and almond shield; edges shed dry ash.
10. **Echo-Crown Marshal** — broken ring crown mounted around a close helm,
    muted violet and antique silver command armor, asymmetric spectral cape,
    longsword and command baton.
