# Holy Knight Helmet Orders v03 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per knight
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Shared direction

Create original full-body game-character concepts on a warm charcoal `#171311`
presentation background. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution pixel art,
authored as though on a native 96 × 96 indexed-palette grid, with hard square
pixels, large connected clusters, broad material planes, restrained highlights,
and approximately 12–18 meaningful colors.

Use the grim weight, weathered materials, silhouette variety, and believable
armor layering associated with dark medieval action RPGs. Do not copy or closely
resemble any recognizable Dark Souls or other game armor set, helmet, weapon,
insignia, costume, or character silhouette.

No green, scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, text, logo, watermark, wings,
oversized fantasy horns, glowing eyes, or generic palette-swap armor.

The knight stands rigidly front-three-quarter with both hands resting on the pommel of a wide two-handed execution sword planted point-down.
