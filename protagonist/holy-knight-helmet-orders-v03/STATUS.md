## Helmet and armor cohesion review

- **Mitre-Helm Confessor:** the twin-lobed head is repeated by vertical grille,
  chest ribs, coat divisions, and planted execution sword.
- **Caged Tribunal Knight:** the wide brim and face cage lead into a broad
  capelet, brigandine body, book reliquary, and upright halberd.
- **Mirror-Face Examiner:** the blank convex mask is supported by smooth curved
  pauldrons and polished fitted plate; hanging testimony seals provide the
  inquisitorial prop language.
- **Hounskull Questioner:** every major line points forward with the visor,
  fluted torso, pointed limbs, oath strips, and poleaxe.
- **Cabasset Chapel Judge:** the tall Iberian-derived crown is paired with a
  grille, compact cuirass, riding coat, straight shoulder cloak, and partisan.
- **Long-Tail Sallet Marshal:** the rear helmet tail is echoed by swept fluting,
  rearward half-cape, long estoc, and tapered shield.
- **Barbute Scripture Guard:** the Y-shaped face, banded torso, scale sleeves,
  flat banner-cape, spear, and book buckler form a coherent scripture order.
- **Bellows-Visor Reliquary:** the horizontal face ribs repeat across the gorget,
  torso, arms, knees, pick, narrow pavise, and mechanical casket.

The set passes the helmet-led design requirement. Each body and equipment system
looks constructed for its helmet rather than placed beneath it afterward.
