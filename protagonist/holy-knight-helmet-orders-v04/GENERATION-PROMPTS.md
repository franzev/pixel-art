# Holy Knight Helmet Orders v04 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per knight
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Originality contract

Use only high-level inspiration from grim medieval action-RPG armor: physical
weight, weathered materials, coherent layering, and bold helmet silhouettes.
Every helmet, armor assembly, cloth treatment, weapon, insignia, and body shape
must be an original construction. Never reproduce or closely resemble a
recognizable Dark Souls or other game design.

## Shared presentation

One full-body character on uniform warm charcoal `#171311`. Deliberately chunky
low-resolution game pixel art, authored as if on a native 96 × 96
indexed-palette grid: hard square pixels, large connected clusters, broad
material planes, restrained highlights, and approximately 12–18 meaningful
colors.

Avoid green, scenery, floor planes, cast shadows, text, logos, watermarks,
glowing eyes, giant fantasy horns, and generic palette-swap armor.
