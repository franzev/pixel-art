## Review

- The helmets remain readable as eight separate constructions at reference size:
  wedge, banded dome, crested peak, long nasal, shutter cube, book spine,
  rosette disc, and chapel brim.
- Each helmet drives a matching body architecture rather than a color swap:
  siege plate, horizontal bands, cavalry half-armor, lamellar and mail,
  vertical columns, layered pages, circular barrel plate, and monastic cloth.
- Weapon profiles alternate across lance-hammer, mace and ledger shield,
  partisan, spear and round shield, great mace, pole-sword, maul and round
  shield, and staff-mace.
- The batch was generated from original text briefs without visual references
  to existing games. Its action-RPG influence is limited to weight, weathering,
  restrained palettes, and strong silhouette design.

## Production note

These are independent concept references, not animation-compatible skins and not
native 96 × 96 sprites.
