# Holy Knight Realism Pass v05 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per knight
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Shared direction

Create original grim medieval holy knights with plausible human proportions and
credible armor mechanics. Use only general functional principles from surviving
armor: close-fitting bowls, side-pivoted visors and bevors, catches, sight slots,
breathing holes, articulated nape plates, fitted cuirasses, and joint clearance.
Do not reproduce a particular museum object or recognizable game design.

Render one full-body character on uniform warm charcoal `#171311` as deliberately
chunky low-resolution pixel art: hard square pixels, large connected clusters,
broad material planes, restrained highlights, and roughly 12–18 meaningful
colors. Every character must be lean or medium-built rather than massive.

## 01 — Pivot-Visor Penitent

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original game character concept
Primary request: Create the Pivot-Visor Penitent, a mechanically believable medium-build holy knight. Use a close-fitting rounded helmet with a modest wedge-shaped upper visor and separate chin bevor rotating on shared side pivots. Include two narrow horizontal sights, small breaths near the mouth, a lifting peg, a right-side catch, and three shallow articulated nape lames. The wearer must have visible neck clearance. Match it with a fitted breastplate, two fauld lames, three narrow tassets, modest spaulders, mail at the armpits, articulated elbows, slim greaves, and a short weathered half-cape fixed to one shoulder. Equip both hands with a practical human-scale poleaxe about the knight's height.
Silhouette target: compact sloped visor, fitted torso, one short half-cape, narrow legs, upright poleaxe.
Color palette: dark steel, charcoal mail, dirty ivory cape, oxblood leather, restrained brass.
Constraints: realistic human proportions; credible joints and fastenings; feet and complete weapon visible; original design only.
Avoid: torso-mounted helmet, frog-mouth shell, giant shoulders, enormous skirt, tank build, fantasy spikes, copied historical artifact, copied game armor, green, scenery, glow, gradients, text, logo, watermark.
```
