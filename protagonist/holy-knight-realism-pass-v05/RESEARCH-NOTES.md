# Functional Armor Research Notes

These notes informed mechanical constraints only. No museum image was supplied
to image generation and no individual artifact was copied.

- The Metropolitan Museum of Art describes field close helmets as using a visor
  and lower-face bevor rotating on shared side pivots.
  <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/26428>
- A second Met close helmet records the same characteristic and provides a
  useful real-world scale: 27.9 cm high and 2.495 kg.
  <https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/23234>
- The Wallace Collection describes practical visor details including sights,
  breaths, a lifting peg, notches, and a spring catch.
  <https://wallacelive.wallacecollection.org/eMP/eMuseumPlus?module=collection&objectId=60517&service=ExternalInterface>
- The Met's helmet overview emphasizes the real design balance between defense,
  sight, ventilation, and comfort.
  <https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2000/european-helmets>
