# Holy Knight Realism Pass v05 — Status

## Goal

Replace the five least plausible or excessively bulky v04 concepts with
mechanically wearable, human-proportioned holy knights.

## Functional rules

- Helmet mass stays close to the head instead of becoming a box or torso shell.
- Visors and bevors use visible side pivots, catches, sight lines, and breaths.
- Gorgets and nape lames preserve neck clearance.
- Plate follows the body with articulated gaps at shoulders, elbows, waist, and
  knees.
- Weapons remain human-scale and visibly grippable.
- Builds are lean or medium; no giant pauldrons, spherical torsos, or tank feet.
