## Production note

These outputs are concept and review references. They are not automatically
native 96 × 96 game sprites. Approved designs should later be rebuilt directly
at native resolution.

## Review

All six drafts preserve the tall left-facing knight, one low sword, empty
off-hand, long trailing cape, warm-charcoal presentation, and readable chunky
pixel treatment. The palette families separate cleanly in the review sheet.

- **Ivory Vow:** clearest orthodox holy-knight read and strongest bright option.
- **Bell-Sanctum:** bell identity is immediately readable; the shoulder badge is
  larger than originally planned.
- **Moon-Salt Warden:** strongest exorcist identity and clearest cool palette.
- **Ember Reliquary:** chest reliquary reads cleanly without becoming infernal.
- **Floodlight Hospitaller:** lantern preserves the empty hand, but would require
  its own secondary-motion treatment in animation.
- **Broken Halo Exile:** strongest narrative silhouette; the halo extends the
  head footprint and therefore is not a pure palette-swap skin.

The sources use warm dark presentation backgrounds with slight generated tonal
variation. They are suitable as draft references, not as transparent production
sprites.
