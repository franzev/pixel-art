# Holy Knight Variations v02

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per distinct character
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`
**Status:** Prompts prepared for generation

## Revised direction

The v01 designs were too tightly locked to one player-character silhouette. This
batch instead treats each knight as a member of a different sacred order with a
different helmet, armor construction, cloth treatment, weapon, pose, body mass,
and outer silhouette.

The collection takes high-level inspiration from the grim medieval mood,
believable armor layering, solemn weight, weathered materials, and silhouette
variety of dark medieval action-RPGs. Every design must remain original. Do not
reproduce a recognizable armor set, helmet, weapon, insignia, costume, or
character silhouette from Dark Souls or any other game.

## Shared presentation

- one full-body character, alone
- square composition with the complete weapon visible
- dark warm-charcoal `#171311` presentation background
- deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art
- authored as though on a native 96 × 96 indexed-palette grid
- large connected square-pixel clusters and broad readable material planes
- approximately 12–18 meaningful colors
- no text, logo, watermark, scenery, floor plane, or cast shadow
- no green

Unlike v01, there is no shared helmet, cape, armor, weapon, stance, or body
silhouette.

## 01 — Bell-Helm Judicator

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original game character concept
Primary request: Create the Bell-Helm Judicator, a completely original holy knight from a grim medieval action-RPG world.
Scene/backdrop: a uniform warm charcoal #171311 presentation background with no scenery, floor plane, texture, aura, or cast shadow.
Subject: one full-body top-heavy knight in a square, braced stance. Give the knight a broad arched great helm shaped by overlapping iron plates like the mouth of an old chapel bell, with a low shadowed visor and no face visible. Build the armor from massive mismatched pauldrons, a short barrel-shaped cuirass, thick articulated forearms, layered hip plates, and sturdy greaves. The left pauldron is much larger and carries a single worn bronze clapper emblem. Replace the long cape with a short square black-and-oxblood mantle ending at the waist, split into two heavy panels. Equip both hands with a long iron polehammer held diagonally across the body, its compact hammer head combining one blunt face and one narrow beak.
Silhouette target: broad shoulders, compact torso, planted legs, large arched helmet, short mantle, and one long polearm diagonal. It must not resemble the Crimson Knight silhouette.
Style/medium: deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art, as if drawn directly on a native 96 × 96 indexed-palette grid; hard square pixels, large connected clusters, broad plate planes, restrained highlights, approximately 12–18 colors.
Lighting/mood: oppressive sacred authority, heavy and solemn, weathered rather than ornate.
Color palette: soot-black iron, dark gunmetal, worn bronze, oxblood cloth, bone-grey edge highlights.
Constraints: original design only; full body and complete polehammer visible; one character; no shield; no sword; no long windblown cape; no rounded sallet.
Avoid: copying or closely resembling any recognizable Dark Souls or other game armor set; onion-shaped armor; stone armor; cathedral scenery; crosses; crown; wings; horns; plume; glowing eyes; green; anti-aliasing; blur; gradients; painterly detail; microtexture; text; logo; watermark.
```
