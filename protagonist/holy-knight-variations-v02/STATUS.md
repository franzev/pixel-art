# Holy Knight Variations v02 — Status

## Goal

Replace the palette-swap feeling of v01 with six independently designed,
original holy knights. Helmet geometry, armor construction, cloth silhouette,
weapon, stance, and body mass must all differ.

## Concepts

1. Bell-Helm Judicator — top-heavy polehammer knight
2. Ivory Processional — tall hooded processional greatblade
3. Salt Bastion — short shield-dominant defensive knight
4. Moon-Thorn Lancer — long-limbed asymmetrical spear knight
5. Ash-Reliquary Pilgrim — gaunt robed censer wanderer
6. Broken-Halo Greatsword — asymmetrical halo-ring exile

## Silhouette-diversity review

The six designs now read as different characters before color is considered:

- **Bell-Helm Judicator:** widest upper body; arched bell helm, giant asymmetric
  pauldron, short skirted mantle, and horizontal polehammer.
- **Ivory Processional:** narrowest and most vertical; hooded gate visor,
  floor-length processional cloak, and planted two-handed sword.
- **Salt Bastion:** shortest and broadest; low dome helm, exposed scale armor,
  no cape, enormous doorway shield, and mace.
- **Moon-Thorn Lancer:** longest limbs and most open negative space; swept helmet,
  asymmetric shoulders, diagonal half-cape, and full-frame spear.
- **Ash-Reliquary Pilgrim:** stooped robed profile; perforated mask, ragged shawl,
  mismatched legs, hanging reliquaries, hooked staff, and censer.
- **Broken-Halo Greatsword:** tall asymmetric exile; smooth sealed mask,
  back-mounted fractured ring, segmented coat panels, and shoulder-carried
  greatsword.

The set passes the requested variation test across helmet, armor construction,
cloth/cape treatment, weapon, stance, body mass, and overall silhouette.
