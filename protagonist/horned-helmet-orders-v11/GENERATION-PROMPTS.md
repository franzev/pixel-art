# Horned Helmet Orders v11 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per character  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`  
**Design contract:** `../KNIGHT-ART-DIRECTION-SPEC.md`

## Shared direction

Render one full-body original horned-helmet knight centered on uniform warm
charcoal `#171311`. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art with
hard square pixels, large connected clusters, broad readable material planes,
controlled edge stepping, restrained highlights, and approximately 12–18
meaningful colors.

Horns are forged or carved helmet fittings mounted through visible reinforced
temple or crown plates. Keep the helmet skull-sized with functional sight,
breathing, and neck clearance. Horn scale must remain balanced and no wider
than necessary for the silhouette. Echo horn direction subtly through armor,
cloth, shield, and weapon lines without turning the knight into an animal
costume.

Keep lean or human-medium proportions, except one role-justified sturdy
gatekeeper. Use articulated shoulders, elbows, waist, knees, and ankles,
stable poses, and practical human-scale weapons with believable grips.

No scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, glow, text, logo, watermark, smooth
painterly rendering, tiny noisy pixels, giant pauldrons, spherical torso, tank
feet, biological horn growth through flesh, exposed monster anatomy, mascot
treatment, random spikes, excessive ornament, or recognizable copied armor.

## Character briefs

1. **Ramcoil Justiciar** — medium judicial knight with compact forged horns
   curling backward in near-circles tight to the temples; close visor with
   horizontal sight and breaths, blackened-silver fitted plate, short ivory
   capelet, rust-red ties, practical polehammer, and tall narrow shield. Curved
   horn rhythm repeats in shield corners and elbow lames.
2. **Ibex-Ridge Lancer** — tall lean lancer whose paired ribbed horns sweep a
   long distance backward along the head and nape rather than upward; skull-sized
   burgonet, marsh-bronze cavalry half-plate over mail, indigo split riding
   coat, brown straps, very long human-scale lance, no shield. Rearward armor
   fluting supports the horn direction.
3. **Oxblood Bull Gatekeeper** — role-justified sturdy but human-scale guard
   with short thick horns projecting laterally from reinforced brow plates,
   low close helm, dark-brass coat of plates, square oxblood mantle, compact
   flanged mace, and tall rectangular door shield. Stable defensive silhouette,
   no giant shoulders or literal bull face.
4. **Spiral-Antelope Duelist** — very lean duelist with two slender upright
   horns carrying a subtle forged twist and slight backward lean; narrow armet,
   pale-steel and smoke-teal lacquered harness, plum cords, no cape, long estoc,
   and small faceted buckler. Vertical horn lines continue through narrow torso
   and leg armor.
5. **Broken-Horn Apostate** — medium field knight with low forward-curving horn
   fittings, the right horn intact and the left broken close to its reinforced
   base; green-black fluted plate, ash-red half-cape, worn old-iron edges,
   longsword, and modest round shield. Asymmetry repeats in cape and shoulder
   protection without demonic anatomy or glow.
