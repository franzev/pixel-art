# Lost Orders Quintet v10 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per character  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`  
**Design contract:** `../KNIGHT-ART-DIRECTION-SPEC.md`

## Shared direction

Render one full-body original dark-fantasy knight centered on uniform warm
charcoal `#171311`. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art with
hard square pixels, large connected clusters, broad readable material planes,
controlled edge stepping, restrained highlights, and approximately 12–18
meaningful colors.

The knight must have a readable skull-sized helmet, visible neck clearance,
articulated shoulders, elbows, waist, knees, and ankles, a stable stance, and
human-scale equipment with believable grip spacing. Keep lean or human-medium
proportions. The helmet's geometry must visibly guide the torso, cloth, shield,
and weapon.

No scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, glow, text, logo, watermark, smooth
painterly rendering, tiny noisy pixels, giant pauldrons, spherical torso,
oversized helmet or weapon, random spikes, excessive ornament, or recognizable
copied game armor.

## Character briefs

1. **Astrolabe Surveyor** — lean knight in midnight-blue blackened fitted plate;
   a close sallet surrounded by one incomplete old-brass meridian ring mounted
   tightly at the temples, with functional sight slit and breaths; narrow ochre
   mantle marked only by stitched geometric seams, long practical partisan, and
   tapered kite shield with shallow surveying-grid grooves. The ring is
   mechanical equipment, not a glowing halo.
2. **Funeral-Cicada Bailiff** — medium knight with a close burgonet whose brow,
   cheek vents, and short rear plates abstract a cicada shell; dark bronze and
   green-black brigandine, plum throat cloth, short split capelet shaped like
   folded wing cases without biological wings; hooked mace and tall narrow
   shield. Restrained insect geometry, no mascot treatment.
3. **Saltglass Duelist** — very lean court duelist with a narrow armet using
   pale smoke-teal enamel visor slits like frosted glass while remaining fully
   opaque and protective; ivory and dark-teal fitted harness with garnet ties,
   no cape, long estoc, and a small crescent buckler. Clean negative space and
   minimal ornament.
4. **Reed-Crown Marsh Lancer** — lean regional lancer with a skull-sized close
   helm carrying a balanced row of thin riveted iron fins like bundled reeds;
   marsh-bronze cavalry half-plate over mail, moss-green straps, faded blue split
   woven coat, very long human-scale lance, no shield. Reeds are translated into
   metal and textile geometry, never literal plants.
5. **Black Orchard Castellan** — medium castle commander with a close black
   helm using a restrained branching lattice as its face grille, readable sight
   openings, oxblood and black coat of plates, ash-grey square half-mantle,
   practical poleaxe, and tall ash-wood shield banded in black iron. Branch
   geometry repeats subtly in shield braces and fastening lines, not spikes.
