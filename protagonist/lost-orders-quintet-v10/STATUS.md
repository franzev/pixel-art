# Lost Orders Quintet v10 — Status

## Goal

Create five surprising original knights using unusual but wearable helmet-led
construction. Every design must differ in silhouette, armor family, cloth,
weapon doctrine, material story, and implied culture while following the
project's active knight art-direction specification.

## Review

- **Silhouettes:** ringed surveyor with gridded kite shield, compact
  insect-shell bailiff, pale open duelist, fin-crowned lancer, and branching
  grille castellan remain distinct at 256 × 256.
- **Proportions:** all five are lean or human-medium with visible waists,
  skull-sized helmets, restrained shoulders, narrow leg harnesses, and stable
  feet.
- **Wearability:** sight lines and neck clearance remain plausible; joints and
  weapon grips are readable; no oversized weapons or blocked articulation.
- **Helmet-to-body coherence:** ring and grid geometry, folded shell and
  capelet, saltglass enamel and clean harness, reed fins and split cloth, and
  branch lattice and shield braces each repeat deliberately through the body.
- **Material and palette range:** midnight steel and brass, bronze and plum,
  ivory and smoke teal, marsh bronze and faded blue, and black iron with
  oxblood produce five separate cultures rather than recolors.
- **Hard gates:** no bulk, spherical torsos, giant pauldrons, random spikes,
  glow, mascot treatment, smooth rendering, or recognizable copied armor.
- **Retention:** all five full generated sources are retained.
