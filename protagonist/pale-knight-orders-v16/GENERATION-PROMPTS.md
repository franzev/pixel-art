# Generation Prompts — Pale Knight Orders v16

## Shared art direction

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: full-body game character concept / pixel-art sprite source

Create one original light-colored medieval-fantasy knight as a polished production-quality pixel-art character. Use the supplied review sheets only as broad art-direction references: Image 1 for grounded wearable knight proportions and restrained rendering; Image 2 for inventive but plausible sacred-order helmets and readable pale cloth/metal separation; Image 3 for the accepted compact silhouettes and unusual gravity-driven cape construction. Do not reproduce any referenced figure, helmet, costume, silhouette, heraldry, or pose.

Exactly one adult knight, centered, fully visible from helmet to feet with every cape end and weapon inside the frame. Lean or medium human build, never bulky, gigantic, or top-heavy. Wearable armor with plausible plate thickness, articulated joints, restrained pauldrons, practical weapon scale, and a stable upright three-quarter frontal stance. Full body coverage and nonsexualized design; no nudity, cleavage, lingerie armor, sculpted breast cups, exposed thighs, high heels, or pin-up pose.

The knight must read as light-colored without becoming bright white, glowing, celestial, or pristine. Use aged pale materials with visible wear, subtle value separation, and one restrained secondary hue. Preserve dark gaps at joints and under overlapping plates so the silhouette remains readable.

ABSOLUTELY NO WINGS OF ANY KIND: no biological wings, feathered wings, bat wings, mechanical wings, decorative wing frames, wing ornaments, wing-like energy, or cape panels spreading outward like wings. The unusual cape must be clearly constructed from cloth, cord, rings, chains, and small weights; physically attached, gravity-driven, and close to the body. It must not hide the hands, armor joints, legs, or primary weapon.

Crisp hand-authored dark-fantasy pixel art, clean clusters and selective dithering, readable at game-sprite scale, roughly 12–18 meaningful colors, restrained highlights, no smooth painterly rendering or anti-aliased vector edges. Solid warm charcoal background `#171311`. No scenery, architecture, particles, magic aura, bloom, halo, cast shadow, floor plane, border, label, text, logo, or watermark.

## 01 — Porcelain Vowkeeper

Adult female knight. Use fitted steel plate coated in worn warm-ivory enamel, with small chips revealing dark metal beneath, fine hairline repair seams picked out in muted old gold, and smoke-blue linen at the joints. The armor remains functional and nonsexualized. Design a compact fluted lantern-like close helm: a low polygonal crown, narrow eye slit, and small perforated bevor, with no horns, halo, or crest.

Her defining cape begins between the shoulder blades as eight narrow smoke-blue linen strips. Each strip is tightly plaited, then neighboring strips are joined at intervals by tiny aged-gold rings so the whole cape forms one narrow articulated textile column. It hangs downward behind one hip and ends in a small ivory ceramic weight. It never spreads laterally. She carries a straight longsword and a slim teardrop shield with abstract geometric scoring, no real-world religious symbol.

## 02 — Salt-Ring Warden

Adult male knight. Use weathered pale silver plate with chalky salt bloom around rivets, sand-colored leather straps, and off-white coarse linen. Design a low-brimmed sallet with a compact perforated bevor and one narrow dark visor line; no plume or ornament. Armor is medium-light field equipment with small shoulder plates and practical layered faulds.

His defining cape is a short circular shoulder mantle of thick chalk linen. The entire lower edge is turned around a single continuous rope sewn into the hem, and that rope passes through four modest bronze gathering rings, pulling the cloth into controlled rounded scallops that hang close to the torso. The silhouette is compact and downward, never flared or wing-like. He holds a realistic ash-shaft spear and a small round shield faced in pale rawhide.

## 03 — Celadon Fold Duelist

Adult female knight. Use lean pale-celadon enameled plate with worn cream edges, oxidized silver hardware, and dark gray-green textile only in the joint gaps. Design a smooth beaked armet with a short practical point, segmented cheek plates, fine ventilation holes, and a low rear neck guard; no animal face or exaggerated beak.

Her defining cape is one rectangular length of heavy cream cloth attached vertically along the left shoulder blade and permanently accordion-pleated into deep alternating folds. It descends close behind the body as one narrow zigzag column, secured by three small horizontal silver bars and ending just below the knee. It is structural cloth, not floating paper, and never spreads sideways. She carries a realistic estoc and compact circular buckler. Reserved, mobile duelist silhouette.

## 04 — Pearl Bell Castellan

Adult male knight. Use pearl-gray polished steel with subdued champagne-brass edging, pale amber quilted cloth, and charcoal mail in the gaps. Design a cylindrical close helm with a shallow domed top, one narrow horizontal visor, restrained vertical breathing slots, and a broad functional gorget. No crown, horns, or oversize crest.

His defining cape consists of three separate short circular mantle tiers attached down the upper back. Each lower tier is slightly narrower and longer than the one above, creating a tapered stacked-bell profile that remains close to the spine. Every tier has a thin weighted champagne-brass chain sewn into its lower edge so the fabric falls vertically instead of flaring. He holds a compact flanged mace and a shoulder-height elongated kite shield. Authoritative medium build, not bulky.

## 05 — Rose-Ash Procession Knight

Adult female knight. Use bone-white articulated plate with dusty-rose leather panels, pale pewter fittings, and dark ash-gray mail at the joints. Design a low mitre-profile processional close helm whose ridged crown is forged as part of the helmet, modest in height and mechanically plausible; narrow crosswise visor slots without any real-world emblem.

Her defining cape starts as a narrow pale-pewter chain lattice laid diagonally across the upper back. Long strips of dusty-rose cloth are threaded through the lattice like a woven textile, then gather into exactly two unequal vertical tails behind opposite hips. Each tail is narrow, heavy, and finished with a small rectangular bone-white metal weight. Nothing projects sideways. She grips a compact practical poleaxe upright in both hands. Ceremonial but combat-ready, lean-medium silhouette.
