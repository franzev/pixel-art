# Pale Knight Orders v16

Status: Complete

## Hard gates

- Exactly five original, full-body light-colored knights.
- Mixed adult men and women.
- Light palettes must come from distinct materials, weathering, and secondary colors—not simple white recolors.
- Absolutely no wings: biological, feathered, bat-like, mechanical, decorative, or cape panels shaped like wings.
- Every knight has a visibly different helmet, armor construction, cape system, weapon loadout, and silhouette.
- Lean or medium human proportions; no oversized tank armor.
- Wearable full-coverage armor, practical joints, believable weapon scale.
- No nudity, cleavage, lingerie armor, sculpted breast cups, high heels, or pin-up poses.
- Warm charcoal `#171311` background, no scenery, floor, cast shadow, text, border, or glow.
