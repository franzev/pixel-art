## Review

- The faction is unified by black iron, soot, oxblood, sallow cloth, ritual
  fasteners, and severe heraldry rather than identical armor.
- Helmet profiles include sloped close helm, flared bevor, veiled burgonet,
  hooked-brow close helm, segmented sallet, ribbed armet, kettle hat, oval
  field helm, open sallet, and smooth command helm.
- Body systems range from fitted plate and brigandine to lamellar, padded
  infantry gear, light mail, and noble field armor.
- Builds remain lean or medium. The Bell-Toll Justiciar and Grave-Tithe Lancer
  are intentionally sturdier, but neither uses fantasy tank proportions.
- All ten were generated from original text briefs without visual game
  references. Every generated source has been retained.
