## Shared direction

Render one full-body original adult female holy inquisitor centered on uniform
warm charcoal `#171311`. Match the approved Sacred Tribunal's severe
dark-fantasy pixel-art language: pale liturgical cloth, dark or aged metal,
compact ecclesiastical helmets, judicial equipment, disciplined posture, hard
square pixels, large connected clusters, broad readable planes, controlled
edge stepping, restrained highlights, and approximately 12–18 meaningful
colors.

The knight is unambiguously an adult woman with realistically proportioned
female anatomy beneath practical armor. Use subtly narrower shoulders and
appropriately fitted waist and hip articulation while preserving strength,
mobility, and full coverage. Do not use sculpted breast cups, cleavage,
corseted waists, exposed thighs, high heels, pin-up poses, or sexualized armor.

Keep the helmet skull-sized with functional sight, breaths, pivots or catches,
and neck clearance. Use stable feet, articulated shoulders, elbows, waist,
knees, and ankles, plus human-scale weapons with believable grips.

No scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, glow, giant crosses, readable scripture,
text, logo, watermark, excessive gold, giant headgear, bulky tank bodies,
smooth painterly rendering, tiny noisy pixels, random spikes, exposed monster
anatomy, or recognizable copied game armor.
