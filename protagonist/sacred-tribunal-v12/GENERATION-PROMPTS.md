# Sacred Tribunal v12 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per character  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`  
**Design contract:** `../KNIGHT-ART-DIRECTION-SPEC.md`

## Shared direction

Render one full-body original holy inquisitor knight centered on uniform warm
charcoal `#171311`. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art with
hard square pixels, large connected clusters, broad readable material planes,
controlled edge stepping, restrained highlights, and approximately 12–18
meaningful colors.

Make the sacred tribunal identity readable through compact ecclesiastical
helmet geometry, functional visor mechanisms, pale liturgical cloth, severe
dark metal, judicial equipment, restrained seal or binding details, and an
upright disciplined posture. Keep helmet shells skull-sized with sight,
breaths, pivots or catches, and neck clearance.

Use lean or human-medium proportions with visible waists, articulated joints,
stable feet, and practical human-scale weapons. The helmet must guide the
armor, cloth, shield, and weapon geometry.

No scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, glow, giant crosses, readable scripture,
text, logo, watermark, smooth painterly rendering, tiny noisy pixels, oversized
mitres, giant pauldrons, spherical torsos, tank feet, excessive gold, random
spikes, exposed monsters, or recognizable copied game armor.

## Character briefs

1. **Three-Lancet Examiner** — lean examiner with a close pale-steel helm whose
   faceplate contains three narrow arched sight-and-breath openings backed by
   dark mesh; fitted plate, charcoal joints, muted-gold fasteners, narrow black
   stole, long estoc, and tapered shield. Triple vertical rhythm continues down
   breastplate and shield without literal cathedral architecture.
2. **Split-Mitre Judicator** — medium judicial knight with a skull-sized armet
   carrying two low folded ridges like a compact split mitre; visible visor
   pivot and side catch, blackened-silver plate, short ivory capelet, oxblood
   ties, practical polehammer, and small round buckler. No tall bishop hat.
3. **Chain-Veil Questioner** — lean field inquisitor wearing a shallow chapel
   hat over a close bevor and fine mail face veil, leaving no visible face;
   old-brass brigandine and mail, olive-black coat, cream shoulder veil,
   human-scale staff-spear, and modest round shield. Layered cloth and chain
   remain practical, with no giant brim.
4. **Lockplate Confessor** — medium confessor with a square close helm whose
   visor uses three stacked horizontal locking bars and a clear side release;
   dark-steel coat of plates, split burgundy tabard, ash-grey collar, compact
   flanged mace, and tall rectangular shield. Horizontal locking rhythm repeats
   across torso bands and shield braces.
5. **White-Nail Penitent** — gaunt lean penitent with a low conical salt-steel
   helm, vertically pierced visor, and a restrained crown of short blunt
   rivet-like nail studs; mail and narrow plate beneath ash-white robe panels,
   faded-violet binding cords, practical two-handed great mace, no shield.
   Severe and holy without literal wounds, spikes, or glow.
