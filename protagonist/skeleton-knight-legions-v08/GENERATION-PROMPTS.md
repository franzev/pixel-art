# Skeleton Knight Legions v08 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per character  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Shared direction

Render one full-body original skeleton knight centered on uniform warm charcoal
`#171311`. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art with hard
square pixels, large connected clusters, broad readable material planes,
restrained highlights, and approximately 12–18 meaningful colors.

Keep the bony anatomy coherent and human-scale: readable skull, ribcage or limb
bones where the design exposes them, armor fitted to a thin skeletal frame,
natural weapon grip, and stable stance. Vary skull framing, helmet family,
armor era, bone treatment, textiles, palette, and weapon role. No scenery,
floor plane, cast shadow, glowing eyes, magical flames, text, logo, watermark,
recognizable copied game designs, comedy, or oversized armor.

## Character briefs

1. **Ivory Catacomb Legionary** — open-faced cheek-guard helmet framing an
   ivory skull, dark red segmented cuirass and skirt plates, short spear and
   rectangular shield; slim disciplined stance.
2. **Verdigris Crypt Spearman** — pointed nasal helm over a long skull,
   weathered copper scale armor with green verdigris, exposed forearms, leaf
   spear and round shield.
3. **Black-Iron Ossuary Knight** — close black sallet surrounding a visible
   jaw and teeth, fitted Gothic plate with open rib gaps, longsword and heater
   shield.
4. **Red-Cord Bone Retainer** — restrained flared lamellar helmet around a
   pale skull, black iron lamellar tied with dark red cords, layered skirt,
   glaive and small round shield.
5. **Salt-Bone Crusader** — square pale great helm with broken face opening
   exposing one skull side, chalk-white plate and sand cloth, mace and kite
   shield.
6. **Grave-Moss Reaver** — low iron cap and broken brow plate, moss-dark
   brigandine over exposed ribs, ragged green-brown mantle, hooked axe and
   buckler.
7. **Funeral-Brass Bellguard** — tall bell-shaped brass helmet framing a black
   eye-socket skull, dark funeral coat with articulated brass plates, polehammer
   and tall narrow shield.
8. **Porcelain-Skull Duelist** — smooth porcelain-white skull with thin repair
   lines beneath a tiny brow circlet, slim midnight-blue dueling harness, estoc
   and faceted buckler.
9. **Charred Marrow Axeman** — smoke-blackened bones, simple ridged burgonet,
   heat-darkened brown plate and leather, two-handed bearded axe; medium build,
   not huge.
10. **Crowned Necropolis Marshal** — narrow crown integrated into an open
    funerary helm, aged ivory bones, black-gold command plate, shredded purple
    mantle, longsword and tall command shield.
