# Alternative Slayer Concepts 01

**Status:** Draft, non-canonical  
**Purpose:** Explore five player-character power and weapon identities suited to
the retained cultists, demons, ghosts, and folklore creatures.

Each concept was generated separately through the built-in image-generation
workflow.

## Shared direction

Create one complete alternative player-character concept for a 2D gothic
Metroidvania on a uniform warm near-black background. Design the character as
though authored on a virtual 96 × 96 native canvas, using hard square pixels,
large connected clusters, broad material shapes, clean negative space, and
approximately 12–16 meaningful colors.

Keep the character readable at 96 × 96. Avoid detailed illustration that was
pixelated afterward, microtexture, isolated noise pixels, gradients, blur,
anti-aliasing, painterly shading, green backgrounds, extra anatomy, cropped
weapons, text, borders, logos, and watermarks.

Every concept must remain distinct from the Crimson Knight: no combination of
full dark plate, compact sallet, long crimson cape, and straight longsword.

## 01 — Flood-Bell Harpooner

A rain-cloaked spirit hunter with a bronze half-mask, one long rope harpoon, and
one heavy handbell. The bell reveals invisible spirits. The harpoon pins large
enemies, pulls targets, and provides traversal across gaps.

Palette: charcoal, faded indigo, wet slate, weathered rope, aged bronze, iron,
and a tiny pale-cyan ward accent.

## 02 — Salt-Loom Hunter

A masked ward-weaver with one oversized iron loom-shuttle spear, a wooden spool
at the lower back, and one thick salt-white abaca cord. Attacks stitch temporary
lines into floors and walls. Crossing lines binds spirits and interrupts ritual
apparatus.

Palette: warm black, dirty ivory, faded indigo, weathered wood, iron, and
salt-white.

## 03 — Ashglass Pistolier

A mirror-visored duelist carrying exactly one large single-shot hand cannon and
one triangular mirror dagger. Three mirror shards at the belt act as ammunition.
Shots ricochet through placed reflections, reveal disguised enemies, and create
brief reflected firing positions.

Palette: charcoal, soot grey, dusty plum, dark leather, tarnished silver,
gunmetal, and a tiny ice-blue reflection accent.

## 04 — Rootbound Outcast

A grounded slayer with one living dark-wood gauntlet and one broad pruning bolo.
The transformed arm absorbs curse projectiles, stores their accent, and launches
one thick grappling root. The character remains sympathetic and anatomically
human rather than becoming a folkloric monster.

Palette: charcoal, ash brown, dark bark, dirty bone, iron, leather, and a muted
bruised-violet curse seam. No green.

## 05 — Thunder-Jar Breaker

A compact chain fighter carrying exactly three clay jars: two sealed storm jars
on a woven shoulder yoke and one large cracked jar used as a meteor hammer. The
weapon releases close thunder bursts and pulls storm energy from flying demons.

Palette: charcoal, dark clay, faded ochre, weathered leather, iron, aged bronze,
and a tiny pale-blue lightning accent.

## Refined direction for concepts 06–15

These concepts deliberately bend one conventional player-character rule apiece
without abandoning production readability.

Create one complete original supernatural slayer on a uniform warm near-black
background. Work as though the character were authored directly on a virtual
96 × 96 canvas. Use visibly consistent square logical pixels, large connected
clusters, clean stair-step contours, broad folds, and solid weapon silhouettes.

Each concept must satisfy a two-level silhouette test:

1. The body plan and primary weapon remain recognizable as a solid black shape.
2. The supernatural function remains understandable from one large prop and one
   restrained accent effect.

Give the defining prop roughly 25–40 percent of the total silhouette. Use
approximately 12–16 meaningful colors and no more than two or three shades per
material. Prefer one controlled accent family per concept.

Break only one major convention per design: an unusual companion, oversized
tool, stolen enemy mechanism, defensive weapon, strange traversal device, or
nonstandard fighting stance. Preserve correct anatomy unless a transformation
is explicitly central. Avoid a pile of unrelated gimmicks.

Do not combine the Crimson Knight's full plate, compact sallet, straight
longsword, and long crimson cape. Avoid detailed illustration that was
pixelated afterward, microtexture, excessive straps, isolated noise pixels,
dithering, gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, soft edges, painterly shading, mixed
pixel sizes, green backgrounds, cropped props, extra anatomy, text, borders,
logos, and watermarks.

## 06 — Coffin-Nail Fencer

A tall grave duelist using one enormous square iron coffin nail as a thrusting
estoc and one compact claw hammer in the off-hand. Successful counters hammer a
spectral nail through an enemy's shadow, pinning teleporters and flying spirits
to the arena.

Palette: charcoal, burial-cloth grey, rusted iron, weathered leather, dirty bone,
and a tiny cold-white shadow-pin accent.

## 07 — Mourning Parasol

A poised slayer wielding one large funeral parasol with iron blade ribs. Closed,
it acts as a thrusting spear; open, it blocks projectiles, catches supernatural
rain, and provides short controlled glides. The design uses one broad circular
silhouette instead of a conventional shield.

Palette: warm black, faded mourning violet, ash grey, dark wood, tarnished
silver, and a restrained pearl-white rain accent.

## 08 — Shrinebreaker Mason

A broad-shouldered ruin hunter carrying one massive wedge-shaped tuning chisel
as a polearm and one square stoneworker's mallet. Strikes carve temporary weak
points into chapel mimics, bells, armor, doors, and ritual apparatus.

Palette: soot black, quarry grey, dusty ochre, aged iron, weathered leather, and
a tiny ember-orange fracture accent.

## 09 — Hollow Drummer

A compact counter-ritual fighter wearing one large empty frame drum at the waist
and wielding exactly two heavy beaters. Timed beats parry sound attacks, cancel
chants, and send low shockwaves through the floor. The drum doubles as frontal
armor rather than a separate shield.

Palette: charcoal, faded burgundy, stretched dirty hide, dark wood, aged brass,
and a small spectral-white vibration accent.

## 10 — Threshold Thief

A lean burglar-slayer with one narrow empty wooden doorframe strapped vertically
to the back and one hooked iron prybar. The stolen frame creates paired
short-range doorways in walls and enemy shadows. Keep the frame visibly empty
and separate from the body.

Palette: soot black, faded brown, smoke grey, oxidized iron, dirty cloth, and a
small pale-blue threshold accent.

## 11 — Grave-Salt Pugilist

A powerful close-range slayer wearing exactly two oversized ceramic gauntlets
filled with consecrated salt. Punches stamp broad ward marks onto enemies, while
clashing the gauntlets creates a brief anti-spirit barrier. No conventional
weapon is carried.

Palette: charcoal, dark umber, cracked dirty ivory ceramic, muted skin, salt
white, and a tiny moon-blue ward accent.

## 12 — Eclipse Netter

A mobile hunter wielding one crescent casting net weighted by exactly five
silver-black moon hooks and one short harvesting sickle. The net grounds flying
creatures, reveals phase-shifting enemies, and briefly turns hostile projectiles
into suspended stars.

Palette: warm black, slate blue, dark rope, tarnished silver, weathered cloth,
and a restrained pale-ice eclipse accent.

## 13 — Funeral-Kite Duelist

A light aerial slayer carrying one large folded diamond-shaped funeral kite on a
short control bar. Folded, it becomes a bladed shield; deployed, it catches wind,
deflects projectiles, and enables long horizontal glides. Use one broad kite
shape with two short tails, not many ribbons.

Palette: charcoal, ash paper, faded cinnabar, bamboo brown, black iron, and a
tiny ember-gold wind accent.

## 14 — Ashhound Pactkeeper

A lone tracker accompanied by exactly one small four-legged ash-grey spectral
hound. The human carries one short forked boar spear. The hound scents invisible
enemies, retrieves thrown weapons, and briefly occupies the slayer's shadow for
an evasive dash.

Palette: charcoal, faded russet, iron grey, weathered leather, ash white, and a
small pale-cyan spectral accent.

## 15 — Wax-Seal Smith

A heavy ritual craftsperson wielding one long-handled heated seal hammer and one
thick wax-coated forearm guard. Hammer strikes stamp temporary seals that disable
an enemy's signature power; collected magic remelts the guard into new seals.

Palette: soot black, furnace brown, dirty bone, black wax, aged brass, ember
orange, and a restrained ivory seal highlight.
