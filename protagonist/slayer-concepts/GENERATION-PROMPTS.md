# Alternative Slayer Concepts 01

**Status:** Draft, non-canonical  
**Purpose:** Explore five player-character power and weapon identities suited to
the retained cultists, demons, ghosts, and folklore creatures.

Each concept was generated separately through the built-in image-generation
workflow.

## Shared direction

Create one complete alternative player-character concept for a 2D gothic
Metroidvania on a uniform warm near-black background. Design the character as
though authored on a virtual 96 × 96 native canvas, using hard square pixels,
large connected clusters, broad material shapes, clean negative space, and
approximately 12–16 meaningful colors.

Keep the character readable at 96 × 96. Avoid detailed illustration that was
pixelated afterward, microtexture, isolated noise pixels, gradients, blur,
anti-aliasing, painterly shading, green backgrounds, extra anatomy, cropped
weapons, text, borders, logos, and watermarks.

Every concept must remain distinct from the Crimson Knight: no combination of
full dark plate, compact sallet, long crimson cape, and straight longsword.

## 01 — Flood-Bell Harpooner

A rain-cloaked spirit hunter with a bronze half-mask, one long rope harpoon, and
one heavy handbell. The bell reveals invisible spirits. The harpoon pins large
enemies, pulls targets, and provides traversal across gaps.

Palette: charcoal, faded indigo, wet slate, weathered rope, aged bronze, iron,
and a tiny pale-cyan ward accent.

## 02 — Salt-Loom Hunter

A masked ward-weaver with one oversized iron loom-shuttle spear, a wooden spool
at the lower back, and one thick salt-white abaca cord. Attacks stitch temporary
lines into floors and walls. Crossing lines binds spirits and interrupts ritual
apparatus.

Palette: warm black, dirty ivory, faded indigo, weathered wood, iron, and
salt-white.

## 03 — Ashglass Pistolier

A mirror-visored duelist carrying exactly one large single-shot hand cannon and
one triangular mirror dagger. Three mirror shards at the belt act as ammunition.
Shots ricochet through placed reflections, reveal disguised enemies, and create
brief reflected firing positions.

Palette: charcoal, soot grey, dusty plum, dark leather, tarnished silver,
gunmetal, and a tiny ice-blue reflection accent.

## 04 — Rootbound Outcast

A grounded slayer with one living dark-wood gauntlet and one broad pruning bolo.
The transformed arm absorbs curse projectiles, stores their accent, and launches
one thick grappling root. The character remains sympathetic and anatomically
human rather than becoming a folkloric monster.

Palette: charcoal, ash brown, dark bark, dirty bone, iron, leather, and a muted
bruised-violet curse seam. No green.

## 05 — Thunder-Jar Breaker

A compact chain fighter carrying exactly three clay jars: two sealed storm jars
on a woven shoulder yoke and one large cracked jar used as a meteor hammer. The
weapon releases close thunder bursts and pulls storm energy from flying demons.

Palette: charcoal, dark clay, faded ochre, weathered leather, iron, aged bronze,
and a tiny pale-blue lightning accent.
