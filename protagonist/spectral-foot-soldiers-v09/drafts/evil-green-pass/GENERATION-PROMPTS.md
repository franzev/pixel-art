# Spectral Foot Soldiers v09 — Evil-Green Pass

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per character  
**Intent:** Generate from a positive ghost-appearance reference  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`  
**Lifecycle:** Unapproved drafts

## Input image

**Image 1:** `../../../references/possessed-washerwoman-style-reference.png`  
**Role:** Positive visual reference for ghost appearance and pixel treatment
only, not an edit target.

Preserve the reference's deliberate crisp pixel clusters, layered spectral
translucency, dark interior shading, luminous rim pixels, and long trailing
wisps. Do not copy the washerwoman, laundry sheet, pose, costume, second figure,
or silhouette.

## Shared prompt direction

Create one original evil-green ghost foot soldier for the Ashen Provinces, a
side-scrolling Philippine-inflected gothic Metroidvania.

- Square 1:1 canvas with exactly one complete adult humanoid.
- Readable three-quarter view facing screen-left.
- Exactly one head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and two feet.
- Uniform warm near-black `#171311` background.
- No scenery, floor, cast shadow, localized backdrop glow, fog, gradient,
  vignette, frame, text, logo, or watermark.
- Use crisp high-density pixel art with hard square pixels, connected clusters,
  broad readable values, restrained ramps, selective highlights, and consistent
  pixel density.
- No anti-aliasing, blur, painterly gradients, or noisy microtexture.
- The exposed head, neck gaps, hands, and small ankle or joint gaps are the only
  spectral body parts.
- Spectral anatomy uses a malignant deep-emerald to acid-mint ramp, dark
  interiors, concentrated pale-mint edge pixels, and controlled ribbon wisps
  trailing screen-right.
- Armor, clothing, mail, leather, wood, weapons, shields, boots, poles, and
  banners are completely physical, opaque, solid, and non-spectral.
- Physical materials dominate in charcoal, soot-black, aged iron, dull steel,
  dark wood, weathered brown leather, and faded black-grey cloth.
- Green light originates only from exposed ghost anatomy and reflects minimally
  on neighboring physical gear.
- Keep all anatomy, equipment, and wisps inside the frame with generous padding.
- No empty armor, spectral equipment, blue or cyan ghost palette, full-body neon
  fill, extra character, extra anatomy, missing anatomy, fused hands, giant
  weapons, graphic gore, or recognizable copyrighted character design.

## 01. Dread Levy Spearman

Create a cautious advance-and-thrust infantry enemy. This is the only member of
the wave with a visible human apparition-face rather than a skull.

- Face: horrifying, gaunt, possessed adult face with a stretched open mouth,
  sunken luminous eyes, and sharply readable facial planes; terrifying but
  non-graphic.
- Posture: upright diagonal advance, about 75 percent of canvas height.
- Physical armor: open aged-iron skullcap, weathered iron shoulder plates and
  forearm guards, dark quilted jack, knee-length soot-black levy coat, brown
  leather belt and boots.
- Equipment: exactly one human-scale solid ash-wood spear with dull iron head
  and one small battered solid iron buckler strapped to the rear forearm.
- Silhouette: long diagonal spear, small counterweight buckler, trailing wisps.
- Critical lock: human ghost face, not a skull; all armor, coat, spear, buckler,
  belt, and boots remain opaque and physical.

## 02. Grave Shieldman

Create a guarded advance-and-counterattack infantry enemy.

- Face: one unmistakable exposed spectral skull with dark eye sockets, readable
  nose cavity, and jaw; not a living face or mask.
- Posture: broad but human-scale guarded stance, about 75 percent of canvas
  height.
- Physical armor: low open aged-iron kettle helmet, overlapping dull-steel
  shoulder and forearm plates, dark heavy gambeson, short soot-black tabard,
  leather belt and boots.
- Equipment: exactly one large but human-scale battered solid iron round shield
  held forward and one short physical arming sword held low behind it.
- Silhouette: broad round shield mass with the skull visible above it.
- Critical lock: helmet, shield, sword, armor, gambeson, tabard, straps, and
  boots remain opaque and physical.

## 03. Gallows Halberdier

Create a tall reach-control and overhead-strike enemy.

- Face: one unmistakable horrifying spectral skull beneath an open soot-black
  physical hood; not a living face or mask.
- Posture: tall, lean, narrow, upright, slightly hunched at the shoulders but
  not crouched; about 77 percent of canvas height.
- Physical armor: open cloth hood, dull mail collar, asymmetric aged-iron
  shoulder plate, layered forearm armor, dark livery coat with split angular
  knee-length hem, leather belt, wrapped trousers, and boots.
- Equipment: exactly one human-scale physical wooden halberd with dull steel axe
  blade and top spike, held nearly vertical and fully visible.
- Silhouette: strong vertical polearm column with one threatening extended hand.
- Critical lock: exactly two hands are present; one grips the halberd and one is
  extended in a threatening gesture. Hood, coat, mail, armor, halberd, belt,
  trousers, and boots remain opaque and physical.

## 04. Crypt Crossbowman

Create a compact ranged ambusher.

- Face: one unmistakable horrifying spectral skull under a shallow open
  aged-iron cap; not a living face or mask.
- Posture: compact forward-leaning firing stance, anatomically believable, about
  72 percent of canvas height.
- Physical armor: compact dull-steel shoulder plates, soot-black brigandine,
  short asymmetric coat, fitted dark trousers, leather belt, and solid boots.
- Equipment: exactly one human-scale physical wooden crossbow with steel bow
  limbs held horizontally across the chest, plus one closed physical bolt case
  at the rear hip; no loose bolts, shield, or secondary weapon.
- Silhouette: low forward diagonal with a broad horizontal crossbow.
- Critical lock: cap, brigandine, coat, crossbow, bolt case, trousers, and boots
  remain opaque and physical.

## 05. Black Standard Bearer

Create a command-and-rally infantry enemy.

- Face: one unmistakable horrifying spectral skull beneath an open solid
  aged-iron nasal helmet; not a living face or mask.
- Posture: gaunt elderly adult in an upright off-balance marching stance, about
  74 percent of canvas height.
- Physical armor: dull-steel shoulder plates, real dark mail shirt, weathered
  soot-black long surcoat with asymmetrical split hem, brown leather belt and
  boots.
- Equipment: exactly one tall solid wooden standard pole held by both hands,
  one high and one low, with exactly one large blank black-grey physical cloth
  banner extending screen-right.
- Banner: opaque, non-glowing, heavy real-cloth folds, three broad torn tails,
  and no emblem, symbol, lettering, face, handprint, or image.
- Silhouette: open asymmetric flag mass, vertical pole, narrow marching body.
- Critical lock: helmet, mail, shoulder plates, surcoat, pole, banner, belt, and
  boots remain opaque and physical.
