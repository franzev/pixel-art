## Shared generation direction

Create exactly one adult humanoid ghost foot soldier for the Ashen Provinces.

- Square 1:1 canvas with exactly one complete padded silhouette.
- Readable three-quarter view facing screen-left.
- Exactly one exposed spectral skull head, two arms, two hands, two legs, and
  two feet.
- Use mature adult proportions, coherent joints, and wearable infantry armor.
- Use a uniform warm near-black `#171311` background.
- Do not add scenery, floor, cast shadow, fog field, gradient, vignette, frame,
  text, logo, watermark, or a green background.
- Use dense, deliberate pixel art with hard square pixels, connected clusters,
  broad readable value masses, restrained ramps, selective highlights,
  consistent pixel density, and clean silhouette edges.
- Do not use anti-aliasing, blur, painterly gradients, filtered-painting detail,
  or noisy microtexture.
- Only the soldier's actual body parts and wisps dissolving directly from those
  body parts are spectral.
- Spectral anatomy uses vivid evil green with translucent darker-green interior
  layers and restrained pale yellow-green rim pixels.
- The exposed skull, neck gaps, hands, and exposed lower-limb gaps are green.
- Armor, clothing, mail, leather, shields, boots, wood, weapon hardware, poles,
  and banners remain opaque, solid, physical, dark neutral materials.
- Green reflection is limited to a few immediately adjacent edge pixels and
  must never wash over the equipment.
- Use simple, historically plausible, human-scale weapon construction.
- Do not add extra skulls, faces, hands, limbs, weapons, magical ammunition,
  fantasy blades, oversized weapons, glowing equipment, translucent equipment,
  gore, or copyrighted character designs.

## 01. Evil-Green Levy Spearman

Create an advancing thrust infantryman with a lean forward-moving stance and an
open diagonal silhouette.

- Physical armor: dark iron open helmet, solid riveted breastplate, small
  shoulder plates, charcoal split surcoat, brown leather belt, and solid boots.
- Equipment: exactly one straight ash-wood spear held in both hands and exactly
  one small solid round buckler strapped to the forearm.
- Spearhead: one compact, smooth, symmetrical, leaf-shaped forged-steel head
  with no teeth, serrations, hooks, barbs, ornament, glow, or fantasy geometry.
- Spectral exposure: skull, neck, hands, shin gaps, and restrained rearward
  wisps.
- No other weapon.

The first generated spearman was edited only to replace a serrated fantasy
spearhead with the accepted plain leaf-shaped spearhead. All other design,
composition, material, anatomy, and pixel-treatment traits were preserved.
