# Evil-Green Skull Foot Soldiers — Status

## Goal

Create five ghost foot soldiers using the supplied possessed-washerwoman ghost
as the positive reference for layered spectral translucency, bright rim light,
dark interior shading, and trailing dissolution.

User locks for this pass:

- All five soldiers have clearly exposed skull faces.
- Only body parts and wisps dissolving directly from body parts are bright evil
  green and spectral.
- Armor, clothing, leather, boots, shields, poles, banners, weapons, and
  ammunition remain opaque, solid, physical materials.
- Weapons use realistic, human-scale historical construction with no fantasy
  distortions or magical treatment.

## Review

- **Faces:** every draft has one clearly exposed green spectral skull.
- **Material separation:** green is confined to exposed anatomy and attached
  wisps; armor, cloth, leather, shields, weapons, banner, and boots remain dark,
  opaque, and physical.
- **Weapon realism:** plain leaf-headed spear, straight arming sword and round
  shield, practical halberd, mechanically coherent crossbow, and straight
  arming sword with a physical standard.
- **Silhouette diversity:** long diagonal spear, broad shield, tall halberd,
  horizontal crossbow, and wide banner-and-sword composition remain distinct at
  256 pixels.
- **Counts:** each saved draft has one skull head, two arms, two hands, two
  legs, and two feet. Equipment counts match the concept descriptions.
- **Framing:** every source is square with a complete padded silhouette on a
  warm near-black field and no scenery or text.
- **Pixel treatment:** crisp connected clusters and broad value masses remain
  readable in the nearest-neighbor references.
- **Rejected attempts:** the first spearman used a serrated fantasy spearhead
  and was replaced by a corrected edit. Two crossbow attempts showed an extra
  hand-like spectral form and were not copied into the project.
