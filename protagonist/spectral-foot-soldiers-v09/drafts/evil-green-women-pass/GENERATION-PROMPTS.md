## Shared generation direction

Create one original adult woman evil-green ghost foot soldier for the Ashen
Provinces, a Philippine-inflected gothic Metroidvania.

- One complete mature adult woman on a square 1:1 canvas.
- Believable lean or medium female proportions; functional soldier design.
- Exactly one intact human female head and face, two arms, two hands, two legs,
  and two feet.
- Her intact apparition-face has an unmistakable sadistic, horrifying smile:
  unnaturally wide tooth-bearing grin, raised mouth corners, narrowed luminous
  eyes, and delighted predatory cruelty.
- Preserve cheeks, lips, teeth, nose, chin, and jaw as human female anatomy.
- No skull, exposed bone, skeletal face, mask, closed visor, scream, frown,
  sadness, neutral expression, or blank face.
- Her exposed face, hair, neck, bare hands, narrow joint gaps, and their wisps
  are the only spectral parts.
- Spectral anatomy uses translucent deep emerald, acid-green midtones, dark
  interiors, pale mint edge pixels, and controlled wisps flowing screen-right.
- Armor, clothing, mail, leather, wood, weapons, shields, drums, straps, and
  boots are completely opaque, solid, physical, and non-spectral.
- Functional armor only: no cleavage, corsets, high heels, pin-up poses, or
  fantasy fashion armor.
- Uniform warm near-black `#171311` background; never green.
- No scenery, floor, cast shadow, localized backdrop glow, gradient, vignette,
  fog, frame, symbols, text, logo, or watermark.
- Deliberate high-density pixel art with hard square pixels, connected clusters,
  broad readable values, restrained ramps, selective highlights, and consistent
  pixel density.
- No anti-aliasing, blur, painterly gradients, noisy microtexture, graphic gore,
  extra anatomy, missing anatomy, or copyrighted character imitation.
