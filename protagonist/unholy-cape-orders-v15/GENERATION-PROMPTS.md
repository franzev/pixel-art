# Generation Prompts — Unholy Cape Orders v15

## Shared art direction

Create one original full-body unholy medieval-fantasy knight as a polished, production-quality pixel-art character sprite. Use the two supplied review sheets only as broad references: Image 1 for restrained infernal materials and the accepted dark palette; Image 2 for grounded human scale, practical armor construction, and readable pixel treatment. Do not reproduce any referenced figure, helmet, costume, silhouette, or pose.

Hard requirements: exactly one adult knight, centered, fully visible from helmet to feet with all cape ends and weapons inside the frame. Lean or medium human build, never bulky or gigantic. The armor must look wearable: plausible plate thickness, articulated joints, restrained pauldrons, practical weapon scale, stable upright stance. Full body coverage and nonsexualized design; no nudity, cleavage, lingerie armor, sculpted breast cups, exposed thighs, high heels, or pin-up pose.

ABSOLUTELY NO WINGS OF ANY KIND: no biological wings, feathered wings, bat wings, mechanical wings, decorative wing frames, wing ornaments, wing-like energy, or cape panels spreading outward like wings. The unusual silhouette must come only from a clearly constructed cape made of cloth, leather, modest chains, and small weights. Keep the cape physically attached and gravity-driven, and do not let it hide the hands, joint structure, legs, or primary weapon.

Visual style: crisp hand-authored dark-fantasy pixel art, clean clusters and selective dithering, readable at game-sprite scale, roughly 12–18 meaningful colors, restrained highlights, no smooth painterly rendering or anti-aliased vector edges. Warm charcoal solid background `#171311`. No scenery, architecture, particles, magic aura, bloom, halo, cast shadow, floor plane, border, label, text, logo, or watermark. Three-quarter frontal character-design view.

## 01 — Braided-Shroud Apostate

Adult male knight. Design a compact, hornless close helm with a low wedge brow, narrow horizontal eye slit, and a small riveted mouth guard. Fit him in soot-black articulated plate over a rust-red arming coat, with modest shoulder plates and bone-colored cord details. His defining cape is made from exactly three broad, visibly separate charcoal cloth bands: the bands cross and braid into a heavy asymmetrical knot over his left shoulder, then merge into one long weighted tail hanging behind the same side. The tail hangs downward and inward; it never fans or resembles a wing. He holds a practical straight longsword low in his right hand and a small battered round shield in his left. Quiet apostate severity, compact silhouette, believable weight.

## 02 — Crescent-Collar Castellan

Adult female knight. Design a narrow enclosed sallet-like helmet with a sharp but plausible brow and a short segmented bevor, entirely original and without horns. Tailored plum-black plate over dark aubergine mail, with restrained brass fasteners, anatomical articulation, and no exaggerated feminine shaping. Her defining cape begins as a tall stiff crescent-shaped standing collar behind the neck, then collapses into a heavy diagonal cloth shroud that wraps closely across the back and around the right hip, ending in one short triangular fall beside the thigh. It stays wrapped close to the body; no flared panels and no wing shape. She carries a slender practical estoc and a narrow oval shield. Controlled castellan authority, medium build, stable stance.

## 03 — Chain-Hem Bailiff

Adult male knight. Design a round-topped judicial close helm with a flat brow band, tiny ventilation slots, and a short blunt faceplate, severe but mechanically plausible. Armor is a dark brass-and-black coat of plates over fitted mail sleeves and articulated greaves, worn rather than ornate. His defining cape is a short, broad rectangular mantle attached straight across the upper back, hanging nearly vertical to the waist. Its lower edge forms five shallow scallops connected by a modest iron chain, with one small weight at each low point so the cloth hangs square and heavy. It must not flare sideways or read as wings. He carries a compact flanged mace and a narrow shoulder-height heater shield. Grounded enforcer, medium build, functional mass.

## 04 — Spiral-Mantle Duelist

Adult female knight. Design a slim enclosed dueling helmet with an offset diagonal visor seam, fine breathing holes, and a low swept rear neck plate; no horns or animal motifs. Armor is lean ash-green blackened steel with pale oxidized edges, layered over a dark padded coat, practical and fully enclosing. Her defining cape is one continuous long strip of heavy ash cloth fastened on the left shoulder, wrapping once in a tight descending spiral across the back and waist, then emerging at the front-right hip as one narrow weighted sash ending near the knee. The spiral must read clearly as one gravity-bound textile path, never a floating ribbon and never a wing. She holds a realistic estoc and a small steel buckler. Reserved duelist poise, compact controlled silhouette.

## 05 — Five-Tongue Procession Marshal

Adult female knight. Design an original high-crested processional close helm whose crest is a narrow solid metal ridge, not horns, feathers, or wings; use a vertical slit cluster over the face and a practical gorget. Armor is oxblood-black command plate with blackened steel edges, modest pauldrons, articulated waist lames, and practical boots. Her defining cape begins as one short shoulder mantle and divides below the shoulder blades into exactly five long vertical cloth tongues of visibly unequal lengths. Every tongue hangs straight down close behind the body and ends with a small rectangular iron votive plaque; none spreads sideways. She holds a compact poleaxe upright with both hands, its head realistic and fully inside frame. Processional menace, lean-medium build, commanding but not theatrical.
