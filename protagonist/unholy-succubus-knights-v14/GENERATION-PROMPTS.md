## Shared direction

Render one full-body original adult female succubus knight centered on uniform
warm charcoal `#171311`. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art
with hard square pixels, large connected clusters, broad readable material
planes, controlled edge stepping, restrained highlights, and approximately
12–18 meaningful colors.

The knight must read as an adult female infernal being and a credible armored
combatant. Use realistically proportioned female anatomy beneath tailored,
fully covering, articulated armor. Express succubus identity through elegant
predatory posture, fitted armor lines, forged horns, an armored tail, folded
wings, velvet, lacquer, and controlled demonic geometry.

Keep helmets skull-sized with functional sight, breaths, pivots or catches, and
neck clearance. Use stable feet and human-scale weapons with believable grips.

No nudity, cleavage, lingerie armor, sculpted breast cups, corseted waist,
exposed buttocks, exposed thighs, fetish straps, high heels, pin-up pose,
sexual act, glowing eyes, constant flame, giant wings, oversized horns, giant
pauldrons, spherical torso, tank feet, smooth painterly rendering, tiny noisy
pixels, scenery, text, logo, watermark, or recognizable copied game armor.

## Character briefs

1. **Velvet-Horn Castellan** — tall lean adult female commander with a
   skull-sized close helm and long forged horns swept backward close to the
   head; tailored black court plate, wine-red articulated skirt panels,
   restrained antique-gold fasteners, split velvet cape, practical longsword,
   and oval shield. No wings or visible skin.
2. **Duskwing Lancer** — lean adult female cavalry demon with compact curved
   temple horns, indigo and dark-bronze cavalry half-plate over mail, smoke-grey
   riding cloth, very long human-scale lance, no shield, and one restrained pair
   of biological leathery wings folded tightly down the back like a short cape.
   Wings must not exceed the body width or obscure the armor.
3. **Scorpion-Tail Duelist** — very lean adult female duelist with a narrow
   closed armet, ivory and garnet lacquered fitted harness, black joint
   protection, no cape, no wings, long estoc, and small buckler; one segmented
   armored demonic tail emerges behind the fauld and curves low beside the
   legs, ending in a restrained metal point. No exposed skin.
4. **Chalice-Maw Gaoler** — medium adult female gaoler with four short
   crown-like horn fittings and a downward chalice-shaped visor ending in
   abstract metal fang plates; violet-black coat of plates, dark-brass chain
   collar, short plum mantle, hooked prison bill, and tall narrow shield.
   Menacing but mechanically wearable, with no literal mouth or tongue.
5. **Ash-Seraph Reaper** — lean-medium adult female reaper with a close helm,
   one backward horn intact and the other broken near its reinforced base;
   ash-white and charcoal fluted plate, dried-red bindings, tattered black cape
   cut into two wing-like cloth panels, practical war-scythe, no shield, and no
   biological wings. Severe fallen-seraph silhouette without halo or glow.
