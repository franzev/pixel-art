## Goal

identity, practical full-coverage armor, elegant predatory silhouettes, and
distinct combat roles. Sensuality must come from posture, tailored construction,
velvet, horns, wings, and controlled demonic geometry rather than nudity or
fetish-costume armor.

## Review

- **Succubus identity:** elegant predatory posture, tailored female armor,
  controlled horns, one folded-wing design, one armored-tail design, velvet,
  lacquer, and infernal face geometry establish the faction without relying on
  nudity.
- **Adult female representation:** all five use realistically proportioned
  adult female bodies beneath articulated full-coverage combat armor.
- **Armor integrity:** no sculpted breast cups, cleavage, lingerie armor,
  corseted waists, exposed thighs, high heels, fetish straps, or pin-up poses.
- **Feature distribution:** only the Duskwing Lancer has biological wings and
  only the Scorpion-Tail Duelist has a tail. The other three use forged horns,
  masks, and cloth architecture.
- **Silhouette and role range:** shielded court commander, unshielded winged
  lancer, narrow tailed duelist, armored gaoler, and scythe-bearing fallen
  seraph remain distinct at 256 × 256.
- **Wearability:** helmets remain skull-sized, wings stay folded, the tail
  clears the legs, weapons are human-scale, and all figures retain readable
  waists, joints, grips, and stable feet.
- **Hard gates:** no giant wings or horns, exposed monsters, glow, bulky bodies,
  repeated armor bases, smooth rendering, or recognizable copied armor.
- **Retention:** all five full generated sources are retained.
