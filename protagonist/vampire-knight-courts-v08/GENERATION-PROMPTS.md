# Vampire Knight Courts v08 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per character  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Shared direction

Render one full-body original vampire knight centered on uniform warm charcoal
`#171311`. Use deliberately chunky low-resolution game pixel art with hard
square pixels, large connected clusters, broad readable material planes,
restrained highlights, and approximately 12–18 meaningful colors.

Express vampirism through aristocratic predatory helmet geometry, tailored
armor, funereal heraldry, capes, and controlled decadence—not exposed monsters,
glowing eyes, or generic black-and-red recolors. Keep realistic human-scale
lean or medium proportions; only one role may be moderately heavy. Vary helmet
families, armor construction, cape shape, palette, stance, and weapon. No
scenery, floor plane, cast shadow, text, logo, watermark, recognizable copied
game designs, bat wings, or oversized armor.

## Character briefs

1. **Chalice-Mask Seneschal** — inverted chalice visor with narrow breathing
   cuts, blackened silver court plate, wine velvet shoulder cape, slender mace
   and tall narrow shield.
2. **Nightwing Dragoon** — swept bat-ear-like burgonet fins without wings,
   midnight indigo cavalry cuirass, grey riding coat, forked partisan and
   curved side sword.
3. **Funeral-Rose Duelist** — closed rose-petal armet, ivory and dried-rose
   lacquer, slim articulated harness, one-sided mourning cape, rapier and
   small faceted buckler.
4. **Crimson Widow Lancer** — narrow veil-like faceguard, long backward crest,
   dark crimson and black fitted plate, split trailing cape, long lance.
5. **Moonless Castellan** — broad flat-topped great helm with tiny silver
   perforations, dark green-black castle guard armor, black mantle, poleaxe
   and medium heater shield.
6. **Leech-Jaw Gaoler** — segmented downward jaw visor with restrained
   sucker-ring vents, bruised plum coat of plates, chain collar, hooked bill
   and prison-key round shield.
7. **Gilded Coffin Paladin** — tall coffin-shaped visor outline, tarnished gold
   and oxblood ceremonial plate, short tabard, straight sword and tall
   reliquary shield.
8. **Velvet Hound Cavalier** — long houndlike muzzle visor kept abstract and
   metallic, tobacco-brown and burgundy cavalry armor, sweeping velvet
   half-cape, saber and oval shield.
9. **White-Fang Inquisitor** — two pale downward visor points like restrained
   fangs, ash-grey and bone-white inquisition armor, high collar, estoc and
   lantern-shaped shield with no light.
10. **Blood-Sun Prince** — broken circular sunburst halo attached directly to
    the helm, black-gold and deep carmine princely plate, asymmetric mantle,
    longsword and command shield.
