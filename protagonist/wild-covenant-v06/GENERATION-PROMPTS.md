# Wild Covenant v06 — Generation Prompts

**Mode:** Built-in image generation, one call per knight  
**Use case:** `stylized-concept`

## Shared presentation and originality

One full-body figure centered on uniform warm charcoal `#171311`. Deliberately
chunky low-resolution game pixel art, authored as if on a native 96 × 96
indexed-palette grid: hard square pixels, large connected clusters, broad
material planes, restrained highlights, and roughly 12–18 meaningful colors.

Use lean or medium human proportions, wearable helmets, clear sight and
breathing paths, articulated joints, restrained shoulder width, and human-scale
weapons. Nature influences practical dyes, textiles, shields, heraldry, and
weathering. No antler helmets, living tree armor, giant leaves, exaggerated
bulk, scenery, floor plane, glow, text, logo, or watermark.

Use only high-level grim medieval action-RPG qualities. Do not reproduce a
recognizable game character or a particular historical artifact.

## 01 — Mosscloak Marcher

Low sallet with short pivoting visor and three nape lames. Fitted breastplate
over mail, modest spaulders, narrow tassets, slim greaves, and a short
weathered moss-green shoulder cloak with frayed edge. Long spear and small
heater shield with abstract stepping-stone bands. Medium build.

## 02 — Grove-Gate Warden

Rounded close helmet with clear eye slit and small leaf-vein-shaped ventilation
clusters that remain functional rather than decorative. Dark green brigandine,
small plate shoulders, mail sleeves, split brown surcoat, plate knees and
shins. Human-height billhook and compact round buckler. Lean-to-medium build.

## 03 — Fen-Reed Sentinel

Shoulder-width kettle hat over mail coif and open sight gap, with a small nasal
and fitted cheek plates. Olive padded jack, short mail mantle, leather arm
guards, light leg plates, and a narrow woven reed rain-cloak hanging down the
back. Practical glaive held upright. Lean build.

## 04 — Granite-Pass Keeper

Pointed bascinet with short hinged visor, clear sights, breaths, and mail
aventail. Compact coat of plates over heavy grey wool, small shoulder plates,
sturdy gauntlets, narrow split skirt, practical boots with plate shins. War pick
and tall narrow shield painted with three pale horizontal stone strata. Medium
build, sturdy but not bulky.

## 05 — Birch-Oath Axeguard

Six-segment rounded helmet with brow band, modest nasal, mail cheek curtains,
and visible face. Light lamellar torso, mail sleeves, pale leather trim,
knee-length forest-grey coat, wrapped calves. Bearded axe and medium oval
wooden shield faced with alternating pale birch-like strips; no literal bark
growth. Medium build.

## 06 — Tidewatch Freeblade

Swept burgonet with short peak, close cheek plates, and four articulated rear
neck lames. Blue-black half-cuirass over salt-stained buff coat, small shoulder
caps, plate gauntlets, fitted boots, narrow sea-grey sash. Short boarding

## 07 — Orchard Hook Knight

Open sallet with fitted bevor, clear face opening, and short rear tail. Amber
rivet brigandine, quilted sleeves, modest shoulder cops, split ochre-brown coat,

small side knife. Lean-to-medium build.

## 08 — Storm-Rook Cavalier

High-combed but compact burgonet with short peak, cheek plates, and articulated

shoulders. Curved saber and modest oval shield with one abstract silver wing.
Lean athletic build.

## 09 — Rootbound Hermit

Simple iron skullcap over fitted mail coif with open face and small bevor.
Layered brown gambeson, short mail sleeves, compact chest plate, narrow leather
straps arranged like branching roots, simple gauntlets, wrapped calves and
boots. Human-height staff-hammer with a small iron head; other hand empty.
Lean weathered build.

## 10 — Moon-Moth Watcher

Smooth low sallet with a narrow visor and short articulated tail, paired with a
fitted bevor and clear sight. Slim blue-grey cuirass, small shoulder plates,
dark mail gaps, narrow knee-length coat, and a short pale triangular mantle

narrow estoc and small round shield with an abstract four-part wing pattern.
Lean build.
