# Angel Contract

Load this with `CHARACTERS.md` for angels and corrupted angels.

Use the first three columns of
`samples/angel-character-direction/angel-character-review-sheet.png` as visual
anchors:

1. Gilded Spear Angel.
2. Veiled Vessel Angel.
3. Hooded Lantern Pilgrim.

## Direction

- One readable role and one primary prop or equipment set.
- Controlled two-wing silhouette unless another exact count is requested.
- Small restrained halo and one coherent clothing system.
- Restrained warm-dark, dirty-ivory, ash, and dull-gold palette.
- Readability at 256 pixels.

For corrupted angels, show one primary physical or religious consequence.
Corruption must be consequential rather than fashionable. Do not use visible
spells, magical accents, auras, or floating effects to communicate it.

Use coherent functional gothic or medieval dress: tunic and tabard, trousers
and boots, wrapped waist garment, cropped surcoat, monastic work clothing, or a
structurally coherent restrained robe.

Avoid giant symmetric wings, youthful-handsome sameness, literal object
gimmicks, bare-chested model bodies, shorts, underwear, or fashion harnesses.

Begin any new corrupted-angel extension with no more than three pilot concepts
and compare them side by side with the approved anchors.
