# Boss Contract

Load this for boss character concepts. Also load another category module when
the boss explicitly belongs to it.

- Create a story and encounter identity, not an enlarged common enemy.
- Prefer transformations that express narrative consequence.
- Make every repeated body part and prop exactly countable.
- Preserve a readable encounter silhouette with clear locomotion and attack
  logic.
- Humanoid giants such as the Bell Giant are valid.
- Do not make the body a building, house, tower, doorway, altar, or other
  architectural structure unless the user explicitly reverses this rule.
- Do not hide required legs or appendages behind the body.
- Do not add extra limbs, containers, weapons, or repeated parts.
