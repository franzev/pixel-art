# Demon Contract

Load this with `CHARACTERS.md` for demon or infernal-humanoid concepts. Also
load `KNIGHTS.md` when the subject is explicitly a demon knight.

## Diversity Lock

- No animal-bodied, animal-headed, or animal-derived demons.
- No imps, small hunched goblin-like bodies, or oversized heads.
- No red-dominant body palette. Scarlet, oxblood, burgundy, flesh-pink, and
  mauve may not become the primary body color.
- Prefer full-height adult humanoid or person-like anatomy in cool or neutral
  body palettes.
- Build variety through posture, clothing, conventional tools, bindings, masks,
  facial planes, gestures, and encounter logic.

Avoid hellhounds, dog demons, bell demons, beetles, bugs, insect-derived demons,
generic bulky brutes, recolored rejected archetypes, whimsical unrelated
animals or objects, repeated veiled women, identical handsome men, and cloned
athletic bodies.

Follow the current armed, martial, religious-gothic direction in
`art-catalog/REVIEW-LEARNINGS.md`. Do not use the Flesh-Veil Oracle or Infernal
Demons Batch 13 as positive anchors.
