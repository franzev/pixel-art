# Narrative Scene Contract

Load this instead of `CHARACTERS.md` for cinematic scenes, redemption scenes,
and anthology illustrations.

These requests are exceptions to the isolated-character composition and flat
`#171311` background, but not to the project-wide canvas requirement. Generate
every original full-quality narrative scene on a square `1:1` canvas with
output width equal to output height.

Create a cinematic narrative illustration with:

- Believable anatomy.
- A clear action demonstrating the requested choice or event.
- No visible spells or spell-like effects; keep gore restrained.
- Strong character hierarchy.
- Environmental storytelling.
- A readable moral or emotional event that does not depend on explanatory text.

Do not reinterpret a narrative illustration as an isolated sprite concept
unless the user asks for one.
